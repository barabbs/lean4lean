# Review of chapter experimental-logrel

## Quality verdict
The contributed branches touch none of this group: all four modules are byte-for-byte identical between master and trproj HEAD, so nothing here is attributable to iota or trproj and the chapter carries no attribution badge. Judged as master code, the design is the strongest idea in the Experimental tree - a finitary shape model in which proof irrelevance is absorbed (every inhabitant of a Prop-shape is bot, WShape.HasType.proofIrrel at 3211), which lets semantic unique typing (StrongSound.uniq, 4750) fall out with no stratified judgement and no Church-Rosser theorem, and that theorem really is sorry-free. The execution is much weaker than the idea: the fundamental theorem LR.adequacy has a live sorry in its const case (ShapeLogRelAdequacy.lean:154), so all three headline inversion results and UniqueTyping.lean are unproved, and everything from ShapeLogRel.lean:5054 onward additionally rests on the sorry `SExpr.IsDefEq.strong` and on the global `axiom Params.extra_pat` that was moved out of `class Params` - none of which is flagged in any comment, while the commit declaring injectivity finished landed further code on top of the hole. Reviewability is poor in a measurable way: seven proofs exceed 100 lines as single uncommented tactic blocks, LR.adequacy is 327, the successor step LRS is a 238-line structure instance with nineteen unnamed field proofs, and 13 docstrings in 6100 lines sit mostly on the obvious definitions rather than on LE_Interp, Shape.WF or LR. Against that, the parts that are finished are genuinely clean - only 23 of 818 declarations are sorry-tainted, the shape algebra and interpretation layer are proved outright, and LE_Interp.subst is an exact biconditional - and LogRel.lean / CoinductiveLogRel.lean are abandoned scratch that should be deleted rather than left to collide by name with three live modules.

## Refuted claims
- 'All four are 100 per cent master (Mario Carneiro)' - the branch claim holds (git diff master HEAD over all four files is empty), but authorship is not exclusively Carneiro: Kim Morrison has two toolchain-bump commits (5518bf8, 779c51f) touching them.
- 'the whole development additionally rests on SExpr.IsDefEq.strong' - only 23 of the 818 declarations of ShapeLogRel.lean are sorryAx-tainted, and the earliest is LE_Interp.strongSound at line 5054. The shape algebra, WShape/TShape layer, shape typing, LE_Interp and its substitution/join/inversion lemmas, and StrongSound.uniq (4750) are all sorry-free.
- 'six of them exceeding one hundred lines' - seven proofs exceed 100 lines (206, 149, 145, 139, 109, 104, 327), and the LRS structure instance is a further 238-line term.
- 'SoundEq/SoundTy are literally the two bullets of soundness.tex Theorem thm:sound' - thm:sound (soundness.tex:216) has three bullets, all about ZFC denotations of terms. The two-bullet statement is thm:sound2 (line 351).
- 'The commit that added the file is titled Finished injectivity!' and 'the commit that introduced it is titled Finished injectivity!' - ShapeLogRelAdequacy.lean was added by e8b539c 'finish lamDF case'; the sorry at line 154 was already present before 84f2b04 'Finished injectivity!', which added UniqueTyping.lean and the injectivity theorems on top of the existing hole.
- 'All six textual sorrys and the one stop in ShapeLogRel.lean live in this dead block' - the block contains ten sorry occurrences on eight lines (1702, 1710, 1711, 1719 twice, 1721 twice, and three commented stubs at 1727/1729/1731) plus one stop; the chapter's own later claim that grep reports eight is a line count, not an occurrence count.
- 'The commented attempt is a 65-line induction' - it runs 1666-1723, 58 lines. 'three further commented stubs ... at lines 1727--1731' - they start at 1725.
- 'Fifteen @[simp] lemmas expose these cases' (LRS.DefEq) - there are fourteen, at 5629-5655, matching the fourteen names already listed in the node's own \lean{}.
- 'each has to be proved by a show plus Subtype.eta dance' - only LRS.DefEq.lam_forallE is; the other thirteen unfolding lemmas are rfl/.rfl.
- 'a single 239-line anonymous-constructor term (lines 5657--5895)' - LRS is a `where` structure instance spanning 5657-5894 (238 lines); 5895 is blank.

Four separate theorems are stacked: RHS.compat_join (3561), Const.compat_mismatch (3751), Const.compat_join (3786), LE_Interp.compat_join (3936).

- 'This is one of only two docstrings in ShapeLogRelAdequacy.lean' - there are four (93, 105, 448, 458).
