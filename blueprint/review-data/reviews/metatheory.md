# Review of chapter metatheory

## Quality verdict
The iota work in this chapter is disciplined and well motivated: one new constructor, eleven mechanical pat cases that all follow a single recipe and delegate their transport to lemmas placed in Pattern.lean where they belong, and a clean decomposition of master's monolithic Ordered.strong into five reusable pieces (EnvStrong.mono, EnvStrong.of_hasType, OnTypes.addConst/addDefEq/addPat), every one of which has a real consumer in EnvLemmas.lean. The one genuinely non-mechanical design decision — annotating IsDefEqStrong.pat with the reduct's typing rather than deriving subject reduction afterwards — is defended in a docstring and is demonstrably load-bearing: five later pat cases (forallE_inv', hasType', EqUpToLevels.instL, EqUpToLevels.defeq, substEq') close in one to four lines each precisely because the right-hand side has an induction hypothesis, and the branch's declarations in Strong.lean are all sorryAx-free. The cost is a single, honestly documented liability: the environment hypothesis of the strong system stopped being derivable, and VEnv.WF.patsStrong (EnvLemmas.lean:334) is an open sorry whose quantified form (VEnv.PatsStrong, EnvLemmas.lean:130) is strictly harder than the per-environment PatsStrongOn it is stated against; the two CoeOut instances make that obligation invisible at use sites, which is convenient but hides it. Two secondary weaknesses: the Params.pat_env docstring justifies the new field by extra_pat, with which it has no logical connection, and the only construction of Params requires DefEqsAsPats, so the new pat case of IsDefEq.church_rosser is presently exercised on no environment containing a def or the quotient rule. Nothing added is dead, and the surrounding master code is in worse shape than the contribution (three sorries in NormalEq.parRed and IsDefEqU.weakN_iff already leave all of Church-Rosser, standardization and type inference unproved).

- Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/ChurchRosser.lean:22 — LOW (master; cost borne by iota) — the Params field pat_app_l is never consumed. A repo-wide grep finds it only at the class declaration, at the obligation VEnv.WF.pat_app_l (InductiveParams.lean:297) and at the toParams line that discharges it (404). The iota branch has to prove a dead obligation for env.pats.
- Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean:89 — LOW (iota) — the check list of IsDefEqStrong.pat is specified by Pattern.Check.Realizes (Pattern.lean:307), which constrains only t.1 and t.2.1 of each triple and leaves the type component t.2.2 free; the rule then demands a typed equality at that free type, so chk is not determined by (p, r, m1, m2). - Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/ChurchRosser.lean:632-641 — LOW (master) — a ten-line commented-out IsDefEqU.applyL draft is left in the file.
- Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/ChurchRosser.lean:389,395,397 — LOW (master) — meas, meas_liftN and meas_lift are `private`, so the measure NormalEq.trans terminates on cannot be reused outside the file.
