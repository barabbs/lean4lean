# Review of chapter trexpr

## Quality verdict
The trproj contribution in this group is substantive and carefully engineered: it replaces a one-line `def TrProj := sorry` plus seven sorry lemmas with an eight-field structure whose every field carries a docstring naming the kernel fact it stands in for, and five of the seven stubs then go through as short, uniform proofs sharing one observation (the pattern key, field index and minor arity see the field telescope only through its length). Two design choices are well judged and verifiable in the code: carrying the expansion's typing as a field rather than deriving it, which makes TrProj.wf unconditional and actually shrinks the proj case of TrExprS.wf, and hiding the expansion data existentially, which left every consumer's statement unchanged except for the two new env/U parameters. The weaknesses are real and honestly documented in-source: TrProj.uniq and TrProj.weak'_inv remain sorry, TrProjCtor.pat pins only the existence of a reduct, the uniq sketch does not address how the per-field level assignment uss is pinned (so the relation may be under-constrained rather than merely unproved), and nothing inhabits TrProjCtor in general since inferProj.WF_struct is sorry. The iota footprint here is small and of mixed value: the empty-context lookup block is a clean support layer for the hand-built Quot axiom shapes (though two of its eight declarations and a misplaced global DecidableEq FVarId instance are dead weight), while the Ordered to OrderedStrong sweep is mechanical but quietly makes every later use of the literal lemmas depend on the admitted VEnv.WF.patsStrong. Overall the code held up better than the chapter's prose: this review refuted roughly twenty chapter-level claims (field counts, line counts, call-site counts, two axiom-footprint attributions, and one entirely fabricated doc/code mismatch) and found no case where the code was worse than described.

## Confirmed code issues
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/Lemmas.lean:992 — MEDIUM — TrProj.uniq is a literal sorry (decls.tsv: sorryAx). Its eight-line docstring lists what a proof needs but never says how the per-field level assignment uss is pinned; two TrProjCtor witnesses for one projection may pick different uss, giving syntactically different recursor applications at different universe instances. Either the sketch is incomplete or TrProjCtor is under-constrained. TrExprS.uniq, TrExprS.defeqDFC, the TrExpr binder builders and TrExpr.proj all inherit the hole.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/Lemmas.lean:745 — MEDIUM — TrProj.weak'_inv is a literal sorry, blocked on the master admission VEnv.IsDefEqU.weakN_iff (Theory/Typing/UniqueTyping.lean:172, sorry at :174). Consequence: TrExprS.weakFV'_inv/weakFV_inv and all three Conditionally*.weakN_inv lemmas stay sorry-dependent. Not a regression, but a large part of the checker's specification.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/Expr.lean:97 — MEDIUM — TrProjCtor.pat asserts only that SOME reduct is registered under the iota key; the reduct is not pinned and the key is a sum, so the relation does not determine reduction behaviour and does not exclude a different motive/minor/index split with the same total. Acknowledged in the docstring; it is the stated obstacle to TrProj.uniq.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/InferType.lean:392 and :407 — MEDIUM — inferProj.WF_struct and inferProj.WF are both sorry, so nothing constructs a TrProjCtor witness in general; the relation is inhabited only by the hand-built witnesses in Lean4Lean/Tests/ProjInhabit.lean. The scope paragraph at Verify/Typing/Expr.lean:84 makes precise prose claims about inferProj that nothing in the repository formalizes.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/Expr.lean:133 — LOW — TrProj existentially quantifies six data, two determined by other fields (np = params.length via params_length; fieldTys via ctor). They reappear as 'params_length := by simpa' and 'field_lt := by rw [hlen]' in each of the three record-rebuilding proofs (641, 1296, 1588).
- Lean4Lean/Verify/LocalContext.lean:183 — LOW — the iota block installs a global 'instance : DecidableEq FVarId' inside namespace Lean.LocalContext under a section heading about the empty local context. Unrelated to LocalContext and, given the project's own LawfulBEq FVarId (Verify/Expr.lean:12), could be instDecidableEqOfLawfulBEq as already done for DefinitionSafety (Verify/Expr.lean:51). Confirmed Lean core v4.33.0-rc2 (Lean/Expr.lean:209) derives only Inhabited/BEq/Hashable for FVarId, so no clash today.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/Lemmas.lean:2349 vs :2344 — LOW — TrExprS.mkAppList_inv overlaps AppStack.build, immediately above it, which decomposes the same translated spine in a different shape. The pointwise List.Forall2 form is what WHNF.lean:87-93 needs, so the duplication is defensible, but two spine-inversion idioms now sit side by side.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Expr.lean:749 — LOW (newly confirmed here) — getAppArgsRevList_mkAppList, one of the three spine lemmas trproj added, has no consumer anywhere outside Verify/Expr.lean; it exists only to prove getAppArgsList_mkAppList at :754 (repo-wide grep).
- Lean4Lean/Verify/LocalContext.lean:188 and :190 — LOW — LocalContext.WF.empty and LocalContext.toList_empty have no consumer outside the iota block; both exist only to prove find?_empty at :194 (repo-wide grep: 0 external hits).
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/Lemmas.lean:1856ff and Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/TrTerm.lean:104,111 — LOW — the iota branch strengthened eight literal-lemma signatures plus TrExprS.ofConst/TrTerm.ofConst from env.Ordered to env.OrderedStrong, forced by HasType.const_inv (Theory/Typing/Strong.lean:915), whose hypothesis moved because IsDefEq.strong (Strong.lean:805) now demands subject reduction of registered iota rules. The lemmas stay sorry-free, but every caller (Verify/TypeChecker/Reduce.lean:16, IsDefEq.lean:406, InferType.lean:420, all passing VContext.Ewf : VEnv.WF) now discharges OrderedStrong through VEnv.WF.orderedStrong (EnvLemmas.lean:339), which rests on the admitted VEnv.WF.patsStrong (EnvLemmas.lean:334).
- Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Injectivity.lean:12,21,34 (master) — CONTEXT — IsDefEqU.sort_inv, forallE_inv_stratified and sort_forallE_inv are master sorries that already taint VEnv.IsDefEq.uniq and hence HasType.defeqU_l on master; relevant because the chapter previously attributed that taint solely to iota's patsStrong.
