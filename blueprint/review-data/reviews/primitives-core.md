# Review of chapter primitives-core

## Quality verdict
The master code in these four files is genuinely good and the chapter's positive assessment survives scrutiny: the reflection schemas are stated about an opaque VExpr and mention no constant until `congr_head`, `PrimitiveResult` quantifies over well-formed extensions so the recogniser's syntax stays out of the metatheory, `ProbeBundle.Done` restricts the caller's obligation to strictly smaller measures so it is the real termination argument, and the docstrings are unusually candid about what is *not* checked (nothing relates `Aty` to the packer; `natBinderTypes` is a hypothesis, not a check). The weak points are all pre-existing: the 1135-line `unfoldNatWellFounded.WF'` at maxHeartbeats 1000000, a stale `BlockQ` docstring, the 123 dead lines of `AddsConsts`/`checkInductive.WF`, and a namespace slip that lands six `List` helpers in `Lean4Lean.List`. The contributed delta is 76 lines and adds no mathematical content, but it is not the trust-neutral edit the chapter originally claimed: it was applied by uniform substitution rather than to the lemmas that need it, and the measurable consequence is that nineteen declarations here (two reflection schemas, the fourteen shared translation atoms, and the three `mkTyEq*` theorems) went from `sorryAx`-free on master to depending on the admitted `VEnv.WF.patsStrong`, dragging all eighteen per-primitive branches with them. That regression, rather than the line count, is the honest cost of the edit, and it is documented nowhere in the source.

## Refuted claims
- "The recogniser itself (Primitive.checkDef) never inspects a definition's identity" (chapter overview). Refuted: Lean4Lean/Primitive.lean:561-582 is `match v.name with` over eighteen names. The property that holds is about the verification, not the code: the reflection schemas are stated about the value as an opaque VExpr.
- "Recursion.lean ... the well-founded-recursion recogniser used by Nat.gcd, Nat.bitwise, Nat.div and Nat.mod" (stated twice). Refuted: `unfoldNatWellFounded` is called only at Lean4Lean/Primitive.lean:373 (checkNatGcd) and :412 (checkNatBitwise); Nat.div and Nat.mod are verified by Primitive.natFuelRec in Basic.lean:1360. The chapter contradicted itself.
- "Twenty theorem signatures changed env.Ordered to env.OrderedStrong" (overview and contribution summary). Refuted: `git diff master iota` over the four files removes 27 `Ordered` hypothesis lines and adds 27 `OrderedStrong` ones; 45 added lines carry `.orderedStrong`; 73 lines changed in total.
- "The 5 lines blamed on trproj are two hunks ... re-wrapping the signatures of wf-app-inv2 and wf-appn-inv". Refuted: `git diff iota HEAD` has exactly two hunks; the first re-wraps the signature of VExpr.WF.app_inv₂ (431-433), the second re-wraps a call site inside the *proof* of VEnv.IsDefEqU.appN (455-456), not a signature.
- "This is the only declaration in the chapter carrying trproj blame" (remark on wf-app-inv2). Refuted: blame/Lean4Lean_Verify_Environment_Primitive_Basic.lean.txt marks 431,432,433 and 455,456 as P; the latter two belong to IsDefEqU.appN.
- "boolIsType' is used only in the Nat.bitwise branch, natIsType' only at Recursion.lean:376". Refuted: natIsType' appears at Recursion.lean:376, Condition.lean:762,763,768,776,777,804,862,1127,1673,2184,2803 and DivMod.lean:102,294; boolIsType' at Condition.lean:1043 and 1131. Neither is in Bitwise.lean.
- "this lemma's single use at Recursion.lean:380" (remark on TrExprS.appN). Refuted: also used at Condition.lean:1466, 1792, 1850, 2037, 2088, 2095, 2747, 2749, 2757.
- "On master ... the GMP-primitive verification was unconditional apart from Injectivity.lean" (High review note). Refuted twice over: (a) Injectivity's sorries reach the layer through IsDefEq.uniq in UniqueTyping.lean as well; (b) every theorem running in the typechecker monad already depended on the master-side sorry at TrProj.weak'_inv through M.WF.withLocalDecl (master Verify/Typing/Lemmas.lean:723; the calling proof in ConditionallyTyped.lean is byte-identical on master).
- "These four genuinely need OrderedStrong" (remark on prim-contains-nat, whose node has seven Lean names). Refuted: all seven need it, five of them transitively through contains_nat_of_hasType.
- "four cases, three of them a single instN of the corresponding probed equation" (proof of of_constructor_cases). Refuted by reading Verify/Primitive.lean:405-423: the (0,0) case uses the closed equation h00 with no instantiation at all; two cases use one instN; the diagonal uses two.
- "using closedness of f and g (HasType.closedN')" (proof of of_unary_step_equations). Refuted: g's closedness is the hypothesis huClosed; only f's is recovered by closedN'.
- "HasType.uniqU and IsType.forallE_inv" (proof of wf-inv-prime). Refuted: app_inv' and betaU apply `.forallE_inv` to the IsDefEqU produced by uniqU, i.e. IsDefEqU.forallE_inv (Injectivity.lean:23), not IsType.forallE_inv.
- "Four near-identical proof scripts differing only in the specification constructor" (remark on data-mkresult). Refuted: mkResult/mkResult1/mkResultBool are near-identical; mkResultTypeEq is a shorter, structurally different proof (and is the only one of the four that is sorryAx-free).
- "Sixteen iota-touched lines here" (remark on prim-atoms). Refuted: the atoms region 1053-1160 has thirteen iota-blamed lines (1074, 1078, 1085, 1089, 1093, 1097, 1101, 1107, 1112, 1116, 1118, 1123, 1124); lines 1185/1195 belong to mkTyEq/mkTyEq1 and 1288 to mkResultBitwise.
- "These three definitions are ... very wide existentials, of seven to eleven components each" (remark on unfold-step). Refuted by counting: StepIH and StepBranch bind nine variables each (three and four conjuncts), Step binds sixteen under nine conjuncts.
- "Nine hypotheses is a lot" (remark on natFuelRec). Refuted: the theorem has ten named hypotheses (hgoT, hleC, hFlt, hFstep, hHwf, hH, hstep, hbb, hlt, hwf).
- "unlike its four sibling schemas" (Low review note on of_pred_equations). Refuted: there are six sibling schemas in the file, all of which take the meta-level function as a parameter.
- "AddsConsts, constants_stable, ... have no consumer anywhere in the repository". Partially refuted: constants_stable, constants_of_mem, holds_of_ne, boolConsts and natConsts are consumed inside the same file by hasPrimitives_bool/nat and checkInductive.WF. - Three srclocs in the Low "interface asymmetries" note pointed at the wrong declarations: of_closed_cases given as Verify/Primitive.lean:429 (that is of_left_cases; of_closed_cases is at 450), natBinLitBool given as Basic.lean:35 (that is applyLit; natBinLitBool is at 52), liftN_lams given as Basic.lean:91 (that is VExpr.appN; liftN_lams is at 195).
