# Review of chapter proj

## Quality verdict
The contributed code this chapter documents is strong and honestly documented, and my checks confirmed the chapter's headline facts: Theory/Proj.lean is 449 lines of trproj work (10 definitions, 38 lemmas, no sorry anywhere in the module per decls.tsv), the four test modules are likewise sorry-free, and the substitution family really is the one its consumers need (projFn_lift'/inst/instL are consumed verbatim by TrProj.weak'/instN/instL at Verify/Typing/Lemmas.lean:658/1316/1603, and instFields_minor_spine, instFields_bvar and instPis_piArity by TrEnv.proj_defeq at Verify/Environment/Lemmas.lean:1149/1154/1050). The design decision to represent Expr.proj as a recursor application is genuinely cheap: IsDefEq gains no rule from this branch (its only non-master constructor, `pat`, is iota's), so the projection computes through the registered iota rule, which Tests/ProjInhabit.lean fires exactly once (line 348) to derive the dependent motive's typing on an instance. The real weakness is the bridge, not the model: inferProj.WF_struct is still `sorry` (InferType.lean:398), the two hand-built environments are never shown well formed (addInduct checks only name clashes and closedness), the witnesses use np=0 and a constant uss so the substitution lemmas are never exercised non-trivially, and the iota oracle is in-repo code that the same branch partly wrote (inductiveReduceRecCore). Test quality is otherwise high, with a dense set of negative controls, though two are weaker than they look (the try/catch Sigma.snd control passes vacuously on any failure, and checkBlockRejected only demands that some clause fail), and I found one documentation error the previous review missed: the IotaShape fixture docstring calls Wrap small-eliminating when Wrap.rec in fact eliminates into Sort u.

## Refuted claims
- REFUTED (deleted from Review notes): 'only forward rules of IsDefEq are used' (Theory/Proj.lean:52) is NOT contradicted by the use of symm and trans: both are constructors of the IsDefEq inductive (Theory/Typing/Basic.lean:20-21), and every step in Tests/ProjInhabit.lean is a constructor application. The docstring's claim is accurate as written.
- REFUTED: 'the numbering skips T4 and hc4 is missing as well, which reads like a leftover from an earlier version of the chain' (ProjInhabit.lean:248-258). Only the lambda-headed intermediates are named; the reduct of beta_4 is the application sel20 a b, and hbeta4 (line 462) and hbeta6 (line 480) are stated on the full spine, so no congruence wrapper is needed. The chain is complete.
- REFUTED: 'it is agreement up to kernel conversion, not the syntactic identity the surrounding prose suggests' (ProjShape.lean:113). The module docstring (line 15) explicitly says 'we check that projTy is isDefEq to the kernel's own inferType'. The genuine defect there is different (projTy vs projMotiveBody naming) and has been substituted.
- PARTIALLY REFUTED: 'the expectation that a nested block fails ctors_positive and recs_over_block ... is pinned nowhere' (IotaShape.lean:295). For Tree both are pinned directly at lines 584-592 (CtorPositive rejects Tree.node; Tree.rec_1's majorFormer? is List and RecShape rejects it). Only the clause names returned by blockFailures, and the other eight nested blocks, are unpinned.
- CORRECTED: 'VEnv.addInduct performs no well-formedness check' (Theory/Inductive.lean:316). It does reject name clashes and non-closed rule reducts; what it never checks is VInductDecl.WF.
- REFUTED (code claim, verified empirically with leanprover/lean4 v4.33.0-rc2): 'small-eliminating Prop inductives (PropLarge, Wrap, SigmaLike)'. Wrap.rec's motive is Wrap p -> Sort u_1, so Wrap is subsingleton-eliminating; PropLarge.rec and SigmaLike.rec have motive into Prop.
- REFUTED: 'VEnv.IsDefEqU.betaN exists in Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Lemmas.lean:1'. It is at line 994 (and is itself trproj-blamed).
- REFUTED: 'These are what make the field telescope of TrProjCtor.ctor stable under TrProj.weakN and TrProj.instN'. instPis_lift'/inst/instL are consumed by TrProj.weak' (Lemmas.lean:641), TrProj.instN (1296) and TrProj.instL (1588); TrProj.weakN (664) is derived from weak'.
