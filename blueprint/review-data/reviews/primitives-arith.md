# Review of chapter primitives-arith

## Quality verdict
The contributed code in this chapter is minimal and mechanically correct: 67 iota lines and 2 trproj lines across 4655, no declaration added or removed, and the master..HEAD diff is 69 insertions against 68 deletions consisting entirely of re-threading one environment hypothesis (61 raise `.ordered` to `.orderedStrong`, 6 in Bitwise.lean lower a raised local back with `.ordered`, and the 2 trproj lines are a pure line-wrap whose content is iota's). The change is forced and the reasoning behind it is sound — master's `Ordered.strong` (Strong.lean:675) was provable only because the iota stage of `Ordered` was a stub, and the honest replacement `VEnv.WF.orderedStrong` carries subject reduction of the registered rules as a third field — but the cost is entirely undeclared where it is paid: no `sorry` and no axiom occurs in the five files, yet all sixteen clause-level `WF` theorems now carry `sorryAx` through `VEnv.WF.patsStrong := sorry`, and not one of the roughly seventy touched call sites, nor either of the two module docstrings that exist, records it. The merge resolution is also visibly minimal rather than considered: within single proofs `c.Ewf.orderedStrong` and bare `c.Ewf` are mixed at identical calls (Condition.lean:1448–1450, 1850–1851), Bitwise.lean's `hE` now advertises a strength seven of its twelve uses immediately discard while Gcd.lean's identical edit needed no such downgrades, and the `CoeOut` instance means the taint reaches declarations no branch edited at all — `Reflection.WF.genTele` is master-attributed on every line and is sorry-dependent now where it was not on master. The master code underneath is of a notably high standard, with design decisions argued in their docstrings (the `natOnly` universe gap, the `WF_ite`/`IteEval` split, the beta-redex gadget), which makes the silence about the new obligation the more conspicuous; the only genuine master-side defects found are one stale module docstring (Condition.lean:26–30) and one dead lemma (`Condition.fvarsIn_ite`).
