# Review of chapter trenv

## Quality verdict
The Lean code under review is, with one class of exception, careful and honest work: every declaration in the chapter's seven files except addDecl.WF's inductDecl case is sorry-free, the docstrings are unusually informative and largely accurate, and the two hardest results (AddInduct.rec_reg feeding TrEnv'.pats_iota_inv_shape, and TrEnv.proj_defeq deriving the projection rule from the registered iota rule rather than postulating it) are architected the right way round. The iota branch's replacement of master's constructorless AddInduct is the substantive change: it turns a relation that could never fire into one that carries real data, derives the block's bookkeeping (names, distinctness, lookup transport, delta-freedom) instead of assuming it as further fields, and is forced into - and delivers - the full 615-line proof of quotient initialization that master had been getting from vacuity, replacing a checkEqType.WF that proved False. The decisive weakness is not in any proof but in the perimeter: nothing in the repository constructs an AddInduct witness, so the entire apparatus is conditional, and the sorry has moved from 'AddInduct is empty' to 'AddInduct has no producer'; symmetrically, proj_defeq's only consumer is a #print axioms test, because reduceProjCore.WF, inferProj.WF_struct and inferProj.WF are all sorry. Smaller defects are real but cheap to fix: two never-projected TrRecursor fields, one dead lemma, a near-duplicate TrEnv-level restatement whose docstring claims a conversion it does not perform, two misfiled families of generic metatheory, and mechanical repetition in addQuot.WF.

## Confirmed code issues
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment.lean:208 - HIGH - the inductDecl case of addDecl.WF is a literal `sorry`, and no declaration anywhere in the repository constructs an AddInduct witness from Environment.addInductive (verified by grepping every AddInduct occurrence: all are eliminations, none an introduction). The whole inductive-block apparatus of both branches is therefore conditional on a hypothesis that is never discharged.
- Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/EnvLemmas.lean:334 - HIGH (outside the chapter's files but on its critical path) - VEnv.WF.patsStrong is a literal `sorry`; VEnv.WF.orderedStrong is defined from it and is supplied by the CoeOut instance to every strong-system inversion lemma. This is why VEnv.HasType.mkApps_inv_head, IsDefEqU.beta_app, mkApps_congr, betaN, TrExpr.mkAppList and TrEnv.proj_defeq all carry sorryAx (decls.tsv usesSorry=true) even though none of them contains a sorry.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Lemmas.lean:1021 - HIGH - TrEnv.proj_defeq, the strongest trproj result here, has no consumer outside Lean4Lean/Tests/ProjInhabit.lean:595 (a #print axioms check). Its would-be consumers reduceProjCore.WF (Verify/TypeChecker/Reduce.lean:143), inferProj.WF_struct (Verify/TypeChecker/InferType.lean:392) and inferProj.WF (InferType.lean:407) are all `sorry`, and its premise TrProjCtor is only ever inhabited by hand in the tests.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Basic.lean:240 and Basic.lean:245 - MEDIUM - TrRecursor.all and TrRecursor.k are never projected anywhere in the repository (grep for `.all`/`.k` over Basic.lean and Lemmas.lean finds only their own declarations; line 612's `htrT.all` is TrIndType.all). They are proof obligations on a producer of AddInduct that buy nothing, and `k` records a flag the model has no rule for.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Basic.lean:165 - MEDIUM - insertConsts_find?_none is dead code: its only occurrence besides its own statement is its own recursive call at line 170.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Lemmas.lean:903 - MEDIUM - TrEnv.pats_iota_inv_shape is `:= TrEnv'.pats_iota_inv_shape H hp` and adds nothing: unlike TrEnv.pats_iota' it does not convert the IotaRule lookups from SMap.find? to Environment.find?. Its docstring ('the constant lookups of IotaRule are then the environment's own find?') claims a conversion it does not perform, and proj_defeq redoes the conversions by hand at Lemmas.lean:1060 and 1072.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Basic.lean:272 - MEDIUM - `structure AddInduct ... where` carries no `: Prop` ascription, so it is Type-valued and the induct constructor of TrEnv' now quantifies over data (ivals, rvals, envT/envC/envR, order) that no Prop-eliminating consumer can read back. Necessary for the derived projections, but an undocumented change from master's `inductive AddInduct ... : Prop`.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Lemmas.lean:959 and Lemmas.lean:1166 - MEDIUM - misfiled material: the beta-telescope family (mkApps_inv_head, beta_app, mkApps_congr, betaN) is generic VExpr metatheory and TrExpr.mkAppList is TrExpr rebuilding; neither has environment-translation content, and both sit in Verify/Environment/Lemmas.lean only because proj_defeq needs them.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Lemmas.lean:917 - MEDIUM - TrEnv.iota_defeq has no TrEnv hypothesis at all (it is a two-line term proof packaging VEnv.IsDefEq.pat into IsDefEqU) yet is named in the TrEnv namespace.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Quot.lean:27 - MEDIUM - AddQuotAux hand-replays the binder names, binder infos and withLocalDecl order of Lean4Lean/Quot.lean with nothing linking the two beyond the proofs breaking; the T1_tr..T4_tr derivations (Quot.lean:331 onwards, about 64 lines) are hand-built TrExprS trees.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Quot.lean:521 and Quot.lean:584 - LOW - mechanical repetition in addQuot.WF: four near-identical seven-line blocks extracting the checkName results, and a 22-line hand-nested chain of Environment.find?_add_of_ne rebuilding safePrimitives.
- Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Quot.lean:20 and Quot.lean:244 - LOW - namespace placement: withLocalDecl_run lands in the Lean4Lean root namespace under a very general name, and the generally useful Environment.get_ok is buried as Lean4Lean.AddQuotAux.Environment.get_ok.
