\chapter{Typing and definitional equality}
\label{chap:typing}

This chapter covers the seven files of \texttt{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing} that state the
idealised type theory and prove its structural metatheory.
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{1} (105 lines) is the judgment itself;
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Env.lean}{1} (66 lines) is well-formedness of an environment
as a sequence of declaration steps;
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Lemmas.lean}{1} (1059 lines) is the structural metatheory
(context liftings, the \texttt{Ordered} environment invariant, weakening, level
instantiation, substitution, context conversion, regularity, sort and $\Pi$ inversion,
simultaneous substitution);
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Meta.lean}{1} (46 lines) is tactic support for closed typing
goals;
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/QuotLemmas.lean}{1} (60 lines) types the quotient block;
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/EnvLemmas.lean}{1} (343 lines) bridges environment
well-formedness to the invariants the rest of the development consumes;
and \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Injectivity.lean}{1} (34 lines) states three admitted
inversion principles and derives one further lemma from them.

Notation. Throughout, $E$ ranges over environments, $U$ over universe arities (the number of
universe parameters in scope), and $\Gamma$ over de Bruijn contexts. We write
$\Gamma \vdash e \equiv e' : A$ for \texttt{VEnv.IsDefEq E U $\Gamma$ e e' A} and
$\Gamma \vdash e : A$ for its diagonal $\Gamma \vdash e \equiv e : A$, which is the Lean
definition of \texttt{HasType}; $\Gamma \vdash A\ \mathsf{type}$ abbreviates
$\exists u.\ \Gamma \vdash A : \mathrm{Sort}\ u$ (\texttt{IsType}). $E \le E'$ is the
environment extension order of Chapter \ref{chap:syntax}: $E'$ has all of $E$'s constants,
definitional axioms and registered reduction rules.

Thesis correspondence. \texttt{IsDefEq} merges \texttt{axioms.tex} §2.1 (typing) and §2.2
(definitional equality) into one relation, with the thesis's abbreviation
$\Gamma \vdash e \equiv e' : \alpha$ taken as primitive and the typing judgment recovered as
its diagonal. \texttt{Lemmas.lean} reproves the thesis's structural lemmas:
\texttt{typesys.tex} Weakening (\texttt{thm:weak}), Substitution (\texttt{thm:subst}) and
Regularity (\texttt{thm:reg}) items 2 and 4. The iota branch's rule \texttt{IsDefEq.pat}
is \texttt{axioms.tex} §2.6.4, but stated in the shape of the thesis's
\emph{regularity of reductions} statement (\texttt{typesys.tex}, ``Regularity continued'',
item \texttt{red\_equiv}; \texttt{unique.tex}, ``Regularity of reductions'' item 1) rather
than in §2.6.4's own fixed-context form; the difference is the subject of §\ref{sec:typing-basic}
and of the review notes.

Contributions. No line of this group comes from the \texttt{trproj} branch. The
\texttt{iota} branch contributes 381 of the group's 1713 lines:
\texttt{EnvLemmas.lean} 237 of 343, \texttt{Lemmas.lean} 74 of 1059, \texttt{Basic.lean} 29
of 105, \texttt{QuotLemmas.lean} 27 of 60, \texttt{Env.lean} 14 of 66;
\texttt{Meta.lean} and \texttt{Injectivity.lean} are untouched master code.
The contribution has two parts. In \texttt{Basic.lean} and \texttt{Lemmas.lean} it is
surgical: one new rule, two new predicates, one new \texttt{Ordered} constructor, six new
lemmas (\texttt{addPat\_le}, \texttt{addPat\_self}, \texttt{PatTyped.mono},
\texttt{PatWF.mono}, \texttt{Ordered.patWF}, \texttt{Ordered.patHeaded}), a case of one to
six lines in each of the nine inductions over \texttt{IsDefEq}, and a one-line case in each
of the three inductions over \texttt{Ordered}. No master lemma gained a hypothesis; the only
master lines deleted in \texttt{Lemmas.lean} are the two anonymous constructors of
\texttt{addConst\_le} and \texttt{addDefEq\_le}, which grew a third component.
In \texttt{Env.lean}, \texttt{QuotLemmas.lean} and \texttt{EnvLemmas.lean} it is a rewrite:
master's eleven-line entry point into the strong system (\texttt{Ordered.strong}, which on
master sat in \texttt{Strong.lean} and is deleted here) is replaced by an induction over
\texttt{VEnv.WF} that threads a new subject-reduction hypothesis through every declaration
form.

Assessment. The design is sound in the part that carries weight: the side condition
\texttt{TemplateHeaded} in \texttt{PatWF} (Definition \ref{def:patwf}) is exactly what keeps
the sort and $\Pi$ inversion lemmas provable with a schematic reduction rule present, and
keeping \texttt{VEnv.WF.strong} parametric in the ι obligation isolates the gap instead of
hiding it. Two things a reader must not miss. First, \texttt{IsDefEq.pat} asserts the reduct
at the redex's type with no typing premise of its own, so the judgment \emph{assumes} subject
reduction for ι; over a merely \texttt{Ordered} environment that assumption is false, as the
contribution's own counterexample shows. Second, the statement that repays it,
\texttt{VEnv.WF.patsStrong}, is \texttt{sorry}, and a \texttt{CoeOut} instance makes every
\texttt{VEnv.WF} hypothesis coerce silently into the strong-system hypotheses, so the whole
verified-typechecker layer above this chapter is conditional on it. Master's inductive layer
was itself two \texttt{sorry}-ed definitions (\texttt{VInductDecl.WF}, \texttt{VEnv.addInduct})
and a \texttt{sorry}-ed soundness theorem (\texttt{addInduct\_WF}), so this is a real trade
rather than a regression, but it must be stated rather than left in a trailing comment.

\section{The judgment}
\label{sec:typing-basic}

\begin{definition}[de Bruijn variable lookup, \texttt{Lookup}]
\label{def:lookup}
\lean{Lean4Lean.Lookup}
\leanok
\uses{def:liftn}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{6}
\texttt{Lookup} relates a context, an index and a type: it holds when the $i$-th entry of
$\Gamma$, lifted over the $i$ binders crossed on the way out, is $A$. The two constructors
are \texttt{zero} (the head of $A :: \Gamma$ is $A$ lifted once) and \texttt{succ}. This is
the de Bruijn reading of $(x : \alpha) \in \Gamma$.
\thesisref{axioms.tex §2.1, the variable rule}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Typing and definitional equality, \texttt{IsDefEq}]
\label{def:isdefeq}
\contrib{mixed}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEq}
\leanok
\uses{def:lookup, struct:venv, ind:vexpr, def:vlevel-wf, inductive:pattern-matches, def:pattern-check-realizes, inductive:pattern-rhs}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{18}
One inductive relation $\Gamma \vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$, relative to an environment and a
universe arity. Master's thirteen rules are \texttt{bvar}, \texttt{symm}, \texttt{trans},
\texttt{sortDF}, \texttt{constDF}, \texttt{appDF}, \texttt{lamDF}, \texttt{forallEDF},
\texttt{defeqDF} (conversion), \texttt{beta}, \texttt{eta}, \texttt{proofIrrel} and
\texttt{extra} (the $\delta$ rule, firing on a registered \texttt{VDefEq} at any level
instantiation). The \texttt{iota} branch adds a fourteenth, \texttt{pat}
(Definition \ref{rule:isdefeq-pat}); master's rules are unchanged.
\thesisref{axioms.tex §2.1 and §2.2 merged into one judgment; §2.6.4 for \texttt{pat}}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The ι rule, \texttt{IsDefEq.pat}]
\label{rule:isdefeq-pat}
\contrib{iota}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEq.pat}
\leanok
\uses{def:isdefeq, struct:venv, inductive:pattern-matches, def:pattern-check-realizes, inductive:pattern-rhs, def:venv-addpat}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{60}
The rule has five premises: $(p, r)$ is registered in the environment's \texttt{pats}
component; $e$ matches the pattern $p$ with level assignment $m_1$ and hole assignment
$m_2$; $\Gamma \vdash e : A$; a list \texttt{chk} realizes $r$'s \texttt{Check}
(\texttt{Pattern.Check.Realizes}); and every triple of \texttt{chk} is a definitional
equality in $\Gamma$. Its conclusion is
$\Gamma \vdash e \equiv r_1.\mathrm{apply}\ m_1\ m_2 : A$. The reduct is asserted at the
\emph{redex's} type, with no typing premise of its own, so together with
\texttt{IsDefEq.hasType} the rule yields $\Gamma \vdash r_1.\mathrm{apply}\ m_1\ m_2 : A$.
The strong system's counterpart \texttt{IsDefEqStrong.pat} (Definition
\ref{inductive:isdefeqstrong-pat}) carries that typing as a premise instead.
\thesisref{axioms.tex §2.6.4, stated in the form of unique.tex ``Regularity of reductions'' item 1}
\reviewnote{The rule builds subject reduction for ι into the judgment. It is false over a
merely \texttt{Ordered} environment (see \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}), so every lemma of
\texttt{Lemmas.lean} proved under \texttt{Ordered} describes a relation that may be strictly
larger than the thesis's. The debt is discharged only by \texttt{VEnv.WF.patsStrong}, which
is \texttt{sorry}. Honestly documented at \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{57}.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Derived judgments and well-formedness of environment entries]
\label{def:hastype-istype}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasType, Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsType, Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqU, Lean4Lean.VExpr.WF, Lean4Lean.VConstant.WF, Lean4Lean.VDefEq.WF}
\leanok
\uses{def:isdefeq}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{68}
\texttt{HasType} is the diagonal $\Gamma \vdash e \equiv e : A$; \texttt{IsType} is
$\exists u.\ \Gamma \vdash A : \mathrm{Sort}\ u$; \texttt{IsDefEqU} forgets the type
($\exists A.\ \Gamma \vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$); \texttt{VExpr.WF} is \texttt{IsDefEqU} on
the diagonal. \texttt{VConstant.WF} says a constant's type is a type in the empty context at
the constant's own universe arity, and \texttt{VDefEq.WF} says both sides of a definitional
axiom have its declared type there.
\thesisref{axioms.tex §2.1 ($\Gamma \vdash \alpha\ \mathsf{type}$) and §2.5}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Typing of a schematic reduction rule, \texttt{PatTyped}]
\label{def:pattyped}
\contrib{iota}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.PatTyped}
\leanok
\uses{inductive:pattern-matches, def:pattern-rhs-generic, def:hastype-istype, def:vlevel-inst}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{92}
A rule $(p, r)$ is typed in $E$ when there exist $U$, $\Gamma$, a redex $e$, a hole
assignment $m_2$ and a type $B$ such that $e$ matches $p$ at the identity level
instantiation \texttt{VLevel.params U}, $r_1$ is generic in $m_2$ over $\Gamma$
(\texttt{Pattern.RHS.Generic}: the holes $r_1$ uses are pairwise distinct variables that
exhaust $\Gamma$), and both $e$ and $r_1.\mathrm{apply}$ have type $B$ in $\Gamma$. The side
conditions $r_2$ are not assumed: a rule must be typed without them. This is the
\texttt{VDefEq.WF} analogue for a schematic rule.
\thesisref{axioms.tex §2.6.4, the parenthetical about substitution instances}
\reviewnote{The predicate is existential in $U$, $\Gamma$, $e$, $m_2$ and $B$, and
\texttt{Generic} constrains only the holes the reduct \emph{uses}; the index arguments and
the constructor's copy of the parameters may be arbitrary terms over $\Gamma$. So a rule can
be ``typed'' at one convenient instantiation. The \texttt{Generic} docstring
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Pattern.lean}{181}) says so, but the name \texttt{PatWF}
promises more than the predicate delivers.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Admissibility of a reduction rule, \texttt{PatWF}]
\label{def:patwf}
\contrib{iota}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.PatWF}
\leanok
\uses{def:pattyped, inductive:pattern-template-headed}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{104}
A rule is well-formed when it is typed and it computes: its reduct is a fixed closed
$\lambda$-term applied to arguments (\texttt{Pattern.RHS.TemplateHeaded}). The shape
condition is what separates a \texttt{pats} entry from a definitional axiom, whose two sides
may be arbitrary closed terms: no instance of a template-headed reduct is a sort, a
$\Pi$-type or a bare hole. It is monotone in the environment
(\ref{fam:patwf-mono}), which is what makes it a legitimate registration condition
(\ref{def:ordered}). It does \emph{not} give subject reduction.
\thesisref{axioms.tex §2.6.4; no thesis counterpart as a separate predicate}
\end{definition}

\section{Well-formed environments}

\begin{definition}[Well-formedness of one declaration step, \texttt{VDecl.WF}]
\label{def:vdecl-wf}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VDecl.WF, Lean4Lean.VDefVal.WF, Lean4Lean.VEnv.addConsts, Lean4Lean.VEnv.addDefEqs}
\leanok
\uses{def:venv-basic, def:venv-addquot, def:ind-add-induct, def:hastype-istype, structure:ind-wf}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Env.lean}{18}
\texttt{VDecl.WF E d E'} says the declaration $d$ may be added to $E$, producing $E'$. The
seven forms are \texttt{axiom} (a \texttt{VConstVal} whose type is a type, added as a
constant), \texttt{def} (a \texttt{VDefVal} whose \emph{value} is typed at its declared type,
\texttt{VDefVal.WF}: the constant is added, then its $\delta$ rule), \texttt{opaque} (the same
premise as \texttt{def}, but only the constant is added, so the value stays hidden),
\texttt{example} (the same premise, environment unchanged), \texttt{mutualDef} (the block's
constant types must be well-formed before it is added with \texttt{addConsts} and its values
typed in the extended environment, after which \texttt{addDefEqs} adds every $\delta$ rule),
\texttt{quot} and \texttt{induct}.
\thesisref{axioms.tex §2.5, §2.6, §2.7.1}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Well-formed environment, \texttt{VEnv.WF}]
\label{def:venv-wf}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.WF', Lean4Lean.VEnv.WF}
\leanok
\uses{def:vdecl-wf, def:venv-basic}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Env.lean}{48}
\texttt{WF' ds E} says $E$ is reached from the empty environment by the declaration list
\texttt{ds}, each step well-formed; \texttt{VEnv.WF E} is the existential closure over
\texttt{ds}. This is the formal notion of an environment the kernel could have built, and
the hypothesis every downstream soundness statement takes.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Well-formed prefix, \texttt{WFPrefix}]
\label{def:wfprefix}
\contrib{iota}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.WFPrefix}
\leanok
\uses{def:vdecl-wf}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Env.lean}{57}

\thesisref{no thesis counterpart; bookkeeping for the formal induction}

\begin{definition}[Ordered environment, \texttt{Ordered}]
\label{def:ordered}
\contrib{mixed}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.Ordered}
\leanok
\uses{def:venv-basic, def:venv-addpat, def:hastype-istype, def:patwf}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Lemmas.lean}{257}
An environment is ordered when it is built from the empty one by steps each checked in the
environment before it: \texttt{const} (the constant's type is a type), \texttt{defeq} (both
sides of the axiom are typed) and, added on the \texttt{iota} branch at

\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[A predicate on every constant type and axiom side, \texttt{OnTypes}]
\label{def:ontypes}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.OnTypes, Lean4Lean.VEnv.OnTypes.mono}
\leanok
\uses{struct:venv}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Lemmas.lean}{267}
\texttt{OnTypes E P} holds when every constant of $E$ satisfies $P$ of its type at some sort,
and both sides of every definitional axiom satisfy $P$ at the axiom's type. It is the shape
of every ``the environment is good'' statement in the group, antitone in the environment and
monotone in $P$ (\texttt{OnTypes.mono}).
\reviewnote{\texttt{OnTypes} deliberately says nothing about \texttt{pats}. This is the

\ref{thm:ordered-induction} and had to rebuild the bootstrap of \ref{thm:wf-strong}.}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Induction over an ordered environment]
\label{thm:ordered-induction}
\contrib{mixed}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.Ordered.induction}
\leanok
\uses{def:ordered, def:ontypes}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Lemmas.lean}{275}
To prove \texttt{OnTypes E (motive E)} for an ordered $E$ it suffices that the motive be
monotone in the environment and that it hold of every closed judgment
$[\,] \vdash e : A$ typed in an ordered environment whose own constants and axioms already
satisfy it.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\uses{def:ordered, def:ontypes, fam:addpat}
\leanok
Induction on \texttt{Ordered}. The \texttt{const} case unfolds \texttt{addConst} and splits
on the name; the \texttt{defeq} case splits on membership in the extended \texttt{defeqs};
the \texttt{iota}-added \texttt{pat} case (line 299) is one line, transporting the induction
hypothesis along \texttt{addPat\_le} because registering a rule contributes nothing to
\texttt{OnTypes}.
\end{proof}

\section{Structural metatheory}

\begin{lemma}[Regularity: free variables are in the context]
\label{thm:isdefeq-closedn}
\contrib{mixed}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEq.closedN', Lean4Lean.VEnv.Ordered.closed, Lean4Lean.VEnv.Ordered.closedC, Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEq.closedN, Lean4Lean.VExpr.WF.closedN}
\leanok
\uses{def:isdefeq, def:onctx, def:ontypes}
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Lemmas.lean}{329}
If every constant type and axiom side of $E$ is closed and $\Gamma$ is closed, then
$\Gamma \vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$ implies that $e_1$, $e_2$ and $A$ have no free variable at
or above $|\Gamma|$. \texttt{Ordered.closed} discharges the environment hypothesis for any
ordered environment, and \texttt{closedC} specialises it to constant types.
\thesisref{typesys.tex Lemma (Regularity) thm:reg item 2}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\uses{def:isdefeq, thm:ordered-induction, family:pattern-transport-iota}
\leanok
Induction on the derivation, using closedness of substitution and lifting in the
\texttt{appDF}, \texttt{beta} and \texttt{eta} cases; \texttt{Ordered.closed} is
\ref{thm:ordered-induction} applied to closedness. The \texttt{iota} \texttt{pat} case
(lines 369--371) takes the redex's closedness from the induction hypothesis and derives the
reduct's from \texttt{Pattern.RHS.apply\_closedN} applied to \texttt{Matches.closedN}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Monotonicity in the environment]

\section{Contribution summary}

Everything contributed in this group belongs to the \texttt{iota} branch (381 lines);
\texttt{trproj} does not touch it. The contribution has three layers.

\emph{The rule and its admissibility condition.} \ref{rule:isdefeq-pat} adds the schematic
ι rule to the judgment, and \ref{def:pattyped} and \ref{def:patwf} say when a rule may be
registered, with \ref{def:ordered} gaining the corresponding \texttt{pat} constructor. The
split into a typing condition and a shape condition is the good idea here: because
\texttt{TemplateHeaded} is monotone (\ref{fam:patwf-mono}) and is recovered from the
environment by \ref{thm:ordered-patwf}, the sort and $\Pi$ inversion lemmas
(\ref{thm:isdefeq-sort-inv}, \ref{thm:isdefeq-foralle-inv}) survive the new rule with a
one-line case each, instead of requiring the open $\Pi$-injectivity of
\ref{thm:injectivity-open}.

\emph{The induction cases.} Nine inductions over \texttt{IsDefEq} gained a \texttt{pat} case
(\ref{thm:isdefeq-closedn}, \ref{thm:isdefeq-mono}, \ref{thm:isdefeq-levelwf},
\ref{thm:isdefeq-weakn}, \ref{thm:isdefeq-instl}, \ref{thm:isdefeq-instn},
\ref{thm:isdefeq-foralle-inv}, \ref{thm:isdefeq-sort-inv} and \ref{thm:isdefeq-istype}), and
three inductions over \texttt{Ordered} gained a one-line one (\ref{thm:ordered-induction} and
the two lemmas of \ref{fam:ordered-entrywf}). They are uniform, one to six lines each, and no
master lemma gained a hypothesis. Each rests on a transport lemma for patterns
(\texttt{liftN\_apply}, \texttt{instN\_apply}, \texttt{instL\_apply} and the
\texttt{Check.Realizes.map\_*} family) proved in Chapter \ref{chap:inductive}.

\emph{The bootstrap.} \ref{def:wfprefix}, \ref{thm:wfprefix-trans}, \ref{thm:vdecl-wf-le},
\ref{def:patsstrong} and the five \texttt{*\_strong} lemmas
(\ref{thm:foldlm-addconst-strong}, \ref{thm:adddefeqs-strong}, \ref{thm:addrules-strong},
\ref{thm:addquot-strong}, \ref{thm:addinduct-strong}) rebuild the entry point into the strong
system of Chapter \ref{chap:metatheory} from \texttt{VEnv.WF} rather than from
\texttt{Ordered}, ending at \ref{thm:wf-strong} and \ref{thm:wf-orderedstrong}.
\ref{thm:addquot-chain} exists to serve \ref{thm:addquot-strong}. The rewrite costs about
200 lines where master needed twelve, and the cost is intrinsic: \texttt{PatsStrong} is not a
property \ref{thm:ordered-induction} can carry, because \texttt{OnTypes}
(\ref{def:ontypes}) says nothing about registered rules.

What remains open is one statement, \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}, and it is the statement the
thesis calls easy. Until it is proved, every theorem in this repository that assumes
\texttt{VEnv.WF} and uses the strong system is conditional on it. The honest framing is that
master had left the inductive layer unspecified (\texttt{VInductDecl.WF} and
\texttt{VEnv.addInduct} were \texttt{sorry}-ed definitions and \texttt{addInduct\_WF} a
\texttt{sorry}-ed theorem), and the branch trades those for a real specification plus one
open lemma; that is a good trade, but the resulting hole is wider than any it replaced.
