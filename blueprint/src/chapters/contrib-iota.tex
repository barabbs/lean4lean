\chapter{Review of the iota contribution}
\label{chap:contrib-iota}

This chapter is a prose review of the \texttt{iota} branch (28 commits, 2026-07-21 to
2026-09-09, merged into \texttt{trproj} at \texttt{20ec229}). It introduces no new graph
nodes: every technical object it discusses is a node defined in Chapters
\ref{chap:syntax}--\ref{chap:typechecker} and referenced here by cross-reference, and every
factual claim is traced to a file under \texttt{.blueprint-work/understand/} or to a
command whose output is quoted. The branch's own scale, from \texttt{git diff --stat
master..iota}: \textbf{45 files, +5628/-331, 28 commits} (\texttt{contrib-iota.md}, confirmed
in \texttt{claims-iota.md} claim 35).

\section{Goal and scope}

On upstream \texttt{master} (\texttt{8223d22}) the abstract model has no account of
$\iota$-reduction. \texttt{Lean4Lean/Theory/Inductive.lean} is a 7-line file whose only two
declarations are
\begin{quote}
\texttt{def VInductDecl.WF (env : VEnv) (decl : VInductDecl) : Prop := sorry} \\
\texttt{def VEnv.addInduct (env : VEnv) (decl : VInductDecl) : Option VEnv := sorry}
\end{quote}
(quoted verbatim from \texttt{git show master:Lean4Lean/Theory/Inductive.lean}; the file is 7
lines, corrected in \texttt{claims-iota.md} claim 36 against \texttt{contrib-iota.md}'s
erroneous ``8 lines''). \texttt{Theory/Typing/InductiveLemmas.lean} on master is 10 lines,
whose only theorem, \texttt{addInduct\_WF}, is also \texttt{:= sorry}. On the refinement side,
master's \texttt{Verify/Environment/Basic.lean} declares \texttt{AddInduct} as a
constructor-less \texttt{inductive ... : Prop} with a \texttt{-- TODO} in place of any
constructor, documented in its own docstring as ``essentially a \texttt{sorry}'' that makes
\texttt{TrEnv'.induct} vacuous; \texttt{Aligned.addInduct} follows by \texttt{nomatch}.
Because no inductive can ever enter a translated environment, master's own
\texttt{checkEqType.WF} is provable only by deriving \texttt{False} from
\texttt{TrEnv'.no\_inductInfo}, and \texttt{addQuot.WF}'s non-initialised branch is discharged
by that \texttt{False.elim} (\texttt{upstream-comparison.md} \S2b--\S2d, byte-compared against
the live upstream repository on 2026-09-14). So on master, no inductive type ever enters a
translated environment, no recursor ever computes, and the modelled type theory has $\beta$,
$\delta$, $\eta$, proof irrelevance and the quotient rule, but not $\iota$.

The branch supplies $\iota$ as a schematic rewrite-rule registry: \texttt{VEnv} gains a third
field \texttt{pats} beside \texttt{constants}/\texttt{defeqs}; \texttt{VEnv.addInduct}
installs one entry per recursor rule; \texttt{IsDefEq} gains one generic constructor
\texttt{pat} that fires any registered entry. This promotes two pieces Mario Carneiro had
already put in the tree as prototypes to live status: the \texttt{SimplePattern.iota}
constructor of \texttt{Pattern.lean}, and the \texttt{VEnv.Params} class of
\texttt{ChurchRosser.lean}, whose \texttt{Pat}/\texttt{pat\_wf} fields were previously an
\emph{assumed} interface with no instance anywhere in the repository
(\texttt{upstream-comparison.md} \S2a, \texttt{claims-iota.md} claim 40). No other upstream
branch and no other open pull request touches any of this
(\texttt{upstream-comparison.md} \S1--\S2).

\section{Design}

\textbf{The pats registry.} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/VEnv.lean}{21} adds the field
\texttt{pats : (p : Pattern) $\to$ p.RHS $\times$ p.Check $\to$ Prop} (\ref{def:venv-addpat}),
with \texttt{addPat} (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/VEnv.lean}{44}) registering one rule and
\texttt{LE.pats} (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/VEnv.lean}{50}) extending environment monotonicity
to it. \texttt{addPat} is total and performs no freshness or consistency check, unlike
\texttt{addConst}; functionality of the registry is recovered only downstream, as the
\texttt{PatsIota} invariant (\ref{structure:indparams-patsiota},
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/InductiveParams.lean}{133}) under \texttt{VEnv.WF}
(\texttt{hygiene-review.md} \S(a)).

\textbf{\texttt{IsDefEq.pat}.} The $\iota$ rule itself
(\ref{rule:isdefeq-pat}, \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{60}) asserts the reduct
at the redex's own type with no typing premise on the reduct: $\Gamma \vdash e : A$ and a
registered match give $\Gamma \vdash e \equiv r.1.\mathtt{apply}\ m_1\ m_2 : A$. The docstring
names this ``the thesis's regularity theorem for $\iota$ taken as a rule''
(\texttt{thesis-map.md} \S3(a), point 3), and the debt is repaid --- or rather deferred --- as
\ref{thm:wf-patsstrong} (\S\ref{sec:contrib-iota-sorry} below).

\textbf{\texttt{PatTyped}/\texttt{PatWF}.} \ref{def:pattyped}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{92}) is the \texttt{VDefEq.WF} analogue for a
schematic rule: existential in $U, \Gamma, e, m_2, B$, so ``the rule is typed'' means only that
\emph{some} instantiation is typable (\texttt{hygiene-review.md} \S(a), MEDIUM). \ref{def:patwf}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Basic.lean}{104}) conjoins \texttt{PatTyped} with
\texttt{RHS.TemplateHeaded} (\ref{inductive:pattern-template-headed}). Rule admissibility is
recoverable from the environment (\texttt{Ordered.patWF}, \ref{thm:ordered-patwf}) and monotone
(\ref{fam:patwf-mono}). The typing/reviews reader (\texttt{reviews/typing.md}) rates this split
as the group's real idea, ``not window dressing''.

\textbf{The inductive block specification, \texttt{VInductDecl.WF}.} A 20-field structure at
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Inductive.lean}{335} (\ref{structure:ind-wf}; field count independently
verified in \texttt{claims-iota.md} claim 39), built from a hierarchy of shape predicates over
an already-elaborated $\Pi$-type: \texttt{CtorResult} (\ref{def:ind-ctor-result}),
\texttt{ValidIndApp}/\texttt{FieldPositive}/\texttt{CtorPositive}
(\ref{def:ind-valid-ind-app}, \ref{def:ind-field-positive}, \ref{def:ind-ctor-positive}),
\texttt{MajorApp} (\ref{def:ind-major-app}), \texttt{MotiveShape}/\texttt{MinorHeaded}/
\texttt{MinorFor} (\ref{def:ind-motive-shape}, \ref{def:ind-minor-headed},
\ref{def:ind-minor-for}), \texttt{RecShape}/\texttt{CtorShape}
(\ref{def:ind-rec-shape}, \ref{def:ind-ctor-shape}) and \texttt{RuleShape}
(\ref{def:ind-rule-shape}), plus \texttt{LargeElim}/\texttt{LargeElimShape}
(\ref{def:ind-large-elim}, \ref{def:ind-large-elim-shape}). Fields
\texttt{ctors\_positive}, \texttt{universes} and \texttt{recs\_elim} carry positivity, the
$\mathtt{imax}(\ell',\ell)\le\ell$ universe bound and large-elimination, contradicting the
stale claim that these were ``not yet enforced'' (\texttt{claims-iota.md} claim 14).

\textbf{\texttt{addInduct}'s four stages.} \ref{family:ind-add-stages}
(\texttt{addTypes}/\texttt{addCtors}/\texttt{addRecs}/\texttt{addRules}) group the kernel's
per-recursor interleaving into four global passes, each typed in the environment the kernel
checks the corresponding constant kind in; \ref{def:ind-add-induct}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Inductive.lean}{316}) chains them and replaces master's
\texttt{sorry}. \ref{def:ind-add-rec-rule} (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Inductive.lean}{257})
installs one $\iota$ rule per recursor rule, registered with \texttt{iotaCheck = .true}: the
kernel performs no parameter check at $\iota$-reduction time, matched exactly by
\texttt{inductiveReduceRecCore} (\ref{def:kernel-iota-core}, \texttt{claims-iota.md} claim 18).
\texttt{addInduct}'s soundness is \ref{thm:indlem-add-induct-wf}, discharged
sorry-free (\texttt{claims-iota.md} claim 2, \texttt{\#print axioms} $=$
\texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}).

\textbf{The \texttt{Params} instance.} \ref{def:indparams-toparams}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/InductiveParams.lean}{393}) builds a
\texttt{Params} instance from \texttt{env.pats}, closing the five structural side conditions
(\ref{family:indparams-params-sides}) via \texttt{PatsIota}
(\ref{structure:indparams-patsiota}) and the new class field \texttt{pat\_env} --- the one
forced addition to Carneiro's class, closing through master's existing
\texttt{ParRed.extra} case of Church--Rosser (\texttt{claims-iota.md} claim 40). It is gated
by \ref{def:indparams-defeqs-as-pats} (\texttt{DefEqsAsPats}), an explicit hypothesis rather
than a proved fact: its own docstring admits it fails as soon as a \texttt{def} or the
quotient rule is declared, because $\delta$ and the quotient redex live in
\texttt{env.defeqs}, not \texttt{env.pats} (\texttt{Theory/Typing/InductiveParams.lean:350--377}).
\ref{def:indparams-induct-params} specialises the instance to a single-block environment and
\ref{thm:indparams-cr-defeq-of-induct} demonstrates Church--Rosser on it --- but both are
\texttt{@[reducible] def}s, not registered instances, and (\S\ref{sec:contrib-iota-hygiene})
have no consumer anywhere else in the repository.

\textbf{Quotient initialisation.} The branch's collateral fix to master's vacuity: with
inductives now enterable, master's \texttt{checkEqType.WF} (proving \texttt{fun \_ =>
False}) becomes false rather than merely unappealing, so it is deleted along with
\texttt{no\_inductInfo}. \ref{def:kernel-check-eq-type} now rejects an \texttt{unsafe}
\texttt{Eq} (one added kernel line, \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Quot.lean}{24}), which discharges the
former \texttt{Environment.EqSafe} assumption; the new 615-line
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Quot.lean}{1} builds the real telescopes and proves
\ref{thm:add-quot-wf}, \ref{thm:exists-add-quot}, \ref{thm:quot-ready} and
\ref{thm:check-eq-type-ok} sorry-free and unconditional
(\texttt{claims-iota.md} claims 22--23).

\textbf{\texttt{AddInduct} bookkeeping in \texttt{Verify}.} \ref{struct:add-induct}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Basic.lean}{272}) is the branch's central rework
(milestone M4 below): a data-carrying \texttt{structure} whose bookkeeping --- names,
distinctness, lookup transport --- is \emph{derived} rather than assumed as further fields,
replacing master's constructor-less \texttt{Prop}. \ref{struct:tr-ind-type} and
\ref{struct:tr-recursor} translate one type former and one recursor; the derived theorems
\texttt{env\_eq}, \texttt{le}, \texttt{wf}, \texttt{find?\_mono}, \texttt{value\_find},
\ref{thm:add-induct-rec-find}, \ref{thm:add-induct-rec-reg} and \ref{thm:add-induct-ctor-find}
were, at an earlier revision, assumed fields (\texttt{claims-iota.md} claim 46, though the
earlier structure had only four of the seven as fields, not seven, correcting an
over-count in the commit message). \ref{thm:aligned-add-induct} and
\ref{thm:aligned-add-rules} extend \texttt{Aligned} with \texttt{pat} and \texttt{block}
clauses mirroring the existing \texttt{defeq}/\texttt{const} cases. \ref{thm:tr-env-pats-iota}
and its variant \texttt{pats\_iota'} expose the $\iota$-rule lookup with the witness named,
feeding \ref{thm:tr-env-iota-defeq} and \ref{thm:tr-env-iota-rec}, which is consumed by
\ref{thm:vtc-iota-reduce-core} --- the one internal consumer of the whole interface
(\S\ref{sec:contrib-iota-hygiene}).

\section{Relation to the thesis}

Correspondences are traced against \texttt{lean-type-theory/axioms.tex} \S2.6.1--2.6.4
(\texttt{thesis-map.md} \S3(a); numbering confirmed against the source: \S2 ``The axioms'',
\S2.6 ``Inductive types''). \textbf{What follows the thesis.} Elimination levels
(\S2.6.2) are faithful: \ref{def:ind-large-elim}'s three disjuncts are close paraphrases of the
thesis's two reasons an inductive can be large-eliminating, down to the ``subsingleton
elimination'' wording. The recursor's universe clause $\kappa$ (\S2.6.3) is handled well by
\texttt{recs\_elim}: a recursor carries the block's universe parameters plus exactly one extra,
which must be \texttt{VLevel.param 0} iff every motive ends in \texttt{Sort (param 0)}, else
\texttt{Prop}. The $\iota$ rule itself (\S2.6.4) is faithful up to three caveats the source
documents in place (below). \ref{def:ind-add-induct}'s four-stage design realises the
thesis's remark that ``$\mu t{:}F.K$ and $c_{\mu t:F.K}$ are implemented as additional
axiomatic constant symbols'' (\texttt{thesis-map.md} \S3(a)).

\textbf{What deviates.} The single biggest architectural choice is that the branch
\emph{carries} kernel-shaped recursor data (\texttt{VRecursor}, \texttt{VRecRule}, the
\texttt{VInductDecl.recs} field) and pins its shape with the 20 fields of
\ref{structure:ind-wf}, where \S2.6.1--2.6.4 instead \emph{generates} the recursor type and the
$\iota$ rules from the block's constructor specification. This survived the post-review
rework (M5 below) and is the item a maintainer is most likely to re-raise
(\texttt{contrib-iota.md} \S(e)). Positivity (\S2.6.1) is a second deviation: the thesis
builds strict positivity into the grammar of its \texttt{ctor} judgment; \ref{def:ind-ctor-result}
and \ref{def:ind-ctor-positive} instead mirror the \emph{kernel's} \texttt{checkPositivity}/
\texttt{isValidIndApp?} over an already-elaborated type, and no lemma anywhere relates the two
formulations (\texttt{thesis-map.md} \S3(a)). Within the $\iota$ rule itself, three
caveats are stated in the source: (1) the recursive-argument vector $v$ is pinned only in
\emph{number} (\texttt{RuleShape}'s \texttt{nrec}), not in the thesis's shape
$v_i = \lambda x{::}\xi_i.\ \mathrm{rec}_P\ C\ e\ \pi_i[b,x]\ (u_i\ x)$; (2) \texttt{rules\_wf}
asserts only that the kernel's stored rule is well-typed and template-headed, not that it
\emph{is} the thesis's generated rule; (3) \ref{rule:isdefeq-pat} carries no typing premise on
the reduct at all (the regularity-as-a-rule move above). The minor premise
(\texttt{MinorFor}/\texttt{MinorHeaded}) is likewise only partially pinned: neither the
$b{::}\beta$ prefix nor the inductive-hypothesis binders $\delta_i$ of \S2.6.3's $\varepsilon_c$
are constrained, and the docstring says the heads of a mutual recursor's other motives are
``unconstrained''.

\textbf{No thesis counterpart.} K-like reduction is absent: the thesis's \S4.1 second
reduction rule for subsingleton eliminators is not registered (\texttt{VRecursor.k} is
recorded and unused, and \texttt{addRecRule}'s own docstring concedes the point). More subtly,
the thesis restricts $\iota$ to \emph{non}-subsingleton inductives, with \texttt{K\textsuperscript{+}}
covering the rest; the branch instead registers $\iota$ for every constructor of every block,
diverging from \S4 on both sides of that split (\texttt{thesis-map.md} \S3(a)). The
four-stage \texttt{addInduct} split and the \texttt{Params}-instance construction
(\texttt{toParams}/\texttt{DefEqsAsPats}) are engineering devices with no thesis analogue at
all; nested inductives are excluded from \ref{structure:ind-wf} by construction
(\texttt{recs\_over\_block}, \texttt{rules\_ctor}), where the thesis's \texttt{spec} judgment
cannot express them either, but for an unrelated reason (\texttt{thesis-map.md} \S5).
