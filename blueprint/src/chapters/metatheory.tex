\chapter{Metatheory: strong typing, uniqueness, Church--Rosser}
\label{chap:metatheory}

This chapter covers the four files that carry the syntactic metatheory of the idealised
theory: \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{1} (1308 lines),
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/UniqueTyping.lean}{1} (279 lines),
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/ChurchRosser.lean}{1} (1394 lines) and
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/HeadReduction.lean}{1} (696 lines). Together they take the
single declarative judgment \texttt{VEnv.IsDefEq} of Chapter~\ref{chap:typing} and prove
about it the four things the rest of the development needs: regularity (made structural by
an annotated copy of the judgment), unique typing, confluence modulo proof irrelevance, and
a syntax-directed reading of typing that the verified type checker can be compared against.
The files are read in import order, which is also dependency order:
\texttt{Strong} $\to$ \texttt{UniqueTyping} $\to$ \texttt{ChurchRosser} $\to$
\texttt{HeadReduction}.

\begin{remark}
Notation used throughout. $\Gamma \vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$ is \texttt{VEnv.IsDefEq},
$\Gamma \vdash e : A$ is \texttt{VEnv.HasType} (the diagonal of the former), and
$\Gamma \vdash e_1 \equiv e_2$ is the untyped \texttt{VEnv.IsDefEqU}. The strong
(fully annotated) judgments are written $\Gamma \Vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$
(\texttt{IsDefEqStrong}) and $\Gamma \Vdash e : A$ / $\Gamma \Vdash e :!\, A$
(\texttt{HasTypeStrong}, with and without the conversion rule);
$\Gamma \vdash_n e : A$ is the stratified judgment. The reduction relations are
$\equiv_p$ (normal equality), $\gg$ (parallel reduction), $\ggg$ (complete parallel
reduction), $\gg^*$ (its iteration), $\gg\ll$ (Church--Rosser equality),
$\rightsquigarrow$ and $\rightsquigarrow^*$ (weak head reduction),
$\rightsquigarrow_<$ (standard reduction), $\triangleright$ and $\triangleright^*$
(type inference). All of them live under a fixed environment and universe count taken
from the class \texttt{Params} (Definition~\ref{class:cr-params}) in the last two files.
\end{remark}

Against the thesis, \texttt{Strong.lean} is Lemma \texttt{thm:reg} (Regularity) of
\texttt{typesys.tex} turned into a structural property, \texttt{UniqueTyping.lean} is
Theorem \texttt{thm:utype} of \texttt{unique.tex}, and \texttt{ChurchRosser.lean} is
\texttt{unique.tex} \texttt{\S sec:church\_rosser} almost verbatim: \texttt{NormalEq} is
$\equiv_p$, \texttt{ParRed} is $\gg_\kappa$, \texttt{ParRed.triangle} is
\texttt{thm:tri}, \texttt{NormalEq.parRed} is \texttt{thm:gg\_compat},
\texttt{ParRedS.church\_rosser} is \texttt{thm:church\_rosser} and
\texttt{IsDefEq.church\_rosser} is the forward half of \texttt{thm:ckappa}.
\texttt{HeadReduction.lean} has no thesis counterpart for its core: standardization is
taken from Kashima (2000), cited in the file header, and used together with confluence to
get the inversions ($\rightsquigarrow$ to a sort, $\rightsquigarrow$ to a $\Pi$) that the
algorithmic layer needs.

Of the 3677 lines, 173 come from the \texttt{iota} branch: 164 in \texttt{Strong.lean} and
9 in \texttt{ChurchRosser.lean}. \texttt{UniqueTyping.lean} and \texttt{HeadReduction.lean}
are byte-identical to master. In \texttt{Strong.lean} the \texttt{iota} work is of three
kinds: one new constructor \texttt{IsDefEqStrong.pat} carrying the typing of the $\iota$
reduct; a \texttt{pat} case in each of the eleven structural inductions over the strong
judgment, each following the same recipe (commute the operation with
\texttt{Pattern.RHS.apply}, transport the match and the realizer, map the check triples); and
the new environment hypothesis
\texttt{PatStrong}/\texttt{PatsStrongOn}/\texttt{OrderedStrong} with the refactoring of
master's \texttt{Ordered.strong} into reusable pieces. In \texttt{ChurchRosser.lean} it is one
class field and one five-line proof case.

The design is good and defended in docstrings a reviewer can check: annotating the strong
rule with the reduct's typing is load-bearing in five later proofs, and isolating the
resulting obligation per rule (rather than per environment) is the granularity a rule-by-rule
discharge needs. Nothing added here is dead. The cost is large and must be stated with every
claim about this chapter: on master \texttt{IsDefEq.strong} needed only \texttt{Ordered env},
which was a proved consequence of well-formedness; on this branch it needs
\texttt{OrderedStrong env}, obtainable from \texttt{VEnv.WF} only through
\texttt{VEnv.WF.orderedStrong} (\ref{thm:wf-orderedstrong}), whose \texttt{patsStrong}
component is admitted (\ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}). Every consumer of the strong system, which
includes all of \texttt{UniqueTyping.lean} and through it most of the other two files,
is therefore conditional on that admission. The declarations of \texttt{Strong.lean} itself
stay free of \texttt{sorryAx}, since they take \texttt{OrderedStrong} as a hypothesis rather
than derive it; the taint enters at the first call site that discharges it from
\texttt{VEnv.WF}, which is every theorem of \texttt{UniqueTyping.lean}. Independently of the
branches, master leaves two \texttt{sorry}s in \texttt{NormalEq.parRed} and one in
\texttt{IsDefEqU.weakN\_iff}, so Church--Rosser and everything it feeds is unproved today; and
the \texttt{Params} class has a single construction, which applies only to environments built
from axioms and inductives.

\section{The strong judgments}

\begin{definition}[The strengthened definitional equality \texttt{IsDefEqStrong}]
\label{def:isdefeqstrong}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong}
\leanok
\contrib{mixed} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{18}
A copy of \texttt{IsDefEq} in which every rule additionally carries the typing derivations
its statement implicitly needs: \texttt{bvar} carries a sort derivation for the looked-up
type, \texttt{appDF} the types of domain, codomain and instantiated codomain, \texttt{beta}
the typing of the reduct $e[e']$, \texttt{eta} the lifted typings, and \texttt{extra} both
sides of the axiom in the empty \emph{and} the current context. Every invariant that
regularity would have to re-derive can then be read off a derivation.
The \texttt{iota} branch adds exactly one constructor, \texttt{pat}
(Definition~\ref{inductive:isdefeqstrong-pat}).
\uses{def:isdefeq, def:lookup, struct:venv, def:pattern-check-realizes}
\thesisref{typesys.tex Lemma thm:reg (Regularity), made structural}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Strong $\iota$ rule, annotated with the reduct's typing]
\label{inductive:isdefeqstrong-pat}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong.pat}
\leanok
\contrib{iota} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{89}
The rule reads: if $env.pats\;p\;r$, the pattern $p$ matches $e$ with level assignment $m_1$
and subterm assignment $m_2$, $\Gamma \Vdash e : A$, $\Gamma \Vdash r_1.apply\;m_1\;m_2 : A$,
the list $chk$ realizes the side conditions $r_2$ at $m_1, m_2$, and every triple
$t \in chk$ satisfies $\Gamma \Vdash t_1 \equiv t_{2.1} : t_{2.2}$, then
$\Gamma \Vdash e \equiv r_1.apply\;m_1\;m_2 : A$. Relative to \texttt{IsDefEq.pat}
(\ref{rule:isdefeq-pat}) the single extra premise is the typing of the \emph{reduct}: subject
reduction for the rule, recorded as an annotation instead of proved later. The choice is
parallel to master's \texttt{beta} (which carries $e[e']$) and \texttt{extra} (which carries
both sides), and it is the premise that lets the \texttt{pat} case of
\ref{thm:strong-foralle-inv}, \ref{thm:strong-hastype-prime}, \ref{def:equptolevels},
\ref{thm:equptolevels-defeq} and \ref{thm:strong-substeq} close: each of those needs an
induction hypothesis for the right-hand side.
\uses{rule:isdefeq-pat, def:isdefeqstrong, def:pattern-check-realizes, def:patstrong}
\thesisref{typesys.tex ``Regularity continued'' item:red\_equiv, subject reduction for $\rightsquigarrow$}
\reviewnote{the check list is specified by \texttt{Pattern.Check.Realizes}, which constrains
only the two term components of each triple and leaves the type component free; the rule then
asks for a typed equality at that free type. Sound, but $chk$ is not determined by
$(p, r, m_1, m_2)$, so two derivations of the same rule instance may carry different $chk$.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The strengthened typing judgment \texttt{HasTypeStrong}]
\label{def:hastypestrong}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasTypeStrong}
\leanok
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{108}
A Boolean-indexed judgment, $\Gamma \Vdash e :!\, A$ at index \texttt{false} for the
structural rules and $\Gamma \Vdash e : A$ at index \texttt{true} after \texttt{base} or
\texttt{defeq}. Separating the conversion rule by an index makes inversion on a structural
rule a one-line \texttt{cases}. It has no $\iota$ rule and needs none: it is a typing, not
an equality, judgment.
\uses{def:isdefeqstrong}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Basic operations on the strong judgment]
\label{fam:strong-basic}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong.hasType,Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong.weak0,Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong.defeqDF_l,Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong.instDF}
\leanok
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{147}
Reflexivity of both sides of a strong equality (\texttt{hasType}); weakening a closed strong
judgment into any context (\texttt{weak0}); retyping along a definitionally equal context head
(\texttt{defeqDF\_l}); and the substitution congruence
$\Gamma \Vdash f[a] \equiv f'[a'] : B[a]$ from
$A :: \Gamma \Vdash f \equiv f' : B$ and $\Gamma \Vdash a \equiv a' : A$ (\texttt{instDF}).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\uses{def:isdefeqstrong, thm:strong-instn, thm:strong-weakn}
\leanok
\texttt{hasType} is $H.trans\;H.symm$ and $H.symm.trans\;H$; \texttt{weak0} lifts from the
empty context using closedness; \texttt{instDF} builds a $\beta$ redex and contracts it,
applying the rule twice, once at $B$ and once at $\mathsf{sort}\;v$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Weakening for the strong judgment]
\label{thm:strong-weakn}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong.weakN}
\leanok
\contrib{mixed} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{153}
For an ordered environment and a context lifting $W : \mathtt{Ctx.LiftN}\;n\;k\;\Gamma\;\Gamma'$,
$\Gamma \Vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$ gives
$\Gamma' \Vdash e_1{\uparrow} \equiv e_2{\uparrow} : A{\uparrow}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\uses{def:isdefeqstrong, family:pattern-transport-master, family:pattern-transport-iota}
\leanok
Induction on the derivation. The \texttt{iota} branch adds the \texttt{pat} case (193--199):
rewrite with \texttt{Pattern.RHS.liftN\_apply} so the lifted reduct is again a reduct, lift
the match with \texttt{Pattern.matches\_liftN} and the realizer with
\texttt{Realizes.map\_liftN}, then lift each check triple pointwise.
\thesisref{typesys.tex Lemma thm:weak}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The strong judgment implies the ordinary one]
\label{thm:strong-defeq}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong.defeq}
\leanok
\contrib{mixed} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{201}
$\Gamma \Vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$ implies $\Gamma \vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$: forgetting the
annotations is sound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\uses{def:isdefeq, def:isdefeqstrong, rule:isdefeq-pat}
\leanok

confirms that \texttt{IsDefEqStrong.pat} is a faithful strengthening of
\texttt{IsDefEq.pat}.

\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Equality up to universe levels]

\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.EqUpToLevels,Lean4Lean.VEnv.EqUpToLevels.instL,Lean4Lean.VEnv.EqUpToLevels.refl,Lean4Lean.VEnv.EqUpToLevels.weakN,Lean4Lean.VEnv.EqUpToLevels.instN}

\begin{definition}[The strong invariant on a closed declaration \texttt{EnvStrong}]
\label{def:envstrong}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.EnvStrong}
\leanok
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{309}
$EnvStrong\;env\;U\;e\;A$ is the conjunction of four things: $[\,] \Vdash e \equiv e : A$;
$A$ is strongly a type in the empty context; any two equivalent level instantiations of $e$
are strongly equal at $A$ instantiated on the left; and if $e$ is $\Pi A'.\,B'$ then $A'$ is
strongly a type and $B'$ is strongly a type in $[A']$.
\uses{def:isdefeqstrong}
\end{definition}

\section{Regularity, read off the annotations}

\begin{theorem}[Inversion of a $\Pi$ type in the strong judgment]
\label{thm:strong-foralle-inv}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong.forallE_inv'}
\leanok
\contrib{mixed} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{472}
Over an ordered environment whose constants and axioms satisfy \texttt{EnvStrong} and a strong
context, if $\Gamma \Vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : V$ and \emph{either} side equals
$\Pi A.\,B$, then $A$ is strongly a type in $\Gamma$ and $B$ is strongly a type in
$A :: \Gamma$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\uses{def:isdefeqstrong, def:envstrong, thm:strong-instn}
\leanok
Induction on the derivation with the equation carried as a disjunction over the two sides;
\texttt{beta} inspects the shape of the body, \texttt{extra} reads the $\Pi$ components out
of the environment invariant. The \texttt{iota} case (518--521) is four lines: whichever side
is the $\Pi$, recurse into the corresponding annotation, the redex's typing or the reduct's
typing. This case is the clearest evidence that the reduct annotation of
\texttt{IsDefEqStrong.pat} is necessary; without it there is no induction hypothesis for the
right-hand side.
\thesisref{typesys.tex Lemma thm:reg, the $\Pi$ components}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regularity for the strong judgment]
\label{thm:strong-istype}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.IsDefEqStrong.isType'}
\leanok

Under the same hypotheses, $\Gamma \Vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$ implies that $A$ is strongly a

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\uses{def:isdefeqstrong, def:envstrong, def:ctxstrong, thm:strong-foralle-inv}
\leanok

\thesisref{typesys.tex Lemma thm:reg (4)}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Replacing both sides of a strong equality by level-equal terms]
\label{thm:equptolevels-defeq}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.EqUpToLevels.defeq}
\leanok
\contrib{mixed} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{577}
Over an ordered environment whose constants and axioms satisfy \texttt{EnvStrong} and a strong
context: if $\Gamma \Vdash e_1 \equiv e_2 : A$ and $e_1$ is level-equal to $e_1'$ and $e_2$ to
$e_2'$, then $\Gamma \Vdash e_1' \equiv e_2' : A$. It is a transport lemma, not a statement
that level-equal terms are equal on their own.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\uses{def:equptolevels, def:isdefeqstrong}
\leanok
Induction on the strong derivation with inversion on the two level-equalities; the
\texttt{trans} case inserts a reflexive level-equality at the midpoint. The \texttt{iota}
case (654--657) sandwiches the unchanged rule instance
\texttt{IsDefEqStrong.pat hp hm he hred hr hall} between the two induction hypotheses by
transitivity.
\thesisref{no thesis counterpart}
\reviewnote{the \texttt{pat} case rebuilds the same rule instance rather than transporting
it. That is correct here because only the two endpoints move, but it does rely on the rule's
own components being untouched by the level change.}
\end{proof}

\section{The environment hypothesis and strengthening}

\begin{definition}[Subject reduction of one reduction rule \texttt{PatStrong}]
\label{def:patstrong}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.PatStrong}
\leanok
\contrib{iota} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{663}
$PatStrong\;env\;p\;r$ says: for every $U$, every strong context $\Gamma$, every $e$ matching
$p$ with assignments $m_1, m_2$ such that $\Gamma \Vdash e \equiv e : A$, every realizer
$chk$ of $r_2$ and every proof that each triple of $chk$ is a strong equality, the reduct
satisfies $\Gamma \Vdash r_1.apply\;m_1\;m_2 \equiv r_1.apply\;m_1\;m_2 : A$.
The granularity is per rule, so the obligation can be discharged rule by rule; all five
premises are consumed at the single use site (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{747}).
\uses{def:isdefeqstrong, def:ctxstrong, def:pattern-check-realizes}
\thesisref{typesys.tex ``Regularity continued'' item:red\_equiv; unique.tex ``Regularity of reductions'' (1)}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Subject reduction of all registered rules \texttt{PatsStrongOn}]
\label{def:patsstrongon}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.PatsStrongOn}
\leanok
\contrib{iota} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{672}
$PatsStrongOn\;env$: every $(p, r)$ with $env.pats\;p\;r$ satisfies $PatStrong\;env\;p\;r$.
The docstring records, correctly, why this is not a consequence of \texttt{Ordered}:
\texttt{Ordered} admits arbitrary well-typed definitional axioms, and under such an axiom an
$\iota$ redex can be well typed while its reduct is not. The concrete counterexample is not
here but in the docstring of \texttt{VEnv.WF.patsStrong}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/EnvLemmas.lean}{327}): under an axiom identifying
\texttt{List Nat} with \texttt{List Bool}, the redex
\texttt{List.rec Nat m n c (List.cons Bool true tl)} is well typed and its reduct is not.
\uses{def:patstrong, struct:venv, def:venv-addpat}
\thesisref{unique.tex ``Regularity of reductions'' (1)}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Environment hypothesis of the strong system \texttt{OrderedStrong}]
\label{struct:orderedstrong}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.OrderedStrong,Lean4Lean.VEnv.OrderedStrong.ordered,Lean4Lean.VEnv.OrderedStrong.strong,Lean4Lean.VEnv.OrderedStrong.pats}
\leanok
\contrib{iota} \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{679}
A structure bundling $Ordered\;env$, $OnTypes\;env\;(EnvStrong\;env)$ (every constant type and
every side of every definitional axiom is strongly typed) and $PatsStrongOn\;env$. It is the
hypothesis under which an arbitrary \texttt{IsDefEq} derivation can be strengthened.
\uses{def:ordered, def:envstrong, def:patsstrongon, def:ontypes}
\thesisref{no direct thesis counterpart; it plays the role of the environment
well-formedness assumed in the thesis' Regularity, plus the new $\iota$ obligation}
\reviewnote{this replaces master's situation, where
\texttt{Ordered.strong : Ordered env} $\to$ \texttt{OnTypes env (EnvStrong env)} was a proved
theorem and \texttt{IsDefEq.strong} needed only \texttt{Ordered env}. Here
\texttt{OrderedStrong} is reachable from \texttt{VEnv.WF} only through
\texttt{VEnv.WF.orderedStrong} (\ref{thm:wf-orderedstrong}), whose \texttt{patsStrong}
component is admitted (\ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}). The change is forced by adding $\iota$ to
\texttt{IsDefEq} and it is documented, but it is the single largest liability of the
contribution.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Abstract parameters of the Church--Rosser development]

\begin{definition}[Normal equality, the proof-irrelevance relation]

\begin{definition}[Parallel reduction]
\label{def:parred}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.ParRed}
\leanok
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/ChurchRosser.lean}{473}
$\Gamma \vdash e \gg e'$: Tait--Martin-L\"of parallel reduction, with a congruence rule for
every term former, the $\beta$ rule, and one rule \texttt{extra} for the abstract family: if
$Pat\;p\;r$, $p$ matches $e$ with $m_1, m_2$, the side conditions $r_2$ hold as
\texttt{Check.OK}, and every matched subterm reduces $m_2\;a \gg m_2'\;a$, then
$e \gg r_1.apply\;m_1\;m_2'$.

\begin{definition}[Complete parallel reduction]
\label{def:cparred}

\leanok

the reduct essentially unique, which is what lets $\ggg$ serve as the apex of the triangle
lemma.

\thesisref{unique.tex \S sec:church\_rosser, the complete reduction $\ggg_\kappa$}

\section{Contribution summary}

The \texttt{iota} branch touches two of the four files of this chapter, adding 164 lines to
\texttt{Strong.lean} and 9 to \texttt{ChurchRosser.lean}; \texttt{UniqueTyping.lean} and
\texttt{HeadReduction.lean} are byte-identical to master. The work is the downstream half of
the new \texttt{IsDefEq.pat} rule (\ref{rule:isdefeq-pat}): once $\iota$ reduction is a rule of
the declarative judgment, the metatheory spine has to be re-proved with it.

What was added, in four pieces. First, the strong rule \ref{inductive:isdefeqstrong-pat},
which mirrors \ref{rule:isdefeq-pat} and adds one premise, the typing of the reduct. This is a
design decision, not bookkeeping: it puts subject reduction for the rule into the derivation
instead of deriving it afterwards, and it is the reason the \texttt{pat} cases of
\ref{thm:strong-foralle-inv}, \ref{thm:strong-hastype-prime}, \ref{def:equptolevels},
\ref{thm:equptolevels-defeq} and \ref{thm:strong-substeq} close at all, each in one to four
lines: those are the five proofs that need an induction hypothesis for the right-hand side.
\ref{thm:strong-istype}, by contrast, reads its \texttt{pat} case off the redex annotation
alone. It is exactly parallel to
the annotations master already carries on \texttt{beta} and \texttt{extra}
(\ref{def:isdefeqstrong}). Second, a \texttt{pat} case in each of the eleven structural
inductions over the strong judgment: \ref{thm:strong-weakn}, \ref{thm:strong-defeq},
\ref{thm:strong-mono}, \ref{def:equptolevels}, \ref{thm:strong-instl},
\ref{thm:strong-instn}, \ref{thm:strong-foralle-inv}, \ref{thm:strong-istype},
\ref{thm:equptolevels-defeq}, \ref{thm:strong-hastype-prime} and \ref{thm:strong-substeq}.
Each follows one recipe, commute the operation with \texttt{Pattern.RHS.apply}, transport the
match and the realizer, map the check triples, and each rests on the transport lemmas
\ref{family:pattern-transport-iota}, which live in \texttt{Pattern.lean} where they belong.
Third, the new environment hypothesis \ref{def:patstrong}, \ref{def:patsstrongon} and
\ref{struct:orderedstrong}, stated per rule so that the obligation can be discharged rule by
rule, with the coercion \ref{instance:coeout-orderedstrong} to keep call sites unchanged; and
with it the decomposition of master's \texttt{Ordered.strong} into \ref{thm:envstrong-mono},
\ref{thm:envstrong-of-hastype}, \ref{thm:ontypes-addconst}, \ref{thm:ontypes-adddefeq} and
\ref{thm:ontypes-addpat}, so that the induction over the environment construction can move to
\texttt{EnvLemmas.lean} (\ref{thm:wf-strong}), where \texttt{pats} is under control. All five
pieces have consumers there; none is dead. Fourth, in \texttt{ChurchRosser.lean}, the class
field \ref{axiom:cr-params-pat-env} and the five-line \texttt{pat} case of
\ref{thm:isdefeq-church-rosser}, which takes one \texttt{ParRed.extra} step after converting
the rule's \texttt{Realizes} witness to a \texttt{Check.OK}.

How it connects. The choke point is \ref{thm:isdefeq-strong}: on master it needed
\texttt{Ordered env}, a proved consequence of well-formedness, and on this branch it needs
\ref{struct:orderedstrong}, which reaches \texttt{VEnv.WF} only through
\ref{thm:wf-orderedstrong}. Everything in this chapter that routes through the strong system
inherits that hypothesis: all of \ref{thm:unique-typing} and its corollaries, the inversion
lemmas \ref{fam:strong-inversions}, the substitution results
\ref{thm:strong-substeq} and \ref{thm:isdefeq-substdf}, and through those most of
\texttt{ChurchRosser.lean} and \texttt{HeadReduction.lean}.

What remains open. The \texttt{patsStrong} component of \ref{thm:wf-orderedstrong} is
\texttt{sorry} (\ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}), so every consequence of \ref{thm:isdefeq-strong} is
conditional; this is real mathematics, subject reduction for $\iota$, and it cannot be avoided
once \ref{rule:isdefeq-pat} exists. \ref{axiom:cr-params-pat-env} is discharged only by the
single \texttt{Params} instance \ref{def:indparams-toparams}, which exists only for
environments with no \texttt{def} and no quotient rule, so the $\iota$ case of
\ref{thm:isdefeq-church-rosser} is today exercised on no realistic environment. And
independently of the branch, the two \texttt{sorry}s in \ref{thm:normaleq-parred} and the one
in \ref{thm:isdefequ-weakn-iff} are master's, and they leave Church--Rosser, standardization
and \ref{thm:infertype-exists} unproved.
