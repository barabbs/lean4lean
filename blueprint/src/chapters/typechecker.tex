\chapter{Soundness of the type checker}
\label{chap:typechecker}

This chapter covers the bridge between the executable kernel re-implementation
(\texttt{Lean4Lean/TypeChecker.lean}, \texttt{Lean4Lean/Inductive/Reduce.lean}) and the model of
Lean's type theory built in \texttt{Lean4Lean/Theory/} and \texttt{Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/}.
The organising pattern is uniform: every kernel function \texttt{f} gets a companion theorem
\texttt{f.WF} saying that \emph{if} the inputs translate into the model, \emph{then} whatever
\texttt{f} returns is justified by the model. Nothing asserts that the kernel succeeds. Failure
and non-termination are unconstrained by the correctness predicate
(\ref{def:vtc-m-wf}), so every result here is partial correctness, that is soundness and never
completeness.

The six files are \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{1} (1041 lines, the
verification harness), \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Reduce.lean}{1} (156 lines,
primitive reduction), \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/WHNF.lean}{1} (309 lines, weak head
normalisation), \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/InferType.lean}{1} (519 lines, type
inference), \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/IsDefEq.lean}{1} (606 lines, algorithmic
definitional equality) and \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker.lean}{1} (252 lines, the public
interface). The four bodies are tied together by a fuel-indexed method table
(\ref{def:vtc-methods-wf}, \ref{thm:vtc-withfuel}), which is what allows each individual proof
to assume its own recursive calls already sound.

Thesis correspondence is indirect. The thesis's algorithmic equality $\Leftrightarrow$
(\texttt{axioms.tex} \S2.3) is never formalised in lean4lean, so the results that are
\emph{about} it, in particular \texttt{typesys.tex}'s \texttt{item:alg\_defn} (``if
$\Gamma \vdash e \Leftrightarrow e'$ then $\Gamma \vdash e \equiv e'$''), have no statement to
point at; this chain is their practical stand-in, with \ref{thm:vtc-isdefeqcore} as the
mechanised form. Head reduction $e \rightsquigarrow^{*} k$ corresponds to \ref{thm:vtc-whnf},
and the $\beta$, $\zeta$, $\delta$ and $\iota$ rules of \texttt{axioms.tex} to
\ref{thm:vtc-whnfcore}, \ref{thm:vtc-whnffvar}, \ref{thm:vtc-unfolddefinition} and
\ref{thm:vtc-iota-reduce-core}. The state invariant \ref{def:vtc-vstate-wf}, which makes the
kernel's five memo caches and its union-find sound, has no thesis counterpart at all.

Of the group's 2883 lines, 2720 are master. The branches contributed 163: 141 lines in
\texttt{WHNF.lean} (the $\iota$ lemma \ref{thm:vtc-iota-reduce-core}) and 22 lines in
\texttt{InferType.lean} (\ref{thm:vtc-inferproj-struct} and the corrected signature of
\ref{thm:vtc-inferproj}). The per-line blame tags all 141 \texttt{WHNF.lean} lines as
\texttt{trproj}, but \texttt{git diff iota trproj} on that file is empty and commits
\texttt{7a68882} (iota) and \texttt{6fd8a1d} (trproj) are the same patch: the lines are the
\texttt{iota} contribution, carried as a duplicate commit on \texttt{trproj} and then merged in
\texttt{20ec229}. They are attributed to \texttt{iota} below.

Assessment. The master code in this group is dense and of high quality: the statements are the
right ones (partial correctness, \texttt{FVarsBelow} retained wherever a caller needs to know
the reduct still lives in the same sub-context, cache invariants folded into the state
invariant rather than bolted on), and the proofs are terse but structured. Six \texttt{sorry}s
remain. Four are master's outright: \ref{thm:vtc-reduceprojcore}, \ref{thm:vtc-reducerecursor},
\ref{thm:vtc-tryetastruct} and \ref{thm:vtc-isdefequnitlike}. The other two are the projection
statements in \texttt{InferType.lean}, and neither is master's: \ref{thm:vtc-inferproj-struct} is
new on \texttt{trproj} (master has no such declaration) and \ref{thm:vtc-inferproj} is
\texttt{mixed}, since \texttt{trproj} rewrote master's signature and conclusion. The
\texttt{iota} contribution is a real theorem, carefully proved and
correctly documented, but it has no consumer: \ref{thm:vtc-reducerecursor} is still open, so it
is one unwritten bridging lemma away from being useful. The \texttt{trproj} contribution here
is a signature repair plus documentation rather than a result; the repair fixes a genuinely
wrong statement, and the documentation converts an invisible hole in the project's headline
soundness theorem into an explicit and well-analysed one, but all three declarations it touches
remain \texttt{sorry}.

Notation. $\Delta$ denotes the model local context (\texttt{VLCtx}) and $\Gamma$ its erasure to
a list of types; $e'$, $ty'$ and so on denote model terms (\texttt{VExpr}) and unprimed names
kernel expressions (\texttt{Expr}). ``$e$ translates to $e'$'' means
\texttt{TrExprS venv lparams} $\Delta$ \texttt{e e'} (\ref{ind:trexprs}), and its up-to-defeq
variant is \texttt{TrExpr}.

\section{The verification context}

\begin{definition}[Model local context, \texttt{MLCtx}]
  \label{def:vtc-mlctx}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.vlctx, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.lctx, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.length, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.decls, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.fvarRevList, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.letValList, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.dropN}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vt-vlctx}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{106}
  A snoc-list with two binder constructors, \texttt{vlam} and \texttt{vlet}, each carrying the
  kernel-side data (free variable id, user name, \texttt{Expr} type, binder info, and for
  \texttt{let} the value) together with the model-side data (\texttt{VExpr} type, and value).
  The projections \texttt{vlctx} and \texttt{lctx} produce the model context $\Delta$ and the
  runtime \texttt{LocalContext} respectively, so one structure keeps the two in lockstep;
  \texttt{dropN}, \texttt{fvarRevList} and \texttt{letValList} expose the last $n$ binders for
  the abstraction lemmas.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Well-formedness of an \texttt{MLCtx}]
  \label{def:vtc-mlctx-wf}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.tr, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.dropN, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.dropN_fvars_subset, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.noBV}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-mlctx, ind:trlctx, ind:trexprs, def:hastype-istype}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{158}
  \texttt{MLCtx.WF env Us c} is defined by recursion on \texttt{c}: at each binder the free
  variable must be absent from the \texttt{lctx} below it, its \texttt{Expr} type must translate
  to its \texttt{VExpr} type, and that model type must be a type (for \texttt{vlet},
  additionally the value translates and has that type). \texttt{MLCtx.WF.tr} packages this as
  the paired invariant \texttt{TrLCtx} between \texttt{c.lctx} and \texttt{c.vlctx}, built from
  \texttt{TrLCtx.mkLocalDecl} and \texttt{TrLCtx.mkLetDecl}. \texttt{noBV} records that the
  resulting $\Delta$ has no bound-variable entries.
  \thesisref{typesys.tex, Regularity; $\vdash \Gamma\ \mathsf{ok}$}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Verified reader context, \texttt{VContext}]
  \label{def:vtc-vcontext}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VContext, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VContext.trlctx, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VContext.Ewf, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VContext.Δwf, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VContext.TrExprS, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VContext.TrExpr, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VContext.TrTyping, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VContext.FVarsBelow, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VContext.MLCWF}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-mlctx-wf, ind:tr-env, ind:primspec, def:isfvarupset}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{190}
  \texttt{VContext} extends the checker's runtime \texttt{Context} with the model data it is
  verified against: a \texttt{VEnv} \texttt{venv}, a proof that it \texttt{HasPrimitives}, the
  discipline \texttt{safePrimitives} (a primitive constant in the kernel environment is
  \texttt{safe} and has no level parameters), a translation \texttt{TrEnv safety env venv}, and
  an \texttt{MLCtx} that is well formed and whose \texttt{lctx} is \emph{definitionally} the
  runtime one (\texttt{lctx\_eq}). The \texttt{nonrec abbrev}s \texttt{TrExprS},
  \texttt{TrExpr}, \texttt{IsType}, \texttt{HasType}, \texttt{IsDefEqU}, \texttt{TrTyping} and
  \texttt{FVarsBelow} specialise the model relations at this context, and \texttt{MLCWF} is the
  instance class letting a proof switch to an extended \texttt{MLCtx}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Checker state invariant, \texttt{VState.WF}]
  \label{def:vtc-vstate-wf}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VState, Lean4Lean.InferCache.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.WHNFCache.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.UnfoldCache.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VState.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VState.LE, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.VState.WF.find?_eq_none}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-vcontext, def:conditionallyhastype, def:conditionallywhnf, def:kernel-equiv-manager}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{229}
  The invariant every mutable field of the checker's state must satisfy: the runtime local
  context translates, every free variable in scope is reserved by the name generator, there is
  a lifted context $\Delta'$ for which the union-find \texttt{eqvManager} is sound, and each
  memo table stores only justified entries, namely \texttt{inferTypeI}/\texttt{inferTypeC}
  against \texttt{ConditionallyHasType}, the two whnf caches against \texttt{ConditionallyWHNF},
  and \texttt{unfold} against the $\delta$-value of a constant. \texttt{VState.LE} is
  monotonicity of the name generator, and \texttt{find?\_eq\_none} turns non-reservation of an
  id into its absence from the local context.
  \reviewnote{The cache clauses are what make the kernel's memoisation sound and they are the
  least thesis-like part of the development; Carneiro's text has no counterpart.}
\end{definition}

\section{The program logic}

\begin{definition}[Partial-correctness predicate for the checker monad, \texttt{M.WF}]
  \label{def:vtc-m-wf}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.bind, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.pure, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.map, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.mono, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.throw, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.le}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-vstate-wf}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{278}
  \texttt{M.WF c s x Q} says: if \texttt{s} satisfies the state invariant, then whenever
  \texttt{x} run at \texttt{c} and \texttt{s} returns \texttt{.ok (a, s')} there is a
  \texttt{VState} whose underlying state is \texttt{s'}, which extends \texttt{s}, still
  satisfies the invariant, and for which \texttt{Q a} holds. A thrown exception or a
  non-terminating run satisfies the predicate vacuously, which is exactly why every
  \texttt{.WF} lemma in the chapter is a soundness and not a completeness statement. The
  accompanying \texttt{bind}, \texttt{pure}, \texttt{map}, \texttt{mono}, \texttt{throw} and
  \texttt{le} combinators are direct unfoldings of the
  \texttt{ReaderT}/\texttt{StateT}/\texttt{Except} stack.
  \thesisref{typesys.tex, \texttt{item:alg\_defn}: soundness of the algorithm only}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Soundness contract of the method table, \texttt{Methods.WF}]
  \label{def:vtc-methods-wf}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Methods.WF}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-m-wf, def:isfvarupset}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{314}
  The kernel's four mutually recursive entry points are bundled as a record of closures;
  \texttt{Methods.WF m} states the specification each field must meet. \texttt{isDefEqCore}
  returning \texttt{true} on two translating terms yields \texttt{IsDefEqU} of their
  translations; \texttt{whnfCore} and \texttt{whnf} return a term that is \texttt{FVarsBelow}
  the input and \texttt{TrExpr}-translates to the same model term; \texttt{inferType}, given
  that the input's free variables are in scope and (at \texttt{inferOnly}) that it already
  translates, returns a type \texttt{ty} with $\exists\, e'\, ty'$,
  \texttt{TrTyping e ty e' ty'}.
  \thesisref{axioms.tex, \S Definitional equality ($\Leftrightarrow$) and head reduction $e \rightsquigarrow^{*} k$}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[The fuelled recursive monad and its program logic, \texttt{RecM.WF}]
  \label{def:vtc-recm-wf}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.liftExcept, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.lift, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.bind, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.bind_le, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.mono, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.get, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.getEnv, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.getLCtx, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.readThe, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.getNGen, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.stateWF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.toLBoolM}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-m-wf, def:vtc-methods-wf}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{325}
  \texttt{RecM.WF c s x Q} quantifies \texttt{M.WF} over every method table satisfying
  \texttt{Methods.WF}, so a proof about a \texttt{RecM} program may assume its recursive calls
  are already sound. The family around it is a small program logic: monadic \texttt{bind}
  (with a variant carrying an earlier state bound), \texttt{mono}, reading the state,
  environment, local context and name generator, \texttt{stateWF} to expose the state
  invariant inside a proof, lifting from \texttt{Except} and from \texttt{M}, and transport
  through the \texttt{Bool}/\texttt{LBool} coercions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Each lemma is a routine unfolding of the monad transformer stack on top of the corresponding
  \texttt{M.WF} combinator; \texttt{lift} is also registered as a \texttt{Coe}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Binder introduction preserves the invariant]
  \label{thm:vtc-withlocaldecl}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.withLocalDecl, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.withLocalDecl, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.withLetDecl}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-mlctx-wf, def:vtc-vstate-wf, def:vtc-recm-wf}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{439}
  Let \texttt{m} be a well-formed \texttt{MLCtx}, \texttt{ty} translate to \texttt{ty'} and
  \texttt{ty'} be a type. If the continuation \texttt{f (.fvar id)} satisfies \texttt{Q} in the
  context extended by \texttt{m.vlam id name ty ty' bi}, for every \texttt{id} not reserved by
  the current name generator, then \texttt{withLocalDecl name bi ty f} satisfies \texttt{Q} at
  \texttt{m}. \texttt{withLetDecl} is the \texttt{vlet} analogue, and there is an \texttt{M}
  level duplicate of the first because the primitive checker introduces binders outside
  \texttt{RecM}.
  \thesisref{typesys.tex, Weakening (\texttt{thm:weak})}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{def:vtc-vstate-wf}
  \leanok
  The fresh id is \texttt{s.ngen.curr}, which is not reserved by \texttt{s} but is by
  \texttt{s.next}; \texttt{VState.WF.find?\_eq\_none} turns this into absence from the local
  context, which is the missing clause of \texttt{MLCtx.WF}. The state invariant is then
  rebuilt at \texttt{s.next}, each cache clause being re-validated under the extended context by
  the \texttt{fresh} and \texttt{mono} lemmas of \texttt{ConditionallyHasType} and
  \texttt{ConditionallyWHNF}.
\end{proof}

\section{Abstraction of binders}

\begin{definition}[Re-abstraction of the last $n$ binders]
  \label{def:vtc-mkbinders}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.mkForall, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.mkForall', Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.mkLambda, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.mkLambda', Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.mkLetArg, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.mkLet, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.AllAbove, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.AllAbove.wf}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-mlctx}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{588}
  \texttt{MLCtx.mkForall c n hn e} abstracts the last $n$ free variables of \texttt{e} back into
  binders, turning a \texttt{vlet} binder into a \texttt{letE} node only when the body actually
  mentions it and lowering the loose bound variable otherwise; \texttt{mkLambda} and
  \texttt{mkLet} are the analogues, and the primed versions do the same on the model side over
  \texttt{VExpr}. \texttt{AllAbove} $\Delta$ \texttt{P} restricts a free-variable predicate to
  the variables already present in $\Delta$, which is what makes \texttt{FVarsBelow} statements
  stable under binder introduction.
  \thesisref{axioms.tex, telescopes $\lambda x{:}\alpha.\ \beta$}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Abstraction commutes with translation]
  \label{thm:vtc-mkbinder-tr}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.mkForall_trS, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.mkForall_hasType, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.mkForall'_congr, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.mkForall_tr, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.mkLambda_trS, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.mkLambda'_congr, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.mkLambda_tr, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.mkLet_trS}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-mkbinders, def:vtc-mlctx-wf, ind:trexprs}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{657}
  If a body translates in the full context and its model type is a type, then its $n$-fold
  abstraction translates, in the context dropped by $n$, to the corresponding model
  abstraction, and is itself a type; \texttt{mkForall\_hasType} gives the typing of the
  abstracted pi type from the list of level translations. The primed \texttt{congr} versions
  transport a model \texttt{IsDefEq} between bodies to one between the abstractions, and the
  unprimed \texttt{\_tr} versions are the \texttt{TrExpr} (up-to-defeq) forms.
  \thesisref{typesys.tex, Weakening (\texttt{thm:weak}) and Substitution (\texttt{thm:subst})}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{thm:trexprs-abstract}
  \leanok
  Induction on $n$ along the \texttt{MLCtx}. The \texttt{vlam} step applies
  \texttt{TrExprS.forallE} to \texttt{TrExprS.abstract}; the \texttt{vlet} step either builds a
  \texttt{TrExprS.letE} or, when the bound variable is unused, rewrites
  \texttt{lowerLooseBVars} into an instantiation and uses \texttt{TrExprS.inst\_let}. The
  freshness side conditions come from \texttt{MLCtx.WF}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Bookkeeping for the model local context]
  \label{thm:vtc-mlctx-admin}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.fvarRevList_prefix, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.fvars_eq_append, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.isFVarUpSet_dropN, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.fvars_nodup, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.fvarRevList_nodup, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.decls_size, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.toList_eq, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.find?_eq, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.mkForall_eq, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.MLCtx.WF.mkLambda_eq}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-mlctx-wf, def:vtc-mkbinders}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{791}
  Routine facts: the reversed free-variable list of the last $n$ binders is a prefix of all of
  them and its entries are pairwise distinct; \texttt{MLCtx.decls} agrees with the runtime
  \texttt{LocalContext}'s \texttt{toList} and \texttt{find?}; dropping a prefix keeps an
  up-set of free variables an up-set; and \texttt{LocalContext.mkForall}/\texttt{mkLambda}
  applied to the matching array agree with \ref{def:vtc-mkbinders}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Structural inductions on the \texttt{MLCtx}. The last two rewrite
  \texttt{LocalContext.mkBinding} into a fold of \texttt{mkBindingList1} and then match it
  binder by binder, the nodup facts being what licenses the congruence at each step.
\end{proof}

\section{The recursive-call interface}

\begin{lemma}[Recursive-call interface]
  \label{thm:vtc-methods-iface}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.whnf.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.isDefEqCore.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.inferType.WF', Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.inferType.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.inferType.WF_uniq, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.checkType.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.whnfCore.WF}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-methods-wf}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{933}
  The four \texttt{Methods} fields re-exposed as \texttt{RecM.WF} lemmas, so that the individual
  soundness proofs can call them. \texttt{inferType.WF} strengthens \texttt{inferType.WF'} when
  the caller already holds a translation \texttt{e'}, concluding
  $\exists\, ty'$, \texttt{TrTyping e ty e' ty'} with the caller's own \texttt{e'};
  \texttt{inferType.WF\_uniq} combines it with uniqueness of typing so that the inferred type
  translates to any model type the term is already known to have; \texttt{checkType.WF} is
  \texttt{inferType.WF'} at \texttt{inferOnly := false}, where the translation hypothesis is
  vacuous.
  \thesisref{unique.tex, \texttt{thm:unique} (uniqueness of typing)}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{thm:trexprs-uniq, thm:isdefeq-iff, fam:defeq-mixing}
  \leanok
  \texttt{WF} replaces the existentially quantified translation by the caller's using
  \texttt{TrExprS.uniq} and \texttt{HasType.defeqU\_l}; \texttt{WF\_uniq} then applies
  \texttt{uniqU}. Since \texttt{TrExprS.uniq} still rests on the open \ref{thm:trproj-uniq},
  everything downstream of \texttt{inferType.WF} carries \texttt{sorryAx}.
  \axfoot{sorryAx via TrExprS.uniq -> TrProj.uniq (Verify/Typing/Lemmas.lean:995)}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Soundness of $\delta$ reduction]
  \label{thm:vtc-unfolddefinition}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.isDelta_is_some, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.UnfoldDefinition.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.unfoldDefinitionCore.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.unfoldDefinition.WF}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-recm-wf, def:isfvarupset}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/Basic.lean}{978}
  \texttt{UnfoldDefinition.WF c e e0 e'} is a two-sided specification of an
  \texttt{Option Expr}: on \texttt{some e1} the reduct is \texttt{FVarsBelow e} and translates
  to \texttt{e'}, and on \texttt{none} there is no constant with a $\delta$-value at the head
  of \texttt{e0} with the right number of level arguments.
  \texttt{unfoldDefinitionCore.WF} establishes it for a constant head, including soundness of
  the \texttt{unfold} memo cache, and \texttt{unfoldDefinition.WF} lifts it through an
  application spine.

\begin{theorem}[The kernel's $\iota$ step agrees with the registered $\iota$ rule]
  \label{thm:vtc-iota-reduce-core}

  \texttt{mkAppList (.const recName ls) as} translates to \texttt{e'}; that the argument at

\begin{lemma}[Soundness of unit-like structure comparison (open)]

\begin{lemma}[Soundness of projection-headed lazy delta reduction]
  \label{thm:vtc-lazydeltaproj}

\begin{theorem}[Soundness of the algorithmic definitional-equality checker]
  \label{thm:vtc-isdefeqcore}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Inner.isDefEqCore'.WF}
  \leanok
  \uses{ind:trexprs, def:isdefeq}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/IsDefEq.lean}{528}
  If \texttt{e1} and \texttt{e2} translate to $e_1'$ and $e_2'$ and
  \texttt{isDefEqCore' e1 e2} returns \texttt{true}, then $e_1'$ and $e_2'$ are definitionally
  equal in the model. This is the mechanised form of ``algorithmic equality implies
  definitional equality''; the converse is false, and the thesis stresses that the algorithmic
  relation is not even transitive.
  \thesisref{typesys.tex, \texttt{item:alg\_defn}: if $\Gamma \vdash e \Leftrightarrow e'$ and both are typed then $\Gamma \vdash e \equiv e'$}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{thm:vtc-quickisdefeq, thm:vtc-whnfcore, thm:vtc-methods-iface, thm:vtc-proofirrel, thm:vtc-lazydeltaloop, thm:vtc-isdefeqapp, thm:vtc-tryetaexpansion, thm:vtc-tryetastruct, thm:vtc-trystringlit, thm:vtc-isdefequnitlike, thm:vtc-lazydeltaproj}
  \leanok

  \texttt{Bool} literal shortcut, \texttt{whnfCore} on both sides, proof irrelevance, the
  lazy-delta loop, comparison of constant, free-variable and projection heads, then
  \texttt{isDefEqApp}, $\eta$ expansion, structure $\eta$, string-literal expansion and the
  unit-like case. The running \texttt{IsDefEqU} facts are composed with \texttt{trans} and
  \texttt{symm} at every stage. It is partial because of \ref{thm:vtc-tryetastruct},
  \ref{thm:vtc-isdefequnitlike} and, through \texttt{whnf}, \ref{thm:vtc-reducerecursor} and
  \ref{thm:vtc-reduceprojcore}.
  \axfoot{sorryAx via tryEtaStructCore.WF, isDefEqUnitLike.WF, reduceRecursor.WF, reduceProjCore.WF}
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Model environments at every safety level]

  that the family is antitone in safety. \texttt{VEnvs.axiom\_of\_choice} turns a pointwise
  existential into such a bundle; despite the name it is a three-way case split, and its
  docstring says so.
  \reviewnote{A reader grepping the project for uses of choice will be misled by the name, even
  though the docstring is explicit.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Model environment at a single safety level]
  \label{def:vtc-venvat}

  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-venvs}

  \texttt{safe}-level model of that environment exists.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The fuel-indexed method table is sound]
  \label{thm:vtc-withfuel}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.Methods.withFuel.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.RecM.WF.run}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-methods-wf, def:vtc-recm-wf}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker.lean}{48}
  For every fuel \texttt{n}, \texttt{Methods.withFuel n} satisfies \texttt{Methods.WF}: at fuel
  \texttt{0} every method throws, which is vacuously sound, and at fuel \texttt{n+1} each
  method is the corresponding primed soundness proof applied to the table at fuel \texttt{n}.
  Consequently \texttt{RecM.run x} inherits any \texttt{RecM.WF} property of \texttt{x}.
  \thesisref{typesys.tex, \S Undecidability of definitional equality: the fuel bound is why the checker terminates at all}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{thm:vtc-isdefeqcore, thm:vtc-whnfcore, thm:vtc-whnf, thm:vtc-infertype-prime}
  \leanok
  Induction on \texttt{n}, feeding the induction hypothesis to \ref{thm:vtc-isdefeqcore},
  \ref{thm:vtc-whnfcore}, \ref{thm:vtc-whnf} and \ref{thm:vtc-infertype-prime}. This is where
  the mutual recursion is tied off, and the reason every individual proof above may assume its
  recursive calls sound.
  \axfoot{sorryAx via all four component theorems}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Discharging the monad at the top level]
  \label{thm:vtc-m-run}

\begin{lemma}[Loop rules for the declaration checker]
  \label{thm:vtc-forin}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.forIn, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.bindThrow, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.forInFresh, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.M.WF.forInForall₂}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-m-wf}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker.lean}{113}
  Three Hoare rules for \texttt{for ... in} loops in \texttt{M}: a generic invariant rule
  indexed by the list still to be processed, so that the conclusion \texttt{Inv []} records
  that every element was handled (the body must \texttt{yield}, since a loop that can
  \texttt{break} is out of scope and none of the kernel's loops do); a rule for
  \texttt{addMutual}'s header loop whose accumulator is the set of names seen, concluding that
  the block is duplicate-free; and a rule for a loop whose elements are already
  \texttt{Forall2}-related to a list of data. \texttt{bindThrow} discharges a bind whose first
  component cannot return.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Induction on the list, threading the invariant through \texttt{M.WF.bind}; the fresh-name
  rule additionally derives \texttt{Nodup} from the per-iteration freshness test. These rules
  are consumed by the declaration-checking group, not by this one.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The public soundness interface of the kernel]
  \label{thm:vtc-toplevel}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.whnf.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.whnfCore.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.unfoldDefinition.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.inferType.WF', Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.inferType.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.checkType.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.isDefEq.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.isProp.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.ensureSort.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.ensureForall.WF, Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.ensureType.WF', Lean4Lean.TypeChecker.ensureType.WF}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vtc-m-wf, def:isfvarupset}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker.lean}{186}
  Every entry point the declaration checker calls, stated at the \texttt{M} level:
  \texttt{whnf} and \texttt{whnfCore} preserve the model term and the free-variable bound;
  \texttt{unfoldDefinition} falls back to its input when there is nothing to unfold, so its
  result is always model-equal; \texttt{inferType} and \texttt{checkType} produce a
  \texttt{TrTyping}; \texttt{isDefEq} returning \texttt{true} gives \texttt{IsDefEqU};
  \texttt{isProp} gives a typing at \texttt{.sort .zero}; and
  \texttt{ensureSort}/\texttt{ensureForall}/\texttt{ensureType} additionally pin the syntactic
  shape of the result.
  \thesisref{typesys.tex, \texttt{item:alg\_defn}; soundness.tex, the Corollary}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{thm:vtc-withfuel, thm:vtc-infertype-prime, thm:vtc-isdefeqcore, thm:vtc-whnf, thm:vtc-proofirrel, thm:vtc-ensuresortcore, thm:vtc-ensureforallcore}

  \texttt{ensureType.WF'} additionally chains \texttt{inferType.WF'} with \texttt{ensureSort.WF}

  verification consumes, and every item in it is partial because of the six remaining

\end{proof}

\section{Contribution summary}

The two branches added 163 lines to this group, in two disjoint places.

\textbf{\texttt{iota}.} Upstream, \texttt{inductiveReduceRec} was one monolithic kernel
function. The branch split out the pure $\iota$ step as \texttt{inductiveReduceRecCore}
(\ref{def:kernel-iota-core}) and proved \ref{thm:vtc-iota-reduce-core} about it, 129 lines of
tactic script. The decomposition is the right one: the K-like conversion, the
literal-to-constructor conversion and the structure-eta of the major premise are monadic and
belong in the caller, whereas the $\iota$ step itself is pure and is the only part that has to
be matched against the model's registered rules (\ref{thm:tr-env-iota-rec}). The proof is a
genuinely fiddly index-arithmetic argument, carried out by slicing both spines and matching them
against the pattern machinery of \ref{thm:pattern-matches-varn-const} and
\ref{thm:pattern-iota-rhs-apply}; it is well commented, contains no \texttt{sorry}, and its
docstring is accurate about the saturation caveat. What it does not do is connect to anything:
\ref{thm:vtc-reducerecursor} is still open, no other declaration in the repository mentions the
lemma, and the hypothesis that the major premise is already a constructor application is
stronger than what the kernel's call site provides. The value of the contribution is therefore
prospective, and the missing bridging lemma is exactly the step that would make it a statement
about a kernel step rather than about a syntactic redex.

\textbf{\texttt{trproj}.} Twenty-two lines in the projection case of type inference. The
signature of \ref{thm:vtc-inferproj} was repaired, and the repair is real: master's version had
an auto-bound implicit standing where the caller's type should be, and asserted that the
projection translates to the translation of its own subterm. \ref{thm:vtc-inferproj-struct} was
added as the same statement scoped to the structures \texttt{TrProjCtor}
(\ref{struct:trprojctor}) covers. The substantive contribution, however, is the documentation:
it records that the general statement is not provable as stated and why, and lists the four
model extensions that would be needed to close it. That is an honest and correct diagnosis of a
pre-existing hole in the project's headline soundness theorem. Against it: all three
declarations remain \texttt{sorry}, \ref{thm:vtc-inferproj-struct} has no consumer at all, its
hypotheses are kernel metadata rather than \texttt{TrProjCtor}, and the two obligations that the
new \texttt{TrProj} metatheory was built to support and that sit in this very group,
\ref{thm:vtc-reduceprojcore} and \ref{thm:vtc-tryetastruct}, were not attempted.
