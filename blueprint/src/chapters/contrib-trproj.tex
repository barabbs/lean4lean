\chapter{Contribution: structure projections (trproj)}
\label{chap:contrib-trproj}

\section{Goal}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Goal} Replace \texttt{TrProj} (\ref{def:trproj}), the relation translating a kernel
projection \texttt{.proj S i e} into the model, with a real definition, and prove as many of its
seven inherited structural lemmas as the definition allows.
\item \lead{Why} on master \texttt{TrProj := sorry} is opaque, not merely unproved: nothing can be
constructed in it or refuted about it (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/Expr.lean}{68}).
\item \lead{Caveat} while \texttt{TrProj} stayed opaque, every declaration whose type merely
mentioned \texttt{TrExprS} or \texttt{TrExpr} carried \texttt{sorryAx} by construction: an
environment containing a structure projection was permanently out of reach of \texttt{TrEnv}.
\end{itemize}

\section{What it adds}
\label{sec:contrib-trproj-design}

The design: a projection is not a new syntactic form but an application of the recursor the
kernel already generates for the structure, $P_i = S.\mathrm{rec}\;(uss\,i)\;ps\;(\lambda
x.\,F_i[f_j := P_j\,x])\;(\lambda f_0\dots f_{n-1}.\,f_i)$, chaining earlier fields' projection
functions into later fields' motives so a dependent field type is expressible.

\item \lead{Caveat} \texttt{TrProj} is not purely syntactic: \texttt{major\_ty} and

\section{Main results}

\begin{itemize}
\item \stTainted{} \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq} (\texttt{TrEnv.proj\_defeq}) -- the headline
theorem: the recursor expansion of projection $i$ reduces to \texttt{fields[i]} when the major
premise is a saturated constructor spine, over the telescope split \ref{thm:tr-env-structure-rec}
(\stProved{}, derived from the kernel's own structure facts, not assumed as a hypothesis). A
complete, non-circular proof, derived from the registered $\iota$ rule (\ref{rule:isdefeq-pat})
rather than assumed; its own body is sorry-free, but its \texttt{sorryAx} cone inherits master's
type-former injectivity gaps and \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}.
\item \stProved{} \ref{thm:trproj-weak}, \ref{thm:trproj-wf}, \ref{thm:trproj-instn},
\ref{thm:trproj-instl} -- four of \texttt{TrProj}'s seven inherited structural lemmas.
\item \stProved{} \ref{thm:trproj-mono} -- a new obligation this contribution introduced for
itself, required by \texttt{TrExprS.mono}.
\item \stTainted{} \ref{thm:trproj-defeqdfc} -- the fifth inherited lemma; proved, but its
\texttt{sorryAx} cone includes \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}.
\item \stTainted{} \ref{thm:vtc-iota-reduce-core} (\texttt{inductiveReduceRecCore.WF}) -- the first
internal consumer of the registered-$\iota$-rule interface (\ref{thm:tr-env-iota-rec}); inherits
\texttt{sorryAx} from \ref{thm:trproj-uniq}.
\item \stProved{} 614 of the 663 declarations whose type mentions \texttt{TrExprS}/\texttt{TrExpr}
are now \texttt{sorryAx}-free, up from zero while \texttt{TrProj} was a \texttt{sorry} definition;
\texttt{TrProj} and \texttt{TrProjCtor} themselves print no axiom beyond \texttt{propext}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Relation to the thesis}

\begin{itemize}
\item \lead{Caveat} Carneiro's thesis never treats \texttt{Expr.proj}: structures, structure
$\eta$ and \texttt{inferProj} go unmentioned throughout.
\item \lead{Idea} the expansion's \hl{shape} borrows the thesis's
$\mathrm{inv}_x:\mathrm{acc}\,x\to\forall y.\,y<x\to\mathrm{acc}\,y$ (typesys.tex's
undecidability-of-conversion section, built from $\mathrm{rec}_{\mathrm{acc}}$ with a non-constant
motive), generalised by unique.tex: ``these $\mathrm{inv}_i$ projection operators can be defined
using the recursor, like we demonstrated for \texttt{acc}''.
\item \lead{Idea} the motive's dependent typing, $P_i\,e : F_i[f_j := P_j\,e]$, follows the shape
of the primitive $\Sigma$-projection rule $\pi_2\,p : \beta[\pi_1\,p/x]$ (Wtypes.tex \S5.1); the
per-field level list \texttt{uss} follows axioms.tex \S2.6.3's remark that the recursor's motive
universe is fresh per use.
\item \lead{Caveat} Wtypes.tex's own system -- eight primitives, a \texttt{good} predicate, and
subsingleton eliminators into a parameterised \texttt{Acc} -- is not formalised here at all; the
borrowed idiom is a shape, not an inherited result.
\item \lead{Caveat} Wtypes.tex's $\Sigma$ carries an $\eta$ rule, $(\pi_1 x,\pi_2 x)\equiv x$,
precisely because it has no recursor for $\Sigma$ to fall back on -- the one rule this model cannot
state, since \texttt{IsDefEq} (\ref{def:isdefeq}) has no structure-$\eta$ clause. Three positions
disagree: the thesis has $\Sigma$-$\eta$; the real kernel has structure $\eta$
(\texttt{tryEtaStructCore}, \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/IsDefEq.lean}{227}, still
\stSorry{}); this model has neither.
\item \lead{Then} independent of the borrowed vocabulary, \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq}
\hl{derives} the thesis's computation rule $\pi_i(a,b)\equiv a_i$ from the registered $\iota$ rule
(\ref{rule:isdefeq-pat}), rather than assuming it.
\end{itemize}

\section{Open items}
\label{sec:contrib-trproj-consumers}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \ref{thm:trproj-uniq} -- the highest-value gap. Its residual is unique typing of the
projection function, type-former injectivity, and functionality of the $\iota$ registry; closing
it removes \texttt{sorryAx} from the translation's whole uniqueness layer.
\item \ref{thm:add-decl-wf}'s \texttt{inductDecl} case -- without a producer for
\texttt{AddInduct}, no result above applies to a concrete environment; prerequisite work shared
with the $\iota$ contribution (Chapter~\ref{chap:contrib-iota}).
\item \ref{thm:vtc-inferproj-struct} -- inhabiting it, even under \texttt{TrProjCtor}'s scope
restriction (non-mutual, single-constructor, non-recursive, non-indexed), is what would let a real
kernel projection reach \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq}.
\item \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong} -- not owned by this contribution, but the one admission that taints
\ref{thm:trproj-defeqdfc} and \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq} alike.
\item \lead{Caveat} \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq} has no consumer today: its three intended
consumers (\ref{thm:vtc-reduceprojcore}, \ref{thm:vtc-inferproj-struct}, \ref{thm:vtc-inferproj})
are all still \stSorry{}, and \texttt{TrProjCtor} is inhabited only by hand, on two test fixtures.
\end{enumerate}
