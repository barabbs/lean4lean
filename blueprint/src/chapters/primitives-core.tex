\chapter{Primitive operations: core}
\label{chap:primitives-core}

Lean's kernel does not evaluate \texttt{Nat.add} by unfolding its recursive definition: it
recognises a fixed list of definitions by name and shape and then computes them with GMP. That
acceleration is sound only if the definition the kernel recognised really denotes the arithmetic
operation it substitutes. This chapter is the verification of that recognition, up to but not
including the eighteen per-primitive branches, which are Chapter~\ref{chap:primitives-arith}.

The recogniser itself (\texttt{Primitive.checkDef},
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Primitive.lean}{561}) does dispatch on the definition's name and compares its
type syntactically, but nothing about its \emph{meaning} is read off the name. It opens probe
variables with \texttt{withLocalDecl} and runs \texttt{isDefEq} tests on the definition's
\emph{value}: for \texttt{Nat.add} it checks
$f\,x\,0 \equiv x$ in context $[\mathtt{nat}]$ and
$f\,x\,(\mathtt{succ}\,y) \equiv \mathtt{succ}\,(f\,x\,y)$ in context
$[\mathtt{nat},\mathtt{nat}]$, both as \emph{open} equations. The four files of this chapter turn
those open equations into the closed $\mathsf{Reflects}$ statements that
$\mathsf{HasPrimitives}$ records, and transport them from the value onto the constant that
\texttt{addDefEq} introduces; every statement below the transfer is about that value as an
opaque \texttt{VExpr} and mentions no constant at all.

The files are
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{1} (595 lines; the reflection schemas and the
value-to-constant transfer),
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Basic.lean}{1} (1399 lines; the vocabulary every
branch shares: telescope combinators, ground judgements, the per-branch \texttt{Data} record and
the \texttt{PrimitiveResult} obligation),
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{1} (1775 lines; the
well-founded-recursion recogniser, whose only two callers are \texttt{Nat.gcd}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Primitive.lean}{373}) and \texttt{Nat.bitwise}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Primitive.lean}{412}); \texttt{Nat.div} and \texttt{Nat.mod} use the fuel
recursion of \texttt{Basic.lean} instead), and
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive.lean}{1} (195 lines; the verification boundary
\texttt{checkDef.WF}, consumed at \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Checker.lean}{201}, plus a
pre-staged specification of the recogniser's inductive clause).

There is no thesis counterpart for the subject matter: Carneiro's system has no GMP-accelerated
primitives, and \texttt{Verify/} as a whole formalises no part of the thesis. The points of
contact are ambient only, and they are cited node by node below: the $\delta$ rule and the
conservativity of definitions (\texttt{axioms.tex}, Definitions), Regularity, Weakening and the
properties of substitution (\texttt{typesys.tex}), and unique typing with definitional inversion
(\texttt{unique.tex}). A node below that carries no thesis-correspondence line has no
counterpart at all. In blueprint terms this chapter is an \emph{extension} of the thesis, not
a formalisation of it: it discharges obligations the real kernel incurs that the thesis's system
does not have.

Of the 3964 lines in these four files, 3888 are master. The contributed delta is 71 lines on
\texttt{iota} and 5 on \texttt{trproj}, and it is a single mechanical edit: twenty-seven
declarations changed an \texttt{env.Ordered} hypothesis to \texttt{env.OrderedStrong}, forty-five
call sites changed \texttt{x.ordered} to \texttt{x.orderedStrong}, one body line at
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{80} became \texttt{henv.ordered.constWF} because
\texttt{OrderedStrong} is a structure over \texttt{Ordered}, and the five \texttt{trproj} lines
re-wrap one signature and one call site that the \texttt{iota} edit had pushed past 100 columns.
No statement about primitives changed and no proof was weakened. What did change is the trust
boundary: \texttt{OrderedStrong} is obtained from \texttt{VEnv.WF} only through
\texttt{VEnv.WF.orderedStrong}, which is built on the admitted \texttt{VEnv.WF.patsStrong}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/EnvLemmas.lean}{334}), so this layer now depends on the
$\iota$ contribution's open obligation, and nineteen declarations in these files that were
\texttt{sorryAx}-free before the branch are not any more. That dependency, and the fact that the
edit was applied uniformly rather than minimally, are the two review points of the chapter; see
Section~\ref{sec:vpc-contribution} and Section~\ref{sec:vpc-review}.

The master code these files consist of is of high quality. Its docstrings routinely say why an
interface has the shape it has and what the recogniser does \emph{not} check, the statements are
the right ones (reflections are proved about the opaque value and only then moved onto the
constant; \texttt{PrimitiveResult} quantifies over well-formed extensions so the recogniser's
syntax stays out of the metatheory; the well-founded recursion's caller obligation is restricted
to smaller measures, so it is the real termination argument). The weak points are all
pre-existing: one 1135-line tactic proof, a syntactic guard on the measure that is a hypothesis
rather than a check, and roughly 120 dead lines in
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive.lean}{73}.

\begin{remark}
Notation. $\Gamma \vdash e : A$ is \texttt{VEnv.HasType}, $\Gamma \vdash e \equiv e'$ is the
type-erased \texttt{VEnv.IsDefEqU} (the typed form carries its type explicitly), and
$\overline{k}$ is the model numeral \texttt{VExpr.natLit k}, that is $k$ applications of
\texttt{Nat.succ} to \texttt{Nat.zero}. All judgements are relative to a universe-parameter count
$U$, suppressed when it is $0$. Throughout, \texttt{HasPrimitives} is the invariant recording,
for each reserved name, what the model assumes about it; it is a fact about the \emph{empty}
context, which is why so much of this chapter is about weakening it to a context of interest and
back.
\end{remark}

\section{Reflection for the primitive definitions}

This section covers \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{1}. It first restates the
\texttt{HasPrimitives} facts in the forms the recurrence proofs consume, then proves the five
recurrence schemas, then carries a reflection of the value onto the constant.

\begin{lemma}[Numerals are closed]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{90}
\label{vpc:lem:prim-closedn-natlit}\lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.closedN_natLit}\leanok
The model numeral $\overline{n}$ is \texttt{ClosedN k} at every de Bruijn depth $k$.
\uses{def:closedn}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
Induction on $n$, unfolding \texttt{VExpr.natLit}, \texttt{natZero} and \texttt{natSucc}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Typing of the Nat/Bool constructors and numerals at an arbitrary context]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{29}
\label{vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natZeroT, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natSuccT, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natPredT, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natLitT, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.boolLitT, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natIsType, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.boolIsType}\leanok
Seven wrappers, all assuming \texttt{env.OrderedStrong}, \texttt{env.HasPrimitives} and the
relevant containment. For \emph{any} context $\Gamma$:
$\Gamma \vdash \mathtt{Nat.zero} : \mathtt{nat}$,
$\Gamma \vdash \mathtt{Nat.succ} : \mathtt{nat} \to \mathtt{nat}$,
$\Gamma \vdash \mathtt{Nat.pred} : \mathtt{nat} \to \mathtt{nat}$ (given
\texttt{contains Nat.pred}),
$\Gamma \vdash \overline{k} : \mathtt{nat}$ at every universe count $U$, and
$\Gamma \vdash \mathtt{boolLit}\ b : \mathtt{bool}$; and $\mathtt{nat}$, $\mathtt{bool}$ are
types in the context of any well-formed \texttt{VLCtx}. These are the closed-context facts
\texttt{HasPrimitives} records, weakened to $\Gamma$, which is the form every proof below uses.
\uses{family:hasprimitives-accessors}
\thesisref{no direct counterpart; the ambient facts are Weakening and Regularity, typesys.tex thm:weak and thm:reg}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{family:trexprs-literals, thm:isdefeq-istype}
Each is the corresponding \texttt{TrExprS.natZero}, \texttt{.natSucc}, \texttt{.natLit} or
\texttt{.boolLit} fact taken at the empty level context and the empty local context and pushed to
$\Gamma$ by \texttt{VEnv.HasType.weak0}; the two \texttt{IsType} members instead take
\texttt{IsDefEq.isType} of the corresponding constructor typing. \texttt{natLitT} additionally
instantiates the universe with \texttt{instL} at the empty level list, rewriting by
\texttt{VExpr.instL\_natLit}, and \texttt{natPredT} reads its typing off the
\texttt{HasPrimitives.natPred} clause rather than off a translation.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
The \texttt{iota} branch changed \texttt{henv : env.Ordered} to \texttt{henv : env.OrderedStrong}
in all seven. None of them needs it: their bodies invoke only \texttt{HasType.weak0}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Lemmas.lean}{610}) and \texttt{IsDefEq.isType}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Lemmas.lean}{945}), both of which still take \texttt{Ordered}.
Because \texttt{OrderedStrong} is here a \emph{hypothesis} rather than something derived, these
seven statements are still free of \texttt{sorryAx}; the cost is paid by every caller that has
only \texttt{VEnv.WF} and must go through \texttt{VEnv.WF.orderedStrong}.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Recovering presence of Nat and Bool from a primitive's recorded type]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{47}
\label{vpc:thm:prim-contains-nat}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.contains_nat_of_hasType, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natOfPred, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natOfAdd, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natOfMul, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natOfDiv, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natOfMod, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.boolOfBitwise}\leanok
If $\vdash e : \mathtt{nat} \to A$ at the empty context then \texttt{Nat} is in the environment.
Hence a branch that guarded only on \texttt{Nat.pred}, \texttt{Nat.add}, \texttt{Nat.mul},
\texttt{Nat.div} or \texttt{Nat.mod} still learns that \texttt{Nat} is present, reading the
typing clause off that primitive's own reflection. Dually, \texttt{Nat.bitwise}'s recorded type
mentions \texttt{Bool}, so a guard on \texttt{Nat.bitwise} alone yields presence of \texttt{Bool},
which is how \texttt{Nat.land}, \texttt{Nat.lor} and \texttt{Nat.xor} obtain it.
\uses{family:hasprimitives-accessors}
\thesisref{Regularity (typesys.tex thm:reg) plus definitional inversion (unique.tex thm:1dinv), specialised to the Pi former}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{fam:strong-inversions, thm:isdefeq-foralle-inv, thm:isdefeq-istype, fam:ordered-entrywf}
Take \texttt{isType} of the hypothesis, invert the $\Pi$ with \texttt{IsType.forallE\_inv}, and
read the head constant off the domain with \texttt{HasType.const\_inv}.
\texttt{boolOfBitwise} first replaces the recorded type by the one the spec prescribes, using
\texttt{HasPrimitives.natBitwise} and \texttt{Ordered.constWF}, and then peels two $\Pi$s.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
All seven genuinely need \texttt{OrderedStrong}: \texttt{contains\_nat\_of\_hasType} and
\texttt{boolOfBitwise} directly, through \texttt{HasType.const\_inv}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Strong.lean}{915}), which moved to \texttt{OrderedStrong} on the
\texttt{iota} branch, and the five \texttt{natOf*} wrappers through
\texttt{contains\_nat\_of\_hasType}. The one non-signature edit in the file, \texttt{henv.constWF}
becoming \texttt{henv.ordered.constWF} at \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{80}, is
faithful.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[The fixed measure lambda evaluates at ground arguments]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{96}
\label{vpc:lem:prim-natfstlamapp}\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.natFstLamApp}\leanok
In any well-formed context $\Gamma$,
$(\lambda\, m \colon \mathtt{nat}.\ \lambda\, \_ \colon \mathtt{nat}.\ m)\ \overline{x}\ \overline{y}
 \equiv \overline{x}$. This is the measure \texttt{unfoldNatWellFounded.WF2} hands the recogniser
for \texttt{Nat.gcd} and \texttt{Nat.bitwise}.
\uses{vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing}
\thesisref{the beta rule, axioms.tex, The core language}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{vpc:lem:prim-closedn-natlit, thm:isdefeq-istype}
Two \texttt{VEnv.IsDefEq.beta} steps. The first needs the inner lambda to be well typed under the
outer binder, which is \texttt{natLitT} plus \texttt{isType}; closedness of the numerals
(\texttt{VExpr.closedN\_natLit}) is what shows that neither the lift under the discarded binder
nor the final instantiation moves them.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
\texttt{iota} strengthened the hypothesis to \texttt{OrderedStrong}; the body uses only
\texttt{natLitT} and \texttt{isType}, so \texttt{Ordered} would still suffice were
\texttt{natLitT} not itself over-strengthened.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[The source constants Nat and Bool translate to their model abbreviations]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{122}
\label{vpc:thm:prim-trnat-trbool}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.trNat, Lean4Lean.VEnv.HasPrimitives.trBool}\leanok
If the environment has the primitives and contains \texttt{Nat} (resp.\ \texttt{Bool}), then the
source expression \texttt{Nat} with no universe arguments translates to the model expression
$\mathtt{nat}$ (resp.\ \texttt{Bool} to $\mathtt{bool}$), at any level context $Us$ and any local
context $\Delta$.
\thesisref{constant typing, axioms.tex, Definitions (delta reduction)}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing, fam:strong-inversions}
From \texttt{natIsType} (resp.\ \texttt{boolIsType}) at the empty context obtain a typing of the
constant, read the constant info and the arity off it with \texttt{HasType.const\_inv}, and
rebuild with the \texttt{TrExprS.const} rule; the side condition that the constant has no
universe parameters comes out of \texttt{const\_inv}.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
These two are the genuine reason the file needs \texttt{OrderedStrong}, and every node above them
in this chapter inherits it from here.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Translation of the checked unary and binary Nat arrow types]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{149}
\label{vpc:thm:prim-natarrow}
\lean{Lean4Lean.TrExprS.natArrow1, Lean4Lean.TrExprS.natArrow2}\leanok
The source types $\forall n_1 : \mathtt{Nat}.\ \mathit{cod}$ and
$\forall n_1\,n_2 : \mathtt{Nat}.\ \mathit{cod}$, generic in the binder names, the
\texttt{mdata} annotations and the binder info, translate to
$\mathtt{nat} \to \mathit{cod}'$ and $\mathtt{nat} \to \mathtt{nat} \to \mathit{cod}'$, where
$\mathit{cod}'$ is the target of the supplied codomain bundle. Genericity in \texttt{mdata} is
what lets these serve the prelude's declarations, which are written with strict-implicit
annotations, verbatim.
\uses{family:trterm-builders}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{vpc:thm:prim-trnat-trbool, vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing}
Assemble the arrow from the \texttt{TrTy.forallE} and \texttt{TrTy.mdata} combinators over
\texttt{trNat} and \texttt{natIsType} at each binder depth, then take \texttt{.trS}. The
\texttt{OnCtx} obligation at depth one is built from the one at depth zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Shape inversions for the translations the String/Char branches check]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{160}
\label{vpc:thm:prim-trexprs-shape-inv}
\lean{Lean4Lean.TrExprS.const0_inv, Lean4Lean.TrExprS.appChar_inv, Lean4Lean.TrExprS.appChar_inv', Lean4Lean.TrExprS.listCharStringArrow_inv, Lean4Lean.TrExprS.contains_nat_of_natArrow2}\leanok
Four inversions and one containment fact. A constant with no universe arguments translates to
itself, and does so at any level and local context; an application $c_{\{0\}}\ \mathtt{Char}$
translates to $c_{\{0\}}\ \mathtt{char}$, and the primed version rebuilds that translation at any
context because both halves are closed; $\mathtt{List\ Char} \to \mathtt{String}$ translates to
$\mathtt{listChar} \to \mathtt{string}$; and a translation of a binary \texttt{Nat} arrow already
witnesses that \texttt{Nat} is in the environment.
\uses{def:vlevel-oflevel}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
Each is a \texttt{let}-pattern match on the \texttt{TrExprS} derivation followed by
\texttt{simp [VLevel.ofLevel]}, which forces the level list to be empty; \texttt{appChar\_inv'}
additionally weakens the two closed sub-typings with \texttt{HasType.weak0} before rebuilding the
application.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
Only \texttt{appChar\_inv'} was touched by \texttt{iota}, and only in its \texttt{henv}
hypothesis; its body uses \texttt{weak0}, which still takes \texttt{Ordered}, so this is another
gratuitous strengthening.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[The operator argument of Nat.bitwise is a boolean binary operator]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{210}
\label{vpc:thm:prim-bitwise-operand}\lean{Lean4Lean.TrExprS.bitwiseOperand}\leanok
If $\mathtt{Nat.bitwise}\ \mathit{op}$ translates to $V$ then

\thesisref{unique typing (unique.tex thm:utype) and Pi-injectivity (unique.tex thm:1dinv)}
\end{lemma}

\uses{thm:foralle-inv-derived, fam:typing-meta}
Match the \texttt{TrExprS.app}; replace the recorded constant type by the spec's using

\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
\texttt{IsDefEqU.forallE\_inv} lives in \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/Injectivity.lean}{23} and

unconditional.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The recurrence schemas}

Each schema is stated about an \emph{opaque} \texttt{VExpr} $f$ and mentions no constant: the
recogniser's tests are about the definition's value, and the constant only enters afterwards
through \texttt{congr\_head}. The hypothesis $\mathtt{hU} : U = 0$ lets a caller hand over facts
stated at \texttt{v.levelParams.length}, which is only propositionally zero, without rewriting
them first. All five also take \texttt{henv : env.WF}, \texttt{env.HasPrimitives} and containment
of \texttt{Nat} (or \texttt{Bool}); the well-formedness hypothesis is where
\texttt{VEnv.WF.orderedStrong}, and with it the $\iota$ obligation, enters, because the numeral
typings they use now ask for \texttt{OrderedStrong}.

\begin{theorem}[Reflection from a recurrence whose step applies a unary operation]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{273}
\label{vpc:thm:reflects-unary-step}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatNat'.of_unary_step_equations}\leanok
Let $\vdash f : \mathtt{nat} \to \mathtt{nat} \to \mathtt{nat}$ and let $g$ be a closed unary
operation with $\vdash g : \mathtt{nat} \to \mathtt{nat}$ evaluating as
$g\ \overline{k} \equiv \overline{G\,k}$ for all $k$. If the checker verified the open equations
$f\ (\mathtt{bvar}\,0)\ \mathtt{zero} \equiv \mathtt{bvar}\,0$ in $[\mathtt{nat}]$ and
$f\ (\mathtt{bvar}\,1)\ (\mathtt{succ}\ (\mathtt{bvar}\,0)) \equiv g\ (f\ (\mathtt{bvar}\,1)\ (\mathtt{bvar}\,0))$
in $[\mathtt{nat},\mathtt{nat}]$, and the intended meaning satisfies $F\,a\,0 = a$ and
$F\,a\,(b+1) = G(F\,a\,b)$, then $f$ reflects $F$. This is \texttt{Nat.add} through
\texttt{Nat.succ} and \texttt{Nat.sub} through \texttt{Nat.pred}.
\axfoot{sorryAx via VEnv.WF.orderedStrong / VEnv.WF.patsStrong, and via IsDefEq.uniq (UniqueTyping/Injectivity)}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{thm:isdefeq-instn, vpc:lem:prim-isdefequ-app-congr, vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing, fam:defeq-mixing}
Induction on $b$. The base case instantiates the zero equation at $\overline{a}$ with
\texttt{IsDefEqU.instN}; the step instantiates the successor equation twice, first at
$\overline{b}$ and then at $\overline{a}$, using closedness of $f$ (recovered by
\texttt{HasType.closedN'}) and the assumed closedness of $g$ to discard the instantiation on
them. The induction hypothesis is
then chained under $g$ by \texttt{IsDefEqU.app\_arg'} and closed with $g$'s own evaluation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Reflection from a recurrence whose step applies a binary operation]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{312}
\label{vpc:thm:reflects-binary-step}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatNat'.of_binary_step_equations}\leanok
As above, but the successor equation has the form
$f\ x\ (\mathtt{succ}\ y) \equiv g\ (f\ x\ y)\ e$ with $g$ reflecting a binary $G$, and the zero
equation returns an arbitrary open term $z$. Two syntactic side conditions say that the open
right-hand sides instantiate to numerals, $z[\overline{a}] = \overline{Z(a)}$ and
$e[\overline{b}][\overline{a}] = \overline{E(a)}$, and the meaning satisfies $F\,a\,0 = Z(a)$ and
$F\,a\,(b+1) = G\,(F\,a\,b)\,(E\,a)$. This covers \texttt{Nat.mul} through \texttt{Nat.add} with
base $0$ and \texttt{Nat.pow} through \texttt{Nat.mul} with base $1$.
\axfoot{sorryAx via VEnv.WF.orderedStrong / VEnv.WF.patsStrong, and via IsDefEq.uniq (UniqueTyping/Injectivity)}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{thm:isdefeq-instn, vpc:lem:prim-isdefequ-app-congr, vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing}
Induction on $b$ with two \texttt{instN} instantiations per step, as in the unary case; the
induction hypothesis is moved under $g$ by \texttt{app\_fun'} composed with \texttt{app\_arg'},
and the result is closed with $g$'s own reflection clause.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
The two instantiation hypotheses are equalities of \texttt{VExpr}s rather than definitional
equalities. That is a slightly awkward interface, but it is exactly what the branch proofs can
produce syntactically, so the choice is defensible.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Reflection from a recurrence that changes its first argument]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{350}
\label{vpc:thm:reflects-first-arg-step}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatNat'.of_first_arg_step_equations}\leanok
If the successor equation recurses with a modified first argument,
$f\ x\ (\mathtt{succ}\ y) \equiv f\ (g\ \overline{C}\ x)\ y$ with $g$ reflecting $G$, and
$F\,a\,0 = a$, $F\,a\,(b+1) = F\,(G\,C\,a)\,b$, then $f$ reflects $F$. This is
\texttt{Nat.shiftLeft}, where the step doubles the first argument.
\axfoot{sorryAx via VEnv.WF.orderedStrong / VEnv.WF.patsStrong, and via IsDefEq.uniq (UniqueTyping/Injectivity)}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{thm:isdefeq-instn, vpc:lem:prim-isdefequ-app-congr, vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing}
Induction on $b$ \emph{generalising the first argument}, which is what makes the changed argument
legitimate; otherwise the same two-step \texttt{instN} pattern, with $g$'s reflection supplying
the new first argument's value.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Reflection of a binary Nat predicate from its four constructor cases]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{386}
\label{vpc:thm:reflects-ctor-cases}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatBool'.of_constructor_cases}\leanok
Given the value of $f$ at $(0,0)$, $(0,\mathtt{succ}\,y)$ and $(\mathtt{succ}\,x,0)$ as boolean
literals, a diagonal recursion
$f\ (\mathtt{succ}\,x)\ (\mathtt{succ}\,y) \equiv f\ x\ y$, and the matching four equations for
$F$, the term $f$ reflects $F$. This covers \texttt{Nat.beq} and \texttt{Nat.ble}, which differ
only in the three constant answers.
\axfoot{sorryAx via VEnv.WF.orderedStrong / VEnv.WF.patsStrong, and via IsDefEq.uniq (UniqueTyping/Injectivity)}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{thm:isdefeq-instn, vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing, fam:defeq-mixing}

closed probed equation verbatim, the two mixed cases are one \texttt{instN} of the corresponding
probed equation each, and the diagonal case is two instantiations followed by the induction
hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Reflection of a boolean binary operator]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{429}

\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsBoolBoolBool'.of_left_cases, Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsBoolBoolBool'.of_closed_cases}\leanok

\axfoot{sorryAx via VEnv.WF.orderedStrong / VEnv.WF.patsStrong, and via IsDefEq.uniq (UniqueTyping/Injectivity)}

\uses{thm:isdefeq-instn, vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing}

\texttt{of\_closed\_cases} is a repackaging of its hypotheses after \texttt{subst}.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
\texttt{of\_closed\_cases} is a trivial repackaging and is arguably not worth a lemma, but it

\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Reflection of Nat.pred from its two probed equations]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{458}

\subsection{From the value to the constant}

\begin{theorem}[Reflection transfers along a definitional equation on the head]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{475}
\label{vpc:thm:reflects-congr-head}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatNat'.congr_head, Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatBool'.congr_head, Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNat'.congr_head}\leanok
If $g \equiv f$, the term $f$ reflects $F$, and $g$ has the expected arrow type, then $g$
reflects $F$. The typings of the numerals are taken as a hypothesis rather than from
\texttt{HasPrimitives}, because the caller reads them in the extended environment. This is the
only place where a \emph{constant} enters the reflection story.
\thesisref{the delta rule, axioms.tex, Definitions (delta reduction)}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{vpc:lem:prim-isdefequ-app-congr, fam:defeq-mixing}
Two applications of \texttt{IsDefEqU.app\_fun'} move the head across the equation at each of the
two arguments (one, for the unary \texttt{ReflectsNatNat'} form), then \texttt{IsDefEqU.trans}
with the value's reflection clause.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The definitional equation addDefEq records for a universe-free definition]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{514}
\label{vpc:thm:isdefequ-todefeq}\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.isDefEqU_toDefEq}\leanok
For a definition value with $\mathit{uvars} = 0$ whose recorded definitional equation is well
formed in the extension, the extension satisfies $\vdash c \equiv v$, where $c$ is the constant
and $v$ the value.
\uses{def:vconstval}
\thesisref{the delta rule, axioms.tex, Definitions (delta reduction)}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\texttt{IsDefEq.extra0} applied to the left-injection membership of the freshly added equation,
then \texttt{simp} away the empty level substitution.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Reflections are monotone in the environment]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{521}
\label{vpc:lem:reflects-mono}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatNat'.mono, Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatBool'.mono, Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNat'.mono}\leanok

in $\mathit{env}'$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{thm:isdefeq-mono}
Componentwise \texttt{HasType.mono} and \texttt{IsDefEqU.mono}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The environment after adding a definition is well formed and equates constant with value]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{536}
\label{vpc:thm:adddefeq-wf}\lean{Lean4Lean.VDefVal.addDefEq_wf}\leanok

there, and adding its constant succeeds, then adding its definitional equation on top yields a
well-formed environment that satisfies $\vdash c \equiv v$ at the empty context.
\uses{def:vdecl-wf}
\thesisref{admissibility of a definition, axioms.tex, Definitions (delta reduction)}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{vpc:thm:isdefequ-todefeq}

\texttt{isDefEqU\_toDefEq} applied to that step, with \texttt{instL\_id} handling the identity

\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Carrying a reflection of the value onto the constant in the extension]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{555}
\label{vpc:thm:reflects-toconst}
\lean{Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatNat'.toConst, Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNatBool'.toConst, Lean4Lean.VEnv.ReflectsNatNat'.toConst}\leanok

definitional equation.
\axfoot{sorryAx via VEnv.WF.orderedStrong / VEnv.WF.patsStrong, and via IsDefEq.uniq (UniqueTyping/Injectivity)}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok

\end{proof}

\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[A reflected primitive applied to two literal-valued arguments, under a closing]

\begin{lemma}[Counting arguments about MLCtx telescopes]

\section{Well-founded recursion}

This section covers \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{1}, the
verification of \texttt{unfoldNatWellFounded}. Its only two callers are \texttt{checkNatGcd}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Primitive.lean}{373}) and \texttt{checkNatBitwise}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Primitive.lean}{412}); \texttt{Nat.div} and \texttt{Nat.mod} go through the
fuel recursion of the previous section instead. The fixpoint
$\mathtt{Nat.fix}\ h\ F\ x$ is \emph{not} definitionally $F\ x\ (\lambda y\,\_.\ \mathtt{Nat.fix}\ h\ F\ y)$,
and the \texttt{Nat.eager} gadget in the fuel deliberately blocks reduction until the measure
evaluates to a numeral, so the caller cannot obtain the unfolding in one step: it has to consume
fuel. That is why the functional and the fuelled \texttt{go} are part of the interface rather
than hidden.

\begin{definition}[What the well-founded-recursion recognizer hands back]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{43}
\label{vpc:struct:probebundle}\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle}\leanok
A record extending the code-level \texttt{Probe} with the model side: translations of the
fixpoint functional $F$, the packer and the induction-hypothesis domain; the packed-argument type
$\mathit{Aty}$ read off the head-normal form of $F$'s inferred type; the fact that the packer
\emph{is} a lambda telescope over \texttt{Nat} domains whose body has type $\mathit{Aty}$; and
the two typings $\mathit{dom} : \mathit{Aty} \to \mathtt{Sort}\ u$ and
$F : (a : \mathit{Aty}) \to \mathit{dom}\ a \to \_$.
\uses{family:kernel-primitive-dsl, ind:trexprs, vpc:def:vexpr-appn-lams}
\thesisref{no counterpart; the ambient inductive machinery is axioms.tex sec:inductive}
\end{definition}
\begin{remark}
The docstrings are unusually candid about what the recogniser does \emph{not} check (nothing here
relates $\mathit{Aty}$ to the packer), and push that obligation to the caller through
\texttt{applied}. The honesty is right, but it means the bundle's interface is wider than it
looks.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[The bundle's pieces applied to a packed argument]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{80}
\label{vpc:thm:probebundle-applied}
\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.applied, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.packAty}\leanok
\texttt{applied}: given a translation of an argument and a proof that it has type $\mathit{Aty}$,
one gets translations of $\mathit{dom}\ a$ and $F\ a$, that $\mathit{dom}\ a$ is a type, and that
$F\ a$, the equation's left-hand side, is well typed, all at the probe's own context, which
extends the recogniser's by whatever variables the pattern binds. \texttt{packAty}: the packer applied to a spine of
\texttt{Nat}-typed arguments of the right length has type $\mathit{Aty}$.
\uses{vpc:struct:probebundle}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{vpc:def:vexpr-forallEs, vpc:ind:argstyped, thm:trexprs-weakfv, vpc:thm:lams-appn}
Weaken the bundle's recogniser-context typings over the probe's lift and apply
\texttt{HasType.app}; the instantiation of the induction-hypothesis domain is consumed by the
lift the domain picked up under the binder. \texttt{packAty} rewrites the lifted packer as a
telescope of \texttt{Nat}s (using that the domains are closed), builds the arguments'
\texttt{ArgsTyped} by \texttt{natTelescope}, closes the telescope with \texttt{lams\_appN'} and
transports the body's type along the resulting substitution.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The bundle's pieces under a closing substitution]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{154}
\label{vpc:def:probebundle-closed}
\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.Fc, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.packc, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.domc, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.arg, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.ihTy, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.pγ, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.parg, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.pihTy, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.plhs, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.ihCtx}\leanok
Accessors for the functional, the packer and the domain closed by a substitution $\gamma$ for the
recogniser's context; the packed argument at ground arguments; and the induction-hypothesis type
there. The \texttt{p}-prefixed family (\texttt{parg}, \texttt{pihTy}, \texttt{plhs}
and the substitution \texttt{p}$\gamma$) is the same inside a probe, where the bundle's translations still carry the
probe's own lift, together with the probe's context (\texttt{ihCtx}) and the closed form of the
equation's left-hand side.
\uses{def:subst, vpc:def:vexpr-appn-lams}
\end{definition}
\begin{remark}
The docstring justifies abbreviations over extra parameters (one bundle instead of a handful of
expressions that have to be kept in step) and notes that the recursion arguments are already
closed, being literals, so they are not closed by $\gamma$.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[One unfolding of the recursion with recursive calls discharged]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{176}
\label{vpc:def:probebundle-done}\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.Done}\leanok
$\mathsf{Done}$ says: for every recursion argument $a$ and every induction hypothesis
$\mathit{ih}$ of the right type, if $\mathit{ih}$ returns $\overline{R(y)}$ on every $y$ of
strictly smaller measure, taking the argument up to definitional equality, then
$F\ (\mathit{pack}\ \overline{\sigma(a)})\ \mathit{ih} \equiv \overline{R(a)}$. This is the
caller's real obligation and the real termination argument.
\uses{vpc:def:probebundle-closed, vpc:struct:vcontext-ext}
\end{definition}
\begin{remark}
The restriction to smaller measures is what stops the hypothesis from being vacuous, and is also
all that the fuel induction can supply: a \texttt{go} at fuel $t$ is stuck below $t$.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[What the recognizer's checks buy, at ground arguments]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{223}
\label{vpc:struct:natfixunfold}\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.NatFixUnfold}\leanok
Three fields over a family $\mathit{goFn}$: the packed argument is well typed; \texttt{entry} says
the value at ground arguments is $\mathit{go}$ at fuel $\mathit{measure}(a)+1$; and \texttt{step}
says one unit of fuel buys one call to $F$ with the recursive calls pointing at $\mathit{go}$ at
the lower fuel, uniformly in a base point $b$. $\mathit{go}$ is a family because closing the
telescope at different arguments may leave different terms, only the functional being checked
telescope-free.
\uses{vpc:def:probebundle-closed}
\end{definition}
\begin{remark}
The twenty-line docstring explaining why $\mathit{go}$ is a family, and why the two axes (the
closing of the recogniser's context and the indexing of the recursion arguments) are separate, is
the single most useful piece of documentation in the chapter.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[The fuel induction]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{247}
\label{vpc:thm:goconverges}
\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.GoConverges, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.GoConverges.succ, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.GoConverges.all, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.NatFixUnfold.reflects}\leanok
$\mathsf{GoConverges}\ t$ says that $\mathit{go}$ at fuel $t$ computes $R$ on every argument whose
measure $t$ outruns. It is preserved by adding one unit of fuel, given \texttt{NatFixUnfold} and
\texttt{Done}, hence holds at every fuel; and at the fuel the recursion is actually entered with
this gives the reflection of the value at ground arguments.
\uses{vpc:struct:natfixunfold, vpc:def:probebundle-done}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{fam:defeq-mixing}
\texttt{succ} takes \texttt{step} to produce the induction hypothesis, feeds it to \texttt{Done},
and discharges \texttt{Done}'s recursive-call premise with the outer induction hypothesis, which
is legitimate because \texttt{Done} only asks about smaller measures and the fuel is one ahead.
\texttt{all} is the structural recursion on the fuel, and \texttt{reflects} instantiates at the
base point \texttt{entry} supplies.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
The induction is on the fuel, not on the argument: the recursive call has a smaller measure but
whatever fuel the outer call had left, so an induction on the argument would not line the two up.
The proof is short and clean, in pleasant contrast to \texttt{unfoldNatWellFounded.WF'}.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Correctness of running one probe against the bundle]
\contrib{mixed}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{344}
\label{vpc:thm:probebundle-probe-wf}\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.ProbeBundle.probe.WF}\leanok
Running a probe with a substitution for the pattern's arguments, a failure continuation and a
right-hand-side builder is well formed, and its postcondition has the shape \texttt{Done} asks
about: for every extension, every closing $\gamma$ of the probe's own variables and every closed
induction hypothesis of the probe's domain type, the probed left-hand side is definitionally
equal to some value the builder produced and described.
\uses{vpc:def:probebundle-closed}
\axfoot{sorryAx via VEnv.WF.orderedStrong / VEnv.WF.patsStrong (TrExprS.appN) and via the typechecker monad's own gaps}
\end{theorem}

\texttt{TrExprS.appN} over the reconciliation \texttt{Expr.mkAppN\_eq}, then \texttt{applied}
gives the binder's type and the left-hand side's typing. Open the binder with

\end{proof}
\begin{remark}

\begin{definition}[The go step equation, exported one telescope at a time]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{445}
\label{vpc:def:unfold-step}

Three nested existential propositions recording what the recogniser's three \texttt{lambdaTelescope}

the outer \texttt{Step} is \texttt{go}'s own telescope, whose body is a recursor applied to the

\texttt{StepBranch} is the successor branch as a function of the recursion argument and its proof;
the inner \texttt{StepIH} is the induction hypothesis as the recursor at the lower fuel.

recursors. These three definitions are, however, very wide existentials:
\texttt{StepIH} and \texttt{StepBranch} bind nine variables each (three and four conjuncts

\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[The eager gadget at literals]

\begin{lemma}[Basic properties of a run of constant additions]

\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{fam:addconsts, thm:primspec-addconst}
Induction on the derivation, using \texttt{VEnv.addConst\_constants\_eq} and the
\texttt{PrimSpec.Holds} transport lemmas for \texttt{addConst} and \texttt{addDefEq}.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The constants contributed by the declarations of Bool and Nat]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive.lean}{114}
\label{vpc:def:bool-nat-consts}
\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.boolConsts, Lean4Lean.Primitive.natConsts}\leanok
The three-element lists $[(\mathtt{Bool},\mathtt{Type}),(\mathtt{false},\mathtt{bool}),(\mathtt{true},\mathtt{bool})]$
and $[(\mathtt{Nat},\mathtt{Type}),(\mathtt{Nat.zero},\mathtt{nat}),(\mathtt{Nat.succ},\mathtt{nat} \to \mathtt{nat})]$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Adding the Bool or Nat block preserves the primitive invariant]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive.lean}{121}
\label{vpc:thm:addsconsts-hasprimitives}
\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.AddsConsts.hasPrimitives_bool, Lean4Lean.Primitive.AddsConsts.hasPrimitives_nat}\leanok
If an environment satisfies \texttt{HasPrimitives} and the next one is obtained by adding exactly
\texttt{boolConsts} (resp.\ \texttt{natConsts}), then it still satisfies \texttt{HasPrimitives}.
\uses{vpc:def:bool-nat-consts, ind:primspec}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{vpc:lem:addsconsts-lemmas}
Case split on whether the specification's name is one of the three added ones. If it is not,
\texttt{holds\_of\_ne}; if it is, decide which by membership in the mapped list and
\texttt{simp [primSpecs]}, then discharge the containment or type equation with
\texttt{constants\_of\_mem}.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
The two are byte-for-byte the same proof script with the constant list swapped; one lemma
parameterised by a three-element list would remove the duplication.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[What the recognizer's inductive clause is worth]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive.lean}{162}
\label{vpc:def:primitive-inductive-result}
\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.PrimitiveInductiveResult}\leanok
The proposition that a flagged inductive declaration is one of the two primitive inductives in
standard form (safe, no level parameters, no parameters, a single type at \texttt{Type}, with
exactly the constructors the primitive table speaks about) and that adding the constants it
contributes preserves \texttt{HasPrimitives}.
\uses{vpc:def:bool-nat-consts, vpc:ind:addsconsts}
\thesisref{inductive specifications, axioms.tex sec:inductive}
\end{definition}
\begin{remark}
The docstring is candid about the missing half of the handshake: a constructive
\texttt{AddInduct} whose added constants are these, which the declaration checker's inductive
case still waits on.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Verification boundary for the recognizer's inductive clause]
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive.lean}{174}
\label{vpc:thm:checkinductive-wf}\lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.checkInductive.WF}\leanok
If \texttt{Primitive.checkInductive} returns \texttt{true} on a declaration, then
\texttt{PrimitiveInductiveResult} holds of it.
\uses{vpc:def:primitive-inductive-result}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
\uses{vpc:thm:addsconsts-hasprimitives, family:expr-beq}
Unfold and \texttt{split} repeatedly on the recogniser's guards, using \texttt{Expr.eqv\_sort} to
read the sort off the checked type, and close each surviving branch with the corresponding
preservation lemma.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
Unused: the environment checker calls \texttt{Primitive.checkInductive}, but no verification of
the declaration checker's inductive case consumes this theorem yet.
\end{remark}

\section{Contribution summary}
\label{sec:vpc-contribution}

Twenty-seven of the seventy-six nodes of this chapter carry a contribution badge, and all of them
carry it for the same reason. No statement about primitives was added, removed or changed on
either branch: the entire delta is one mechanical adaptation of a hypothesis. Counted on the
\texttt{iota} branch itself, twenty-seven declarations changed an \texttt{henv : env.Ordered}
hypothesis to \texttt{henv : env.OrderedStrong} (twenty-five \texttt{theorem} lines, one
\texttt{variable!} line covering \texttt{VExpr.WF.appN\_inv}, and one continuation line of
\texttt{TrExprS.appChar\_inv'}), forty-five call sites changed \texttt{x.ordered} to
\texttt{x.orderedStrong}, and one body line at \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Primitive.lean}{80} became
\texttt{henv.ordered.constWF} because \texttt{OrderedStrong} is a structure whose first field is
\texttt{Ordered}. That is 73 lines on the branch, of which 71 still carry \texttt{iota} blame at
\texttt{HEAD}; one of them is the single line in
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Recursion.lean}{380}. The 5 lines blamed on
\texttt{trproj} are the two hunks of \texttt{git diff iota HEAD} over these files: a three-line
re-wrap of the signature of \ref{vpc:thm:wf-app-inv2} (431--433) and a two-line re-wrap of a call
site inside the proof of \ref{vpc:thm:wf-appn-inv} (455--456), both pushed past 100 columns by the
\texttt{iota} edit. There is no projection content anywhere in this chapter.

The reason for the adaptation is upstream of this chapter. On \texttt{master} the inversion
lemmas this layer lives on, \texttt{VExpr.WF.app\_inv}, \texttt{VExpr.WF.lam\_inv} and
\texttt{HasType.const\_inv}, were provable from \texttt{Ordered} alone. On \texttt{iota} they were
restated over \ref{struct:orderedstrong}, whose third field is subject reduction for the
registered $\iota$ rules (\ref{def:patsstrongon}), and every consumer was dragged along. Within
this chapter the genuine consumers are \ref{vpc:thm:prim-contains-nat} and
\ref{vpc:thm:prim-trnat-trbool}, which go through \texttt{const\_inv}, and
\ref{vpc:thm:wf-appn-inv}, \ref{vpc:thm:wf-inv-prime}, \ref{vpc:thm:lams-ctx},
\ref{vpc:thm:lams-appn}, \ref{vpc:thm:trexprs-appn} and \ref{vpc:thm:goargs}, which go through
\texttt{app\_inv} or \texttt{lam\_inv}. Everything else inherits the hypothesis from those,
mostly through \ref{vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing} and \ref{vpc:def:prim-atoms}.

How it connects. The strengthened hypothesis is carried from the reflection schemas onto the
constant by \ref{vpc:thm:reflects-toconst}, packaged into the recogniser's obligation by
\ref{vpc:thm:data-mkresult} and \ref{vpc:thm:data-mkresultbitwise}, and surfaces at the
verification boundary \ref{vpc:thm:checkdef-wf}, which the declaration checker consumes at
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Checker.lean}{201}. Since \texttt{OrderedStrong} is obtained
from \texttt{VEnv.WF} only through \ref{thm:wf-orderedstrong}, and that rests on the admitted
\ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}, the guarantee the primitive layer offers the kernel is now conditional on
the $\iota$ contribution's one open obligation. That obligation is a \emph{new} source, not the
first: before the branch this layer already reached the \texttt{sorry}s of
\texttt{Theory/Typing/Injectivity.lean} and \texttt{Theory/Typing/UniqueTyping.lean}, through
\ref{vpc:thm:prim-bitwise-operand} and through every node that chains two definitional equalities
(\ref{vpc:lem:prim-isdefequ-app-congr}), and the \texttt{sorry} at \texttt{TrProj.weak'\_inv}
through the typechecker monad's \texttt{M.WF.withLocalDecl}. What is new is that nineteen
declarations in these four files which were \texttt{sorryAx}-free on \texttt{master} are not any
more; they are listed in Section~\ref{sec:vpc-review}.

What remains open on the contributed side is exactly \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}; nothing in this
chapter can discharge it, and nothing in this chapter is made harder or easier by it. What remains
open as a matter of hygiene is the scope of the edit: it was applied uniformly rather than to the
lemmas that need it, so the blast radius of \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong} in this layer is larger than
the proofs require, and it is what turned nineteen previously unconditional declarations into
conditional ones. Both points are recorded in Section~\ref{sec:vpc-review}.
