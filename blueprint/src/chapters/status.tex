\section{Census}
\label{status:sec:census}

\texttt{decls.tsv} tabulates every user-level declaration in the compiled environment (name,
module, kind, declaration line, axiom set, whether that axiom set contains \texttt{sorryAx},
and public/private), produced by \texttt{census/Census.lean} run once over the whole project and
once per \texttt{Experimental} module (those modules cannot be imported together; see
\texttt{census.md} \S0). Internal names (numeric components, \texttt{\_}-prefixed names,
\texttt{match\_}/\texttt{proof\_}/\texttt{eq\_} prefixes, recursor/eliminator/\texttt{sizeOf}
suffixes) are filtered, leaving 7833 of 20725 raw constants. Branch attribution is the majority
blame tag (\texttt{M}/\texttt{I}/\texttt{P}, from \texttt{blame/*.txt}) over the lines from a
declaration's range start to the next declaration's start; \texttt{mixed} means the range mixes
tags, typically a master declaration edited on a branch. 377 further rows have no
\texttt{declRangeExt} entry at all (auto-generated equation lemmas that survived the name
filter) and are omitted from the branch breakdown below, so per-directory branch counts do not
sum to the decl count (\texttt{census.md} \S1's own caption gives 377; summing decls minus the
four branch columns in the table actually gives 535 for this run --- a minor inconsistency in
the census document's caption, not in the underlying per-declaration data, which is what every
other number in this chapter is computed from).

Directory definitions follow \texttt{census.md} \S1: \texttt{kernel} is the executable kernel
and its support (\texttt{Declaration}, \texttt{Environment*}, \texttt{EquivManager},
\texttt{Expr}, \texttt{Inductive/*}, \texttt{Level}, \texttt{Primitive}, \texttt{PtrEq},
\texttt{Quot}, \texttt{Replay}, \texttt{Std/*}, \texttt{TypeChecker}, plus \texttt{Main});
\texttt{Theory}, \texttt{Verify}, \texttt{Tests} and \texttt{Experimental} are the corresponding
top-level directories.

where the sorry census below and the issue registry (\S\ref{status:sec:issues}) also
concentrate.

\section{Trust boundary}
\label{status:sec:trust}

Beyond \texttt{sorryAx} and the three standard axioms (\texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}), the project declares 110 \texttt{axiom}
constants and 12 \texttt{opaque} constants project-wide (\texttt{census.md} \S3--\S4). Grouped by
what they assert:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The core-implementation bridge, \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Axioms.lean}{1} (32
axioms).} These relate the \texttt{VEnv}/\texttt{VExpr} model to the actual, unverified
implementation of Lean's own \texttt{Expr}, \texttt{Level}, \texttt{PersistentHashMap},
\texttt{PersistentArray} and \texttt{Syntax} types (e.g.\ \texttt{Lean.Expr.instantiate\_eq},
\texttt{Lean.Level.normalize\_eq}, \texttt{Lean.PersistentHashMap.WF.find?\_eq}). They are
master's, pre-date both branches, and are by far the most heavily relied-on axioms in the
project: \texttt{PersistentHashMap.WF.find?\_eq} and \texttt{.WF.toList'\_insert} each reach 229
declarations, \texttt{PersistentArray.toList'\_push} reaches 154, and
\texttt{Level.instLawfulBEqLevel} reaches 152 (\texttt{census.md} \S5). Two upstream-linked
\texttt{Std.TreeMap} axioms (\texttt{all\_eq\_all\_toList}, \texttt{any\_eq\_any\_toList},
\texttt{Verify/Axioms.lean:10,14}) additionally gate the soundness of the level comparison
(Chapter \ref{chap:levels}).
\item \textbf{Native \texttt{bv\_decide} certificates (7 axioms,
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Expr.lean}{1}, \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Level.lean}{1}).}
Auto-generated LRAT certificate axioms, narrow reach, pre-existing master infrastructure.
\item \textbf{\texttt{PtrEq} soundness (2 axioms, \srcloc{Lean4Lean/PtrEq.lean}{1}):}
\texttt{ptrEqExpr\_eq} and \texttt{ptrEqConstantInfo\_eq}, reaching 65 and 63 declarations
respectively. Genuine, honestly documented trusted assumptions (pointer equality implies
structural equality) used in two places; one of those two, at
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/TypeChecker.lean}{739}, gates a different algorithm rather than a mere
shortcut (Chapter \ref{chap:kernel}, Review notes).
\item \textbf{\texttt{Experimental} axioms}, confined to modules outside
\texttt{defaultTargets}: \texttt{axiom Params.extra\_pat}
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Experimental/SExpr.lean}{614}, reaching 14 declarations) asserts that every
environment defeq is an instance of a registered pattern rule, the hard content of $\iota$,
outside the \texttt{Params} class it should logically be a field of; \texttt{axiom mySorry :
$\alpha$} (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Experimental/Thierry.lean}{9},
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Experimental/Thierry2.lean}{9}, reaching 25 declarations) is an inhabitant of
every type, i.e.\ a proof of \texttt{False}, making both files inconsistent by construction;
\texttt{Thierry2.lean} additionally axiomatizes the judgment it interprets
(\texttt{DefEq}/\texttt{DefEq.U}/\texttt{DefEq.left}, reaching up to 20 declarations); a further
eleven small axioms in \texttt{MoreStepIndexed.lean} and \texttt{StepIndexed.lean}
(\texttt{InferTypeN}, \texttt{NormalEqN}, \texttt{IsTy}, \ldots) posit the existence and
unfolding equations of fixpoints those files exist to construct.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What the top-level theorems assume.} The kernel's public soundness interface
\ref{thm:vtc-toplevel} (\texttt{inferType.WF'}, \texttt{checkType.WF}, \texttt{isDefEq.WF},
\ldots) depends, beyond the standard axioms and the \texttt{Verify/Axioms.lean} bridge it
inherits throughout, on \texttt{sorryAx} via the four master structure/recursor obligations of
\S\ref{status:sec:sorries} and via \texttt{patsStrong} wherever \texttt{OrderedStrong} is
discharged from \texttt{VEnv.WF}; every statement in \ref{thm:vtc-toplevel} is therefore partial
as of this HEAD (Chapter \ref{chap:typechecker}). \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq}
(\texttt{TrEnv.proj\_defeq}), the strongest trproj result connecting the projection model to a
translated environment, has the pinned axiom profile \texttt{[propext, Classical.choice,
Quot.sound, sorryAx, Lean.PersistentHashMap.WF.find?\_eq, .WF.toList'\_insert,
.findAux\_isSome]} (\texttt{Tests/ProjInhabit.lean:586--595}, a \texttt{\#guard\_msgs} pin), with
the \texttt{sorryAx} traced through unique typing, $\Pi$-injectivity (both master) and
\texttt{VEnv.WF.patsStrong} (iota). The \ref{struct:add-induct} family of theorems is currently
vacuous end-to-end: its only producer, \texttt{addDecl.WF}'s \texttt{inductDecl} case, is
master's sorry, so no theorem in that family is known true of any environment actually produced
by \texttt{Environment.addInductive}. CakeML-style ``verified modulo an axiom'' framing does not
apply to any part of this project's own trust story --- there is no single labelled ``trust
axiom''; the trust boundary is the union of the bridge axioms above and the sorry set of
\S\ref{status:sec:sorries}, and it should be quoted per top-level theorem rather than as one
number.

\section{Issue registry}
\label{status:sec:issues}

Every issue below is sourced from at least one of: the \emph{Review notes} section of one of the
fourteen module chapters (Chapters \ref{chap:syntax}--\ref{chap:experimental-reduction}),
\texttt{reviews/*.md} (the adversarial per-chapter reviews), \texttt{hygiene-review.md},
\texttt{unused-contrib.md}, or a FALSE/PARTIAL verdict in \texttt{claims-iota.md}/
\texttt{claims-trproj.md}. Entries confirmed by more than one source are merged into one entry
citing the fullest description; the parenthetical after each entry names the chapter(s) and/or
report the entry is drawn from. No fixes are proposed. Severity and attribution follow the
source material's own judgement.

\section{Blueprint limitations}
\label{status:sec:limits}

What this blueprint does not check, stated plainly so its claims are not over-read:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One build snapshot.} \texttt{decls.tsv}/\texttt{all-constants.tsv} come from a
single compiled tree (2026-09-10, HEAD \texttt{20ec229}); nothing was rebuilt for this pass
(\texttt{CONVENTIONS.md} forbids \texttt{lake build}). A later commit on \texttt{trproj} is not
reflected anywhere in this blueprint until the census is regenerated.
\item \textbf{\texttt{Experimental/} is only partially censused.} Its five mutually-exclusive
draft modules cannot be imported together, so the census ran one pass per module and merged;
cross-module interactions (e.g.\ the three duplicate \texttt{LogRel} declarations of
\S\ref{status:sec:issues}) are visible only because a reviewer read the source, not because any
one build surfaces them.
\item \textbf{Reach counts are exact only where cited to a source.} The five reach numbers in
\S\ref{status:sec:sorries} (343/138/92/61/0) come from \texttt{sorry-grep.md}'s own dependency
walk; reach for the eleven master sorries is described qualitatively (which named lemmas and
families become conditional), not as an exhaustively enumerated transitive closure, because no
committed tool computes that closure for arbitrary declarations.
\item \textbf{Blame attribution is a line-majority heuristic, not a commit-history fact.} A
declaration whose range mixes tags is \texttt{mixed}; a declaration whose file has no blame
entry is assumed byte-identical to master. This mis-attributes at least one file outright
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/TypeChecker/WHNF.lean}{1}, tagged entirely trproj by line count while
being, by commit content, iota's --- \S\ref{status:sec:issues}), and 16 lines elsewhere are
known-relocated master code counted as contributed (\S\ref{status:sec:issues}, trenv).
\item \textbf{\texttt{sorry}-detection is textual.} \texttt{sorry-grep.md} and this chapter's own
re-verification find literal \texttt{sorry} tokens by regex; the \texttt{stop} tactic
(\texttt{repeat sorry}) and axioms that assert a fixpoint's existence achieve the same effect
without the token, and are caught in this chapter only where a chapter's own review notes
happened to name them (\S\ref{status:sec:issues}, experimental-logrel/-reduction). An
exhaustive audit for every such disguised gap was not performed.
\item \textbf{No claim here was checked by building.} Every axiom profile and
\texttt{\#print axioms} figure quoted in \S\ref{status:sec:trust}--\ref{status:sec:issues} was
either read from a committed \texttt{\#guard\_msgs} pin or, per \texttt{claims-iota.md}/
\texttt{claims-trproj.md}'s own methodology, obtained via \texttt{lake env lean} on individual
files, never a full \texttt{lake build}; PARTIAL verdicts in those two files exist specifically
because the full build was out of scope.
\item \textbf{The claims audit is not exhaustively reproduced here.} \texttt{claims-iota.md} and
\texttt{claims-trproj.md} between them adjudicate 110 claims (60 iota, 50 trproj) from the
branches' own PR descriptions; \S\ref{status:sec:issues} reproduces only the handful whose
FALSE/PARTIAL verdict
corresponds to a concrete code- or documentation-level issue distinct from what the module
chapters already record, not the full claim-by-claim ledger.
\item \textbf{Severity and branch labels are inherited, not re-derived.} Where a chapter's Review
notes or a \texttt{reviews/*.md} file calls something High/Medium/Low or attributes it to a
branch, this chapter keeps that judgement; it does not independently re-score severity.
\item \textbf{This chapter is not a duplicate of Chapters \ref{chap:contrib-iota}/
\ref{chap:contrib-trproj}.} Those chapters argue the overall quality case for each branch in
narrative form; this chapter is the flat, deduplicated ledger behind both of them plus every
master-only and \texttt{Experimental} issue neither branch chapter is scoped to cover.
\end{itemize}
