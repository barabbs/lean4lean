\section*{At a glance}
\begin{itemize}

\item \lead{Status} \stSorry{} 17 live sorries outside \texttt{Experimental/} (11 master, 4 trproj, 2 iota); \stTainted{} \ref{thm:vtc-toplevel} and \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq} depend on them.
\item \lead{Contribution} iota adds sorry-free origin lemmas for well-formed environments and inductive blocks, plus an ambient-context generalization of the checker's soundness; its two open obligations are \texttt{patsStrong} (343 declarations) and the newly registered, unconsumed \texttt{const\_arity\_inv}. trproj's \texttt{TrProj}/\texttt{TrProjCtor} metatheory proves \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq} but leaves \texttt{inferProj.WF} and \texttt{inferProj.WF\_struct} open.
\item \lead{Lean files} none of its own: this chapter aggregates Chapters~\ref{chap:syntax}--\ref{chap:experimental-reduction}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Trust boundary}
\label{status:sec:trust}

Beyond the three standard axioms and \texttt{sorryAx}, the project declares 110 \texttt{axiom} constants and 12 \texttt{opaque} constants.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Standard axioms.} \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}, trusted unconditionally.
\item \textbf{Core-implementation bridge.} 32 axioms in \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Axioms.lean}{1} relate the \texttt{VEnv}/\texttt{VExpr} model to Lean's own, unverified \texttt{Expr}, \texttt{Level}, \texttt{PersistentHashMap}, \texttt{PersistentArray} and \texttt{Syntax}. Master's, predating both branches, and the most heavily relied on: \texttt{PersistentHashMap.WF.find?\_eq} and \texttt{.WF.toList'\_insert} each reach 229 declarations, \texttt{PersistentArray.toList'\_push} 154, \texttt{Level.instLawfulBEqLevel} 152. Two \texttt{Std.TreeMap} axioms (\texttt{all\_eq\_all\_toList}, \texttt{any\_eq\_any\_toList}, \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Axioms.lean}{10}) additionally gate the level-comparison soundness of Chapter~\ref{chap:levels}.
\item \textbf{Native certificates.} 7 \texttt{bv\_decide}-generated LRAT axioms (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Expr.lean}{1}, \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Level.lean}{1}), narrow reach, pre-existing.
\item \textbf{Pointer equality.} \texttt{ptrEqExpr\_eq} and \texttt{ptrEqConstantInfo\_eq} (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/PtrEq.lean}{1}), reaching 65 and 63 declarations: pointer equality implies structural equality, honestly documented. At \srcloc{Lean4Lean/TypeChecker.lean}{739} the assumption gates a different algorithm, not a mere shortcut.
\item \textbf{Experimental axioms,} confined outside \texttt{defaultTargets}: \texttt{Params.extra\_pat} (14 declarations) asserts that every environment defeq instantiates a registered pattern rule, the hard content of $\iota$; \texttt{mySorry : $\alpha$} (two files, 25 declarations) is a proof of \texttt{False}, making both files inconsistent by construction; \texttt{Thierry2.lean} further axiomatizes the judgment it interprets (\texttt{DefEq}/\texttt{DefEq.U}/\texttt{DefEq.left}, up to 20 declarations); eleven further small axioms (e.g.\ \texttt{InferTypeN}, \texttt{IsTy}) posit the fixpoints several draft files exist to construct.
\item \textbf{sorryAx.} The seventeen live sorries of \S\ref{status:sec:sorries}, plus further ones confined to \texttt{Experimental/}.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What the top-level theorems assume.} \ref{thm:vtc-toplevel} (\texttt{inferType.WF'}, \texttt{checkType.WF}, \texttt{isDefEq.WF}) depends, beyond the standard axioms and the \texttt{Verify/Axioms.lean} bridge, on \texttt{sorryAx} via the four structure/recursor obligations of \S\ref{status:sec:sorries} and via \texttt{patsStrong} wherever \texttt{OrderedStrong} is discharged from \texttt{VEnv.WF}; every statement in \ref{thm:vtc-toplevel} is partial as of this HEAD. \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq} has the pinned axiom profile \texttt{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound, sorryAx, Lean.PersistentHashMap.WF.find?\_eq, .WF.toList'\_insert, .findAux\_isSome]}, its \texttt{sorryAx} traced through unique typing, $\Pi$-injectivity and \texttt{patsStrong}. The \ref{struct:add-induct} family is vacuous end-to-end: no theorem in it is known true of any environment \texttt{Environment.addInductive} actually produces. There is no single labelled trust axiom: the trust boundary is the union of the bridge axioms above and the sorry set of \S\ref{status:sec:sorries}, quoted per top-level theorem.

\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item One build snapshot: every number above comes from a single compiled tree, HEAD \texttt{8cc17a5}, 2026-09-23; nothing here is rebuilt.
\item \texttt{Experimental/}'s five modules cannot be imported together, so its census is one pass per module, merged; cross-module clashes surface only where a source read caught them.
\item Reach counts (343/138/92/61/0 in \S\ref{status:sec:sorries}) are exact where a source computed them; reach for the master sorries is qualitative, not an exhaustive transitive closure.
\item Branch attribution is a line-majority heuristic, not a commit-history fact: a file can be mis-attributed whole, and relocated master code can be counted as contributed.
\item \texttt{sorry}-detection is textual: a regex for the literal token. The \texttt{stop} tactic and an axiom asserting a fixpoint's existence achieve the same effect without it, and are caught only where noted above.
\item No claim above was checked by a full build: axiom profiles come from committed \texttt{\#guard\_msgs} pins or single-file \texttt{lake env lean} runs.
\end{itemize}
