\chapter{Structure projections}
\label{chap:proj}

The model expression type \texttt{VExpr} (\ref{ind:vexpr}) has no projection node. The design
decision of the \texttt{trproj} branch is that a kernel projection \texttt{.proj S i e} of a value
$e : S\;usS\;ps$ is represented by an application of the recursor the kernel generates for $S$.
Writing $P_i$ for that application, the projection is $P_i\,e$, where
$P_i = S.\mathrm{rec}\;(uss\,i)\;ps\;(\lambda x{:}S\,usS\,ps.\;F_i[f_j := P_j\,x])\;(\lambda f_0\dots f_{n-1}.\,f_i)$.
Here $Fs = [F_0,\dots,F_{n-1}]$ is the constructor's field telescope instantiated at the
parameters $ps$, $F_i$ lives over $\Gamma, f_0\dots f_{i-1}$, and the earlier projection
\emph{functions} $P_j$ ($j<i$) are the same expansions. This chapter covers the file that builds
that expansion and proves its substitution metatheory, plus the four test modules that validate it
and the $\iota$-rule specification of Chapter~\ref{chap:inductive} against the real kernel.

The files are \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{1} (449 lines, entirely \texttt{trproj}: 10
definitions and 38 lemmas, no \texttt{sorry}), \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Tests/ProjShape.lean}{1} (185
lines, entirely \texttt{trproj}), \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Tests/ProjInhabit.lean}{1} (597 lines, entirely
\texttt{trproj}), \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Tests/IotaShape.lean}{1} (607 lines: 603 \texttt{iota}, 4
\texttt{trproj}) and \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Tests/ShapeDecide.lean}{1} (78 lines: 67 \texttt{iota}, 11
\texttt{trproj}). There is no master code in this group at all: every node below is contributed.
\texttt{Theory/Proj.lean} is the definitional half of the \texttt{trproj} contribution; its
consumer is \texttt{TrProjCtor} (\ref{struct:trprojctor}) in Chapter~\ref{chap:trexpr}, and its
substitution lemmas are what \texttt{TrProj.weak'}, \texttt{TrProj.instN}, \texttt{TrProj.instL}
and \texttt{TrEnv.proj\_defeq} (\ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq}) consume.

\emph{Conventions.} Contexts are listed outermost first and $\mathrm{bvar}\;0$ is the innermost
binder. Over $\Gamma, f_0\dots f_{n-1}$ the field $f_j$ is $\mathrm{bvar}(n-1-j)$. The level list
$uss\,j$ is per field, because the elimination level of $P_j$ is the sort of $F_j$: for the
standard $\Sigma$ type, $\mathrm{Sigma.fst}$ needs $\mathrm{Sigma.rec}.\{u+1,u,v\}$ and
$\mathrm{Sigma.snd}$ needs $\mathrm{Sigma.rec}.\{v+1,u,v\}$.

\emph{Thesis correspondence.} The thesis has no treatment of Lean's \texttt{Expr.proj}: it never
mentions structures, structure eta or \texttt{inferProj}. Projections appear only indirectly, and
the branch borrows both appearances. The \emph{shape} of the expansion is the thesis's
$\mathrm{inv}_x$ (typesys.tex \S3.1), which projects the argument of $\mathrm{intro}$ out of a
proof of $\mathrm{acc}\;x$ through $\mathrm{rec}_{\mathrm{acc}}$; the \emph{dependent typing}
$P_i\,e : F_i[f_j := P_j\,e]$ is the shape of the primitive $\Sigma$ rule
$\pi_2\,p:\beta[\pi_1\,p/x]$ of Wtypes.tex \S5.1; the per-field level list realises the remark of
axioms.tex \S2.6.3 that the motive universe $u$ in the motive type
$\kappa=\forall a{:}\alpha.\,P\,a\to\mathcal{U}_u$ is fresh per use of $\mathrm{rec}_P$.
Each citation is individually accurate, but
they are borrowed idioms, not inherited results: $\mathrm{inv}_x$ is introduced in the thesis to
prove conversion \emph{undecidable}, and $\pi_2$ belongs to the W-type system that \emph{replaces}
general inductives, which lean4lean does not formalise.

\emph{Assessment.} The representation is well chosen and well documented: it leaves
\texttt{IsDefEq} (\ref{def:isdefeq}) untouched, adding no rule of its own and computing through the
\texttt{iota} branch's $\iota$ rule (\ref{rule:isdefeq-pat}); it is layered so that each builder is
proved once against \texttt{Subst} and the weakening and instantiation forms are read off, and the
lemma set matches its consumers almost exactly. \texttt{Theory/Proj.lean} contains no
\texttt{sorry}, and the four inhabitation witnesses of \texttt{Tests/ProjInhabit.lean}, two per
hand-built block, are sorry-free. What is \emph{not} established is the bridge from the kernel: \texttt{inferProj.WF\_struct} (\ref{thm:vtc-inferproj-struct})
is \texttt{sorry}, so nothing yet shows that a kernel-accepted projection yields a
\texttt{TrProjCtor}; the general construction exists only as a fifteen-line informal derivation in
the module docstring, mechanised on two hand-built instances. The right reading is: the model and
its metatheory are in place and demonstrably satisfiable, and the bridge from the kernel is open.
Structure eta is the one thing the representation cannot carry, and the docstring says so.

\section{The projection expansion}

\begin{definition}[Field selector \texttt{fieldSelector}]
  \label{def:proj-field-selector}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.fieldSelector}
  \leanok
  \uses{ind:vexpr}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{70}
  \thesisref{axioms.tex \S2.6.3, the recursor: the minor premise
  $\varepsilon_c=\forall b{:}\beta.\forall v{:}\delta.\,C\,p[b]\,(c\,b)$.}
  $\mathrm{fieldSelector}\;Fs\;i$ is $Fs.\mathrm{foldr}\;\lambda\;(\mathrm{bvar}(|Fs|-1-i))$, the
  $\lambda$-telescope over the field types whose body is the $i$-th binder counted from the
  outside. It is the minor premise handed to the structure's recursor to read out field $i$.
  \reviewnote{The \texttt{Nat} subtraction $|Fs|-1-i$ is unguarded: for $i\ge|Fs|$ it truncates to
  $0$ and silently selects the innermost binder instead of being undefined. Callers are expected to
  carry \texttt{TrProjCtor.field\_lt}.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Instantiating leading Pi-binders, \texttt{instPis}]
  \label{def:proj-inst-pis}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.instPis}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:inst}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{75}
  \thesisref{no direct counterpart; it models the parameter loop of the kernel's
  \texttt{inferProj}.}
  $\mathrm{instPis} : \mathtt{VExpr}\to\mathtt{List\ VExpr}\to\mathtt{Option\ VExpr}$ peels the
  leading $\Pi$-binders of $ty$ and instantiates them with $args$, outermost first:
  $\mathrm{instPis}\;e\;[] = \mathrm{some}\;e$,
  $\mathrm{instPis}\;(\forall A.\,B)\;(a::as) = \mathrm{instPis}\;(B.\mathrm{inst}\;a)\;as$, and
  $\mathrm{none}$ on any other head with a non-empty argument list.
  \reviewnote{Manifest binders only. The kernel \texttt{whnf}s at each binder, so a constructor type
  whose telescope hides behind a definition makes this \texttt{none}; that is a documented
  completeness boundary of \texttt{TrProjCtor.ctor}, not a soundness gap.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Telescope substitution at descending indices, \texttt{instFields}]
  \label{def:proj-inst-fields}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.instFields}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:inst}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{84}
  \thesisref{no counterpart; a de Bruijn convention of the formalisation.}
  $\mathrm{instFields}\;F\;[] = F$ and
  $\mathrm{instFields}\;F\;(P::Ps) = \mathrm{instFields}\;(F.\mathrm{inst}\;P\;|Ps|)\;Ps$: the
  $m=|Ps|$ innermost binders of $F$ (the fields $f_j=\mathrm{bvar}(m-1-j)$) are replaced by the
  $P_j$, outermost first, each $P_j$ living over the outer context alone. Substituting for an
  unused binder is the identity.
  \reviewnote{This is a \emph{second} telescope-substitution convention next to the master
  \texttt{VExpr.insts}, which substitutes at index $0$ once per binder. The section docstring
  (lines 195--203) argues convincingly that neither is a special case of the other, but no lemma
  relates them, so the two conventions only ever meet through raw \texttt{inst}.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Motive body relative to given projections, \texttt{projMotiveBodyOf}]
  \label{def:proj-motive-body-of}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.projMotiveBodyOf}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-inst-fields, def:liftn}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{91}
  \thesisref{Wtypes.tex \S5.1, the primitive $\Sigma$ rules $\pi_1\,p:\alpha$ and
  $\pi_2\,p:\beta[\pi_1\,p/x]$.}
  $\mathrm{projMotiveBodyOf}\;Fs\;i\;Ps = \mathrm{instFields}\;F'\;(Ps.\mathrm{map}\;\lambda P.\;P.\mathrm{lift}\;(\mathrm{bvar}\;0))$,
  where $F' = (Fs.\mathrm{getD}\;i\;\mathrm{default}).\mathrm{liftN}\;1\;i$: the field type $F_i$, which
  lives over $\Gamma, f_0\dots f_{i-1}$, is first moved under the motive's new binder $x$ by
  $\mathrm{liftN}\;1\;i$, then each earlier field $f_j$ is replaced by $P_j\,x$.
  \reviewnote{Out-of-range $i$ falls back to $\mathrm{default}=\mathrm{sort}\;\mathrm{zero}$ through
  \texttt{List.getD}, so the definition is total but meaningless above the field count.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Projection function relative to given projections, \texttt{projFnOf}]
  \label{def:proj-fn-of}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.projFnOf}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-motive-body-of, def:proj-field-selector, def:mkapps}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{96}
  \thesisref{typesys.tex \S3.1, the projection $\mathrm{inv}_x$ of $\mathrm{acc}\,x$ through
  $\mathrm{rec}_{\mathrm{acc}}$; axioms.tex \S2.6.3, the recursor.}
  $\mathrm{projFnOf}\;S\;usS\;uss\;ps\;Fs\;i\;Ps$ is the recursor application
  $\mathrm{const}\,(S.\mathrm{rec})\,(uss\,i)$ applied to
  $ps \mathbin{+\!\!+} [\lambda x{:}(S\,usS)\,ps.\;\mathrm{projMotiveBodyOf}\;Fs\;i\;Ps,\;
   \mathrm{fieldSelector}\;Fs\;i]$. It is the expansion of field $i$ \emph{given} the earlier
  projection functions $Ps$; separating it from \ref{def:proj-fn} is what lets the substitution
  lemmas below be stated generically in $Ps$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The first \texorpdfstring{$i$}{i} projection functions, \texttt{projFns}]
  \label{def:proj-fns}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.projFns}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-fn-of}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{108}
  \thesisref{axioms.tex \S2.6.3, the recursor: $u$ is a fresh universe variable per use of
  $\mathrm{rec}_P$.}
  By recursion on $i$: $\mathrm{projFns}\dots 0 = []$ and
  $\mathrm{projFns}\dots(i+1) = \mathrm{projFns}\dots i \mathbin{+\!\!+}
   [\mathrm{projFnOf}\dots i\;(\mathrm{projFns}\dots i)]$, so that
  $\mathrm{projFns}\dots i = [P_0,\dots,P_{i-1}]$.
  \reviewnote{The defining equation mentions $\mathrm{projFns}\dots i$ twice, so the unfolded
  expansion of field $i$ is exponential in $i$ (each motive embeds every earlier projection
  function in full) and every induction over it must apply the induction hypothesis twice. An
  accumulating fold returning the list built so far would be linear.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Motive body of projection \texorpdfstring{$i$}{i}, \texttt{projMotiveBody}]
  \label{def:proj-motive-body}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.projMotiveBody}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-motive-body-of, def:proj-fns}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{114}

  (\ref{struct:trprojctor}): $P_i : \forall x{:}S\,usS\,ps,\;\mathrm{projMotiveBody}\dots i$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The projection function, \texttt{projFn}]
  \label{def:proj-fn}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.projFn}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-motive-body, def:proj-field-selector, def:mkapps}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{120}

  $\mathrm{projFn}\;S\;usS\;uss\;ps\;Fs\;i$ is
  $\mathrm{const}\,(S.\mathrm{rec})\,(uss\,i)$ applied to

  $\mathrm{projFnOf}\dots i\;(\mathrm{projFns}\dots i)$ (\ref{fam:proj-unfolding}).
\end{definition}

  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.projTy}

  (it should follow from \texttt{instFields\_subst} and \texttt{inst\_liftN}), so the definition

\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Arity of a binder of a telescope, \texttt{binderArity?}]
  \label{def:proj-binder-arity}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.binderArity?}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:pi-telescope}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{136}
  \thesisref{axioms.tex \S2.6.3, the recursor: the minor premise binds the constructor's arguments
  and its inductive hypotheses.}
  $ty.\mathrm{binderArity?}\;k = ty.\mathrm{piBinders}[k]?.\mathrm{map}\;\mathrm{piArity}$, the
  $\Pi$-arity of the $k$-th binder of $ty$'s telescope. Used as \texttt{TrProjCtor.minor\_arity}:
  $rci.\mathrm{type}.\mathrm{binderArity?}(np+1) = \mathrm{some}\;n_f$ says the minor premise of the
  structure's recursor binds exactly the fields and no inductive hypothesis, that is, the
  constructor is non-recursive.
  \reviewnote{It pins only the \emph{number} of binders, not their types: a recursor whose minor
  bound $n_f$ unrelated things would pass. In \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq} the gap is closed by the
  kernel-side structure facts, so it is contained, but the docstring's wording
  ``has exactly the \texttt{nf} fields as binders'' over-reads what is checked.}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Unfolding facts for the projection builders]
  \label{fam:proj-unfolding}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.projFn_eq, Lean4Lean.VExpr.projFns_succ, Lean4Lean.VExpr.projFns_length, Lean4Lean.VExpr.projMotiveBody_zero}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-fn, def:proj-fns, def:proj-motive-body}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{140}
  \thesisref{no counterpart; definitional bookkeeping.}
  Four facts: $\mathrm{projFn}\dots i = \mathrm{projFnOf}\dots i\;(\mathrm{projFns}\dots i)$;
  $\mathrm{projFns}\dots(i+1) = \mathrm{projFns}\dots i \mathbin{+\!\!+} [\mathrm{projFn}\dots i]$;
  $(\mathrm{projFns}\dots i).\mathrm{length} = i$; and the motive of field $0$ is the constant
  motive $(Fs.\mathrm{getD}\;0\;\mathrm{default}).\mathrm{lift}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  All four are \texttt{rfl} except \texttt{projFns\_length}, which is an induction on $i$ using
  \texttt{projFns\_succ}.
  \reviewnote{\texttt{projMotiveBody\_zero} is marked \texttt{@[simp]} but is never invoked
  explicitly anywhere, as is \texttt{instFields\_nil}; both are cheap, so this is noise rather than
  a defect.}
\end{proof}

\section{The builders under substitution}

The design note at \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{155} is explicit and, in this reviewer's
judgement, correct: \texttt{VExpr.lift'} and \texttt{VExpr.inst} are both instances of
\texttt{VExpr.subst} (\ref{def:subst}), and every builder shifts its pieces by one binder per
field, so each is proved once against \texttt{Subst} with $\sigma.\mathrm{liftN}\;j$ for the piece
standing under $j$ binders, and the weakening and instantiation forms are read off. Level
instantiation substitutes levels rather than terms, so the \texttt{instL} family keeps its own
proofs. The thirty-odd lemmas of this section have no thesis counterpart; they are the de Bruijn
bookkeeping the model needs.

\begin{lemma}[Lambda-telescopes under substitution]
  \label{fam:proj-foldr-lam}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.foldr_lam_subst, Lean4Lean.VExpr.foldr_lam_inst, Lean4Lean.VExpr.foldr_lam_instL}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:subst}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{164}
  \thesisref{no counterpart; de Bruijn bookkeeping.}
  Pushing a substitution through a $\lambda$-telescope: $(\mathrm{foldr}\;\lambda\;base\;Fs).
  \mathrm{subst}\;\sigma = \mathrm{foldr}\;\lambda\;(base.\mathrm{subst}\;(\sigma.\mathrm{liftN}\;
  |Fs|))\;(Fs.\mathrm{mapIdx}\;\lambda j\,F.\;F.\mathrm{subst}\;(\sigma.\mathrm{liftN}\;j))$, so
  the base is substituted under $|Fs|$ extra binders and binder $j$ under $j$ of them. The
  instantiation form is the same with $\mathrm{inst}\;e_0\;(k+j)$, and the level-instantiation form
  maps $\mathrm{instL}$ over the telescope.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{fam:subst-liftn-algebra, fam:subst-basic}
  \leanok
  \texttt{foldr\_lam\_subst} by structural recursion on $Fs$, using
  \texttt{Subst.lift\_liftN}; \texttt{foldr\_lam\_inst} is then \texttt{instN\_eq} plus
  \texttt{Subst.liftN\_liftN}; \texttt{foldr\_lam\_instL} is a separate induction.
  \texttt{foldr\_lam\_inst} is what \texttt{VEnv.IsDefEqU.betaN}
  (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Lemmas.lean}{994}) consumes, the $\beta$ phase of
  \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The field selector under substitution]
  \label{fam:proj-fieldselector-subst}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.fieldSelector_subst, Lean4Lean.VExpr.fieldSelector_instL}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-field-selector, def:subst}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{185}
  \thesisref{no counterpart; de Bruijn bookkeeping.}
  For $i<|Fs|$, $(\mathrm{fieldSelector}\;Fs\;i).\mathrm{subst}\;\sigma =
  \mathrm{fieldSelector}\;(Fs.\mathrm{mapIdx}\;\lambda j\,F.\;F.\mathrm{subst}\;
  (\sigma.\mathrm{liftN}\;j))\;i$; the level-instantiation form holds with no side condition.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{fam:proj-foldr-lam, fam:subst-liftn-algebra}
  \leanok
  Rewrite with \ref{fam:proj-foldr-lam} and \texttt{subst\_bvar}, then apply
  \texttt{Subst.Fixes.liftN} to the selected variable $|Fs|-1-i$, which is where $i<|Fs|$ is used.
  The \texttt{instL} form is \texttt{foldr\_lam\_instL} followed by \texttt{simp}.
  \reviewnote{Used only inside this file, by \ref{fam:proj-fnof-subst}.}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{instFields} under substitution and level instantiation]
  \label{fam:proj-instfields-subst}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.instFields_subst, Lean4Lean.VExpr.instFields_instL}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-inst-fields, def:subst}

  \thesisref{no counterpart; de Bruijn bookkeeping.}
  $(\mathrm{instFields}\;F\;Ps).\mathrm{subst}\;\sigma =
  \mathrm{instFields}\;(F.\mathrm{subst}\;(\sigma.\mathrm{liftN}\;|Ps|))\;
  (Ps.\mathrm{map}\;(\cdot.\mathrm{subst}\;\sigma))$, and the same with $\mathrm{instL}\;ls$ in

\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{instPis} commutes with substitution]
  \label{fam:proj-instpis-subst}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.instPis_subst, Lean4Lean.VExpr.instPis_lift', Lean4Lean.VExpr.instPis_inst, Lean4Lean.VExpr.instPis_instL}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-inst-pis, def:subst}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{226}
  \thesisref{no counterpart; de Bruijn bookkeeping.}
  If $T.\mathrm{instPis}\;ps = \mathrm{some}\;cty$ then $(T.\mathrm{subst}\;\sigma).\mathrm{instPis}
  \;(ps.\mathrm{map}\;(\cdot.\mathrm{subst}\;\sigma)) = \mathrm{some}\;(cty.\mathrm{subst}\;\sigma)$,
  and likewise for $\mathrm{lift'}\;\rho$, $\mathrm{inst}\;e_0\;k$ and $\mathrm{instL}\;ls$. Note
  that \emph{success} is preserved, not merely the value.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{fam:subst-basic, thm:lift-eq-subst}
  \leanok
  Recursion on $ps$ with \texttt{subst\_inst} at each $\Pi$-binder and \texttt{nomatch} on the five
  non-$\Pi$ heads; the \texttt{lift'} and \texttt{inst} forms are \texttt{simpa} corollaries through
  \texttt{lift'\_eq\_subst} and \texttt{instN\_eq}. These are what make the field telescope of
  \texttt{TrProjCtor.ctor} stable under \texttt{TrProj.weak'}, \texttt{TrProj.instN} and
  \texttt{TrProj.instL}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{instPis} preserves \texttt{CtorHeaded}]
  \label{thm:proj-instpis-ctorheaded}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.instPis_ctorHeaded}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-inst-pis, def:ctorheaded}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{254}
  \thesisref{no counterpart; a stability property of the formalisation's shape predicates.}
  If $T$ is \texttt{CtorHeaded} (its $\Pi$-telescope ends in a constant application, as every
  constructor type does) and $T.\mathrm{instPis}\;ps=\mathrm{some}\;cty$, then $cty$ is
  \texttt{CtorHeaded}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}

  \leanok

  non-$\Pi$ heads cannot occur because \texttt{instPis} would have returned \texttt{none}.
\end{proof}

\section{Field selection on variable spines}

The last group is what \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq} needs to compute: the $\iota$ reduct of a
structure's rule is the template $\lambda\,\mathit{params}\,\mathit{motive}\,\mathit{minor}\,
\mathit{fields}.\;\mathit{minor}\;\mathit{fields}$ applied to the redex's arguments, and
saturating a $\lambda$-telescope by its arguments substitutes them outermost first, that is, by
$\mathrm{instFields}$.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{instFields} on applications and on lifted terms]
  \label{fam:proj-instfields-spine}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.instFields_app, Lean4Lean.VExpr.instFields_mkApps, Lean4Lean.VExpr.instFields_liftN}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-inst-fields, def:mkapps, def:liftn}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{394}
  \thesisref{no counterpart; de Bruijn bookkeeping.}
  $\mathrm{instFields}$ distributes over application and over $\mathrm{mkApps}$, and cancels a
  lifting by the telescope's length: $\mathrm{instFields}\;(e.\mathrm{liftN}\;|Ps|)\;Ps = e$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{fam:proj-instfields-simp, def:inst}
  \leanok
  Structural recursion on $Ps$, except for \texttt{instFields\_mkApps}, which recurses on the
  argument list; the last additionally uses
  \texttt{liftN'\_liftN\_lo} and \texttt{inst\_liftN} to cancel one lift per step.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{instFields} on a bound variable]
  \label{thm:proj-instfields-bvar}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.instFields_bvar}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-inst-fields}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{413}
  \thesisref{no counterpart; de Bruijn bookkeeping.}
  For $j<|Ps|$, $\mathrm{instFields}\;(\mathrm{bvar}\;j)\;Ps =
  Ps.\mathrm{getD}\;(|Ps|-1-j)\;\mathrm{default}$: the variable $j$, counted innermost first,
  becomes the correspondingly indexed argument.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{fam:proj-instfields-spine}
  \leanok
  Recursion on $Ps$, splitting \texttt{instVar} into its three cases; the hit case is closed by
  \texttt{instFields\_liftN} and the index arithmetic by \texttt{omega}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The iota reduct on the minor and field spine]
  \label{thm:proj-instfields-minor-spine}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.VExpr.instFields_minor_spine}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:proj-inst-fields, def:mkapps, def:bvarsdesc}
  \contrib{trproj}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean}{428}
  \thesisref{axioms.tex \S2.6.4, the computation rule (iota reduction):
  $\mathrm{rec}_P\,C\,e\,p[b]\,(c\,b)\equiv e_c\,b\,v$.}
  $\mathrm{instFields}\;((\mathrm{bvar}\;|fs|).\mathrm{mkApps}\;(\mathrm{bvarsDesc}\;0\;|fs|))\;
  (pre \mathbin{+\!\!+} s :: fs) = s.\mathrm{mkApps}\;fs$: saturating the template
  $\lambda\,\mathit{params}\,\mathit{motive}\,\mathit{minor}\,\mathit{fields}.\;
  \mathit{minor}\;\mathit{fields}$ on the arguments $pre \mathbin{+\!\!+} s :: fs$ selects the
  minor $s$ and applies it to the fields.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{fam:proj-instfields-spine, thm:proj-instfields-bvar}
  \leanok
  Rewrite by \texttt{instFields\_mkApps}, then by \ref{thm:proj-instfields-bvar} twice: once on the
  head $\mathrm{bvar}\;|fs|$, which lands on $s$ because
  $|pre \mathbin{+\!\!+} s::fs|-1-|fs| = |pre|$, and once under
  \texttt{List.ext\_getElem} on each argument, using \texttt{getElem\_bvarsDesc} and \texttt{omega}
  for the indices. This is the arithmetic core of the $\beta$ phase of
  \ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq}, and it is stated in exactly the shape that proof needs.
\end{proof}

\begin{example}[Executable pattern matcher, \texttt{matchPat}]
  \label{def:iotashape-matchpat}

  \contrib{iota}\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Tests/IotaShape.lean}{103}
  \thesisref{axioms.tex \S2.6.4, the computation rule (iota reduction).}
  $\mathrm{matchPat}\;p\;e$ returns, as an $\mathrm{Option}$, a level assignment and a
  path-indexed argument assignment $p.\mathrm{Path}\to\mathtt{VExpr}$, by structural recursion on
  the pattern: $\mathrm{const}\;c$ matches $\mathrm{const}\;c'\;ls$ when $c=c'$, returning $ls$
  and the empty assignment; $\mathrm{var}\;f$ matches an application, recording its argument at
  the new path and recursing into the function; $\mathrm{app}\;f\;a$ recurses into both halves and

\section{Negative controls and the test battery}

Two declarations Lean's elaborator refuses to accept are written directly as \texttt{VExpr} and
\texttt{VInductDecl} literals, so that the clauses meant to exclude them can be run on something.
They are the sharpest part of the file: they test not that the specification accepts what the
kernel accepts, but that it rejects what the kernel would reject.

\section{The model side: a structure with a dependent motive}

The \texttt{Dependent} namespace is the interesting half. To type the second selector at the minor
type $\forall a\,b,\;M_{21}\,(\mathrm{mk}\;a\;b)$ one must show
$B\,a \equiv B\,(P_0\,(\mathrm{mk}\;a\;b))$, that is, that the \emph{first} projection computes on
the generic constructor spine. That is where the registered $\iota$ rule is actually fired.

\section{Contribution summary}

Two contributions meet in this group, and the whole of it is contributed. The \texttt{trproj}
branch adds \texttt{Lean4Lean/Theory/Proj.lean} in full. Its content is one design decision and
its consequences: a kernel projection is not a new node of \texttt{VExpr} (\ref{ind:vexpr}) but an
application of the recursor the kernel already generates for the structure. That buys a great
deal. \texttt{IsDefEq} (\ref{def:isdefeq}) gains no rule, so no metatheory has to be redone, and a
projection computes because the structure's $\iota$ rule computes, not because a projection rule
was added. The expansion is spelled out by \ref{def:proj-field-selector} through
\ref{def:proj-fn}, its type by \ref{def:proj-motive-body} and \ref{def:proj-ty}, and the
de Bruijn metatheory those builders need by \ref{fam:proj-foldr-lam} through
\ref{fam:proj-motivebody-subst}, all stated once against \texttt{Subst} (\ref{def:subst}) and
specialised to weakening and instantiation afterwards.

It connects downstream at three points. \texttt{TrProjCtor} (\ref{struct:trprojctor}) in
Chapter~\ref{chap:trexpr} is the consumer: its \texttt{eq} clause is \ref{def:proj-fn}, its
\texttt{fn\_ty} clause is \ref{def:proj-motive-body}, its \texttt{ctor} clause is
\ref{def:proj-inst-pis} and its \texttt{minor\_arity} clause is \ref{def:proj-binder-arity}. The
four forms of \ref{fam:proj-fn-subst} are exactly what \texttt{TrProj.weak'},
\texttt{TrProj.instN} and \texttt{TrProj.instL} consume. And
\ref{thm:proj-instfields-minor-spine}, with \ref{thm:proj-instfields-bvar} beneath it, is the
arithmetic \texttt{TrEnv.proj\_defeq} (\ref{thm:tr-env-proj-defeq}) needs to compute the $\iota$
reduct of a structure's rule. The same branch adds the three modules that make the design
falsifiable: \ref{def:projshape-checkproj} confronts the expansion with the real Lean kernel on
eleven structure-and-field pairs, and \ref{thm:projinhabit-plain-inhab0},
\ref{thm:projinhabit-plain-inhab1} and \ref{thm:projinhabit-dep-inhab} inhabit
\texttt{TrProjCtor}, the last of these resting on \ref{thm:projinhabit-dep-hp1ty}, which
mechanises on an instance the claim that the dependent motive is typable using only $\beta$ and a
registered $\iota$ rule.

The \texttt{iota} branch contributes the two specification tests.
\ref{fam:shapedecide-deceq} through \ref{fam:shapedecide-shapes} make every syntactic predicate of
Chapter~\ref{chap:inductive} executable without moving it out of \texttt{Prop} in the library, and
\ref{def:iotashape-ruleshapeat} through \ref{test:iotashape-run} decide every \emph{syntactic}
clause of \ref{structure:ind-wf} on the kernel's own data, compare the model's $\iota$ reduct with the
executable kernel's on twelve rules (\ref{def:iotashape-checkiota}, sound by
\ref{thm:iotashape-matchpat-sound}), and supply the negative controls
(\ref{thm:iotashape-positivity}, \ref{thm:iotashape-twoctorprop}) that make the specification look
tight rather than merely satisfiable. The \texttt{trproj} contribution to those two files is
fifteen lines: eleven in \texttt{Tests/ShapeDecide.lean} (the head-constructor tests of
\ref{fam:shapedecide-head}, which \texttt{TrProjCtor.ctor} needs, and the \texttt{some} branch of
the option instance), and four in \texttt{Tests/IotaShape.lean} (the constant-application guard
inside \texttt{checkIotaAuto} and one minor-premise control in \ref{test:iotashape-run}).

What remains open is the bridge from the kernel. \texttt{inferProj.WF\_struct}
(\ref{thm:vtc-inferproj-struct}) is \texttt{sorry}, so nothing yet shows that a kernel-accepted
projection yields a \texttt{TrProjCtor}; the general construction exists as a fifteen-line
informal derivation in the module docstring of \texttt{Theory/Proj.lean} and is mechanised only on
the two hand-built instances of \ref{def:projinhabit-plain-block} and
\ref{def:projinhabit-dep-block}, whose environments are never shown well formed. \ref{def:proj-ty}
is not connected to \ref{def:proj-motive-body} by any lemma. The witnesses cover neither
parameters nor a non-trivial per-field level list, so \ref{fam:proj-fn-subst} is never exercised
on a non-trivial instance. Structure eta is outside what this representation can express, and the
docstring says so. On the \texttt{iota} side, nothing derives \ref{structure:ind-wf} from the
repository's own inductive checker, so \ref{test:iotashape-run} remains evidence about the
specification rather than a proof about it.
