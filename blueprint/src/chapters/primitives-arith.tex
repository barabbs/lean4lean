\chapter{Primitive operations: arithmetic}
\label{chap:primitives-arith}

Lean's kernel does not evaluate \texttt{Nat.add} by unfolding its definition: it recognises
certain declarations by name and swaps in GMP arithmetic. That recognition is a trusted step of
the real kernel, and \texttt{Lean4Lean/Primitive.lean} re-implements it as a \emph{checker} --
for each recognised name, a branch that opens probe variables, runs \texttt{isDefEq} checks
against the equations the primitive is supposed to satisfy, and accepts only if they all pass.
The five files of this chapter are the soundness proof of that checker: a theorem per branch
over a shared vocabulary,
each saying that if the branch accepts then the declaration really reflects the arithmetic
function the kernel will afterwards compute with, i.e.\ that it satisfies the entry recorded for
it in \texttt{primSpecs} (\ref{ind:primspec}, \ref{vpc:struct:primitiveresult}).

The files are \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{1} (3117 lines), the
shared vocabulary; \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Clauses.lean}{1} (438 lines),
fifteen short theorems covering fourteen simple clauses;
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/DivMod.lean}{1}
(547 lines), which factors \texttt{Nat.div} and \texttt{Nat.mod} through the fuel recursion they
share; \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Gcd.lean}{1} (148 lines), a single proof for
a well-founded recursion; and \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Bitwise.lean}{1}
(405 lines), the hard case, where the operator is a free variable of the recognizer's context.
The vocabulary the recognizers are written in (\texttt{Condition}, \texttt{Reflection},
\texttt{checkNatFuelRec}, \texttt{unfoldNatWellFounded}) lives in
\ref{family:kernel-primitive-dsl}, and the surrounding infrastructure --
\texttt{Data}, \texttt{PrimitiveResult}, the probe combinators, the \texttt{HasPrimitives}
accessors -- in chapter~\ref{chap:primitives-core}.

Notation. $c \vdash e \leadsto e'$ abbreviates \texttt{TrExprS c.venv c.lparams c.vlctx e e'}
(\ref{ind:trexprs}), the reading of a source \texttt{Expr} as a model \texttt{VExpr};
$\Gamma \vdash e : A$ is \texttt{VEnv.HasType}; $\equiv$ is \texttt{VEnv.IsDefEqU}, definitional
equality up to the type. $E$ ranges over environment extensions of the checker context $c$, so a
statement quantified over $E$ is one that survives further declarations being added.

Relation to the thesis is thin, and that is expected: \texttt{lean-type-theory/axioms.tex} covers
$\beta$, $\zeta$, $\delta$, $\iota$, quotients, \texttt{propext} and choice, and has no section on
the kernel's GMP-accelerated \texttt{Nat}/\texttt{String} primitives at all. The correspondences
that do exist are structural: the transport lemmas instantiate Weakening
(\texttt{typesys.tex}, \texttt{thm:weak}) and Properties of substitution
(\texttt{typesys.tex}, \texttt{thm:subst}) for the \texttt{Expr}-to-\texttt{VExpr} translation
relation, and \ref{prim-cond:thm:isdefequ-natproj} and \ref{prim-cond:thm:condition-gadget} are
applications of the $\beta$ rule.

The contributed material is small and mechanical. Of 4655 lines, 67 are attributed to
\texttt{iota} (27 in \texttt{Condition.lean}, 14 in \texttt{Clauses.lean}, 13 in
\texttt{DivMod.lean}, 7 in \texttt{Bitwise.lean}, 6 in \texttt{Gcd.lean}) and 2 to
\texttt{trproj} (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Bitwise.lean}{203}, a re-wrap of
one over-long line). All 67 re-thread the same hypothesis between \texttt{VEnv.Ordered} and
\texttt{VEnv.OrderedStrong} (\ref{struct:orderedstrong}), but not all in the same direction: 61
replace \texttt{.ordered} by \texttt{.orderedStrong}, and 6 -- every one of them in
\texttt{Bitwise.lean} -- insert \texttt{.ordered} at a use of the single local abbreviation that
was raised. Six theorem \emph{statements} changed
(\ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-boolprop}, \ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-propboolprop},
\ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-boolnat3}, \ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-natnatprop},
\ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-divgotype}, \ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-natproj}); of the remaining 61
proof lines, 55 write \texttt{c.Ewf.orderedStrong} or \texttt{E.wf.orderedStrong} where master
wrote \texttt{.ordered}, and 6 write \texttt{hE.ordered} where master wrote \texttt{hE}.

Assessment. The reasoning behind the change is sound and belongs to another chapter: once
$\iota$ rules have a real specification, \texttt{Ordered.strong} is no longer provable, and
the honest replacement names the missing obligation as \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}. What this chapter
records is the cost. No \texttt{sorry} occurs in any of these five files, and on \texttt{master}
they needed only \texttt{VEnv.Ordered}; after the merge every arithmetic-primitive result here
depends on \texttt{sorryAx} through \ref{thm:wf-orderedstrong}. The \texttt{CoeOut} instance at
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/EnvLemmas.lean}{343} hides most of the coercions, so nothing at
the call sites records it: not one of the 67 touched lines says so, and three of the five files
carry no module docstring at all. Nor is the effect confined to the touched lines -- master-side
proofs whose text neither branch changed, \ref{prim-cond:thm:reflection-gentele} among them, are
\texttt{sorry}-dependent now and were not on \texttt{master}, because the target of that
coercion moved under them. The master code these edits sit in is of an unusually high
standard -- nearly every design decision is documented with its reason, and several of the
docstrings argue a point a reviewer would otherwise have to reconstruct -- which makes the silence
about the new obligation the more conspicuous.

\section{Conditions and their model-side reading}

Several primitives (\texttt{Nat.div}, \texttt{Nat.mod}, \texttt{Nat.gcd}, \texttt{Nat.bitwise})
are defined with conditionals, so the checker must be able to conclude that some \texttt{ite} or
\texttt{dite} in a definition really decides a particular predicate. A \texttt{Condition} packages
a decidable predicate with a decision procedure and the evidence relating them; the definitions
below are what a successful \texttt{Condition.check} hands back.

\begin{definition}[Model-side shapes of a conditional]
  \label{prim-cond:def:ite-dite-app}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.iteApp, Lean4Lean.Primitive.diteApp}
  \leanok
  \uses{ind:vexpr}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{40}
  $\mathtt{iteApp}\ \alpha\ p\ \mathit{inst}\ t\ e$ is the \texttt{VExpr} spelling of
  $@\mathtt{ite}.\{1\}\ \alpha\ p\ \mathit{inst}\ t\ e$, and
  $\mathtt{diteApp}\ p\ \mathit{inst}\ t\ e$ that of
  $@\mathtt{dite}.\{1\}\ \mathtt{Nat}\ p\ \mathit{inst}\ t\ e$, whose two branches are functions
  of the decision's proof. Everything the conditional layer states is stated about these two
  shapes.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[A reflection together with its translation, \texttt{Reflection.WF}]
  \label{prim-cond:struct:reflection-wf}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.Reflection.WF}
  \leanok
  \uses{ind:trexprs, family:kernel-primitive-dsl}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{51}
  For a source-level \texttt{Reflection} $r$ and a checker context $c$, \texttt{Reflection.WF c r}
  carries a \texttt{VExpr} $\mathtt{type}'$, a translation $c \vdash r.\mathtt{type} \leadsto
  \mathtt{type}'$, and the typing $\mathtt{type}' : \mathtt{Prop} \to \mathtt{Bool} \to
  \mathtt{Prop}$ established by \texttt{Reflection.check}. The translation is carried as a field
  rather than existentially bound because \texttt{WF\_ite} and \texttt{WF\_dite} are equations
  about it.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[What is known about \texttt{toDec}'s type]
  \label{prim-cond:def:reflection-todect}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.Reflection.WF.ToDecT, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Reflection.WF.ToDecT.spine}
  \leanok
  \uses{prim-cond:struct:reflection-wf, fam:strong-inversions}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{68}
  \texttt{ToDecT toDec'} says that $\mathtt{toDec}'$ applied to a proposition $p$, a boolean $bb$
  and evidence $H : \mathtt{type}'\ p\ bb$ is well formed, in every extension $E$ and every
  well-formed context $\Gamma$. \texttt{ToDecT.spine} peels that application into the three pi
  types of the spine, each paired with the typing of the argument it was applied to; it is three
  uses of \texttt{VExpr.WF.app\_inv} and makes no attempt to identify the domains with
  $\mathtt{Prop}$, $\mathtt{Bool}$, $\mathtt{type}\ p\ bb$, since a consumer converts against
  whatever domain it finds. The docstring records why this is not a field of
  \texttt{Reflection.WF}: only the two optional checks establish it.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The two optional contents of a reflection check]
  \label{prim-cond:def:reflection-wf-ite-dite}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.Reflection.WF.WF_ite, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Reflection.WF.WF_dite}
  \leanok
  \uses{prim-cond:struct:reflection-wf, prim-cond:def:reflection-todect}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{106}
  \texttt{WF\_ite} says that at evidence $H : \mathtt{type}'\ p\ (\mathtt{boolLit}\ b)$ for a
  boolean literal $b$, the \texttt{ite} the reflection builds on $\mathtt{toDec}'\ p\ b\ H$ is
  definitionally the branch $b$ selects, for every proposition, type $\alpha$ and pair of
  branches, in every extension and context. \texttt{WF\_dite} is the same for the \texttt{dite}
  gadget at \texttt{Nat}, whose branches are applied to the proof that \texttt{ofTrue}/%
  \texttt{ofFalse} extract from the evidence; those two are existentially bound, since only this
  check types them. Both are quantified over the context, unlike the fields of
  \ref{prim-cond:struct:reflection-wf}, because they speak about the caller's terms.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Model-side reading of a checked condition, \texttt{Condition.WF}]
  \label{prim-cond:struct:condition-wf}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ConditionImpl.domain, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ConditionImpl.WF, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ReflectNatNat.WF}
  \leanok
  \uses{family:kernel-primitive-dsl, prim-cond:struct:reflection-wf, ind:trexprs}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{150}
  \texttt{Condition.WF c cnd} carries the translations $\mathtt{prop}'$ and $\mathtt{dec}'$ of the
  condition's predicate and decision procedure; the \texttt{impl}-dependent datum
  \texttt{ConditionImpl.WF} (\texttt{Unit} for \texttt{.bool}, a \texttt{ReflectNatNat.WF} for a
  \texttt{reflectNatNat}, which adds the reflection's own \texttt{WF} plus translations of
  \texttt{asBool} and \texttt{proof}); the field \texttt{propT}, saying $\mathtt{prop}'$ applied to
  arguments of the types listed by \texttt{ConditionImpl.domain} is a proposition; and
  \texttt{vnil : c.vlctx = []}, recording that the check ran before any binder. \texttt{propT} is
  stated applied rather than as an arity because applied is what the checks give.
  \reviewnote{\texttt{ConditionImpl.WF} and \texttt{ReflectNatNat.WF} are the only two
  declarations in this block with no docstring. The structure lives in \texttt{Type} and is
  consumed under an existential in \ref{prim-cond:thm:condition-check-wf}, so no consumer can
  project its data out except through a \texttt{Prop}-valued continuation; that is deliberate and
  consistent, but it is nowhere said.}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[The two translations at the empty context, and their closedness]
  \label{prim-cond:family:condition-wf-projections}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.hprop0, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.hdec0, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.propC, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.decC}
  \leanok
  \uses{prim-cond:struct:condition-wf}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{173}
  Four one-line consequences of \texttt{vnil}: the predicate and the decision procedure translate
  at the empty local context, and their translations $\mathtt{prop}'$, $\mathtt{dec}'$ are closed
  \texttt{VExpr}s.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{thm:isdefeq-closedn}
  \leanok
  Rewrite the context by \texttt{vnil}; closedness is then \texttt{TrExprS.closedN\_nil} at the
  ordered environment.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Building a checked conditional: the positive specifications]
  \label{prim-cond:def:condition-wf-ite-dite}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.ConditionImpl.WF.apply, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.WF_ite, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.WF_dite}
  \leanok
  \uses{prim-cond:struct:condition-wf, prim-cond:def:ite-dite-app}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{189}
  \texttt{ConditionImpl.WF.apply} fixes when the decision is known to evaluate: the scrutinee
  itself for \texttt{.bool}, $\mathtt{asBool}'$ at the arguments for a \texttt{reflectNatNat}.
  \texttt{WF\_ite} then says that \texttt{Condition.ite} applied to translated, well-typed pieces
  translates to exactly $\mathtt{iteApp}\ \alpha'\ (\mathtt{prop}'\ \mathit{args}')\
  (\mathtt{dec}'\ \mathit{args}')\ t'\ e'$, and \texttt{WF\_dite} likewise for \texttt{dite},
  whose branches are read one binder deep: the \emph{then} branch under the predicate at the
  arguments, the \emph{else} branch under its negation. Both are quantified over an arbitrary
  well-formed \texttt{MLCtx}, because callers apply them to pieces that live past binders the
  caller has opened. The \texttt{natOnly} flag on \texttt{WF\_ite} is argued at length in the
  docstring: nothing in the model pins \texttt{Nat}'s universe, so a \texttt{.bool} condition
  cannot produce $\mathtt{Nat} : \mathtt{Type}$ and is restricted to the \texttt{Nat} conditional
  it read off its own \texttt{checkType} call. That is a real modelling gap, declared rather than
  papered over.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Evaluating a checked conditional]
  \label{prim-cond:def:condition-wf-eval}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.DiteEval, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.IteEval, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.DecT}
  \leanok
  \uses{prim-cond:def:condition-wf-ite-dite, prim-cond:def:ite-dite-app}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{260}
  \texttt{IteEval} says that whenever the decision at the arguments is worth the boolean literal
  $b$, the term $\mathtt{iteApp}\ \alpha'\ (\mathtt{prop}'\ \mathit{args}')\ (\mathtt{dec}'\
  \mathit{args}')\ t'\ e'$ is definitionally $t'$ or $e'$ accordingly; \texttt{DiteEval} is the
  same for \texttt{diteApp}, producing the proof its branch is applied to; \texttt{DecT} says
  $\mathtt{dec}'$ is well formed at arguments of the \texttt{impl}'s domain types. All three are
  stated purely about \texttt{VExpr}s. The split from \ref{prim-cond:def:condition-wf-ite-dite} is
  the central design choice of the file and is documented with its reason: a caller that
  \emph{builds} a conditional still has an \texttt{Expr}, while a caller that reached one by
  closing off probe variables does not.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Projection-free expressions, \texttt{noProj}]
  \label{prim-cond:def:noproj}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.noProj, Lean4Lean.Primitive.noProj.isUnique}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:trexprs-isunique}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{307}
  \texttt{noProj e} is the decidable predicate that $e$ contains no \texttt{Expr.proj} node;
  \texttt{noProj.isUnique} derives \texttt{TrExprS.IsUnique e} from it by structural induction,
  the \texttt{proj} case being vacuous. Uniqueness of the reading is what every identification of
  a closed piece below rests on.
  \reviewnote{\texttt{noProj} exists precisely to sidestep transporting a \texttt{TrProj}
  obligation (the docstring of \ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-weakr} says so). The primitives layer
  therefore excludes projections by construction rather than supporting them, which is the one
  direct point of contact between this chapter and the \texttt{trproj} work in
  chapter~\ref{chap:trexpr}; nothing in either place records the connection.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Side conditions every piece of a condition satisfies]
  \label{prim-cond:def:condok}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.CondOK, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ConditionImpl.OK, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.OK, Lean4Lean.Primitive.ConditionImpl.OK.reflect, Lean4Lean.Primitive.CondOK.fvarsIn, Lean4Lean.Primitive.CondOK.closed, Lean4Lean.Primitive.CondOK.looseBVarRange, Lean4Lean.Primitive.CondOK.noProj, Lean4Lean.Primitive.CondOK.isUnique}
  \leanok
  \uses{prim-cond:def:noproj}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{327}
  \texttt{CondOK e} is the decidable conjunction: no free variables, no level or expression
  metavariables, no loose bound variables, and \texttt{noProj}. \texttt{ConditionImpl.OK} and the
  class \texttt{Condition.OK} lift it to a whole condition, and five projections extract the
  individual facts, each by unfolding the boolean conjunction. The three conditions the checker
  ships (\texttt{bool}, \texttt{natLE}, \texttt{natEq}) discharge the class by \texttt{rfl} in
  three anonymous instances at
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{412}.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[The two reflected decisions at literal arguments]
  \label{prim-cond:family:reflect-apply-lit}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.ReflectNatNat.WF.apply_lit, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.natLE_apply, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.natEq_apply, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.natLE_apply₀, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.natEq_apply₀}
  \leanok
  \uses{prim-cond:struct:condition-wf, prim-cond:def:condition-wf-ite-dite, family:vt-reflects}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{372}
  If a condition's \texttt{asBool} is a constant reflecting $f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} \to
  \mathtt{Bool}$, then $\mathtt{asBool}'$ at two literals is definitionally the boolean literal
  $f\ a\ b$. Specialised to the two conditions the checker ships: \texttt{natLE} decides by
  \texttt{Nat.ble} and \texttt{natEq} by \texttt{Nat.beq}, at $c$ and, in the
  $\emptyset$-subscripted forms, in an arbitrary extension.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  \texttt{apply\_lit} inverts the translation of the constant \texttt{asBool} (a constant
  translates only one way, and only if it is in the environment) and then applies
  \texttt{natBinLitBool} at the two literals. The four specialisations rewrite through
  \texttt{ConditionImpl.WF.apply} and, for the $\emptyset$ forms, move the fact along the
  extension and rewrite by \texttt{vnil}.
\end{proof}

\section{Transport between binder depths}

The reflection's pieces are checked at several different binder depths: \texttt{type} and
\texttt{toDec} are read once at the empty context and again four binders deep inside the
\texttt{ite} gadget. Nothing in \texttt{TrExprS} identifies the two readings, so this section
builds the transport kit that does. It is generic \texttt{VLCtx} and \texttt{TrExprS}
infrastructure, declared inside the \texttt{Lean4Lean.Primitive} namespace.

\begin{lemma}[Appending local contexts, and literals are projection-free]
  \label{prim-cond:family:vlctx-append}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.VLCtx.toCtx_append, Lean4Lean.Primitive.VLCtx.find?_append_inv, Lean4Lean.Primitive.VLCtx.find?_append, Lean4Lean.Primitive.noProj_toConstructor}
  \leanok
  \uses{def:vt-vlctx, prim-cond:def:noproj}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{438}
  \texttt{toCtx\_append} is the simp lemma $(\Delta \mathbin{+\!\!+} \Delta').\mathtt{toCtx} =
  \Delta.\mathtt{toCtx} \mathbin{+\!\!+} \Delta'.\mathtt{toCtx}$; \texttt{find?\_append} and its
  converse \texttt{find?\_append\_inv} say that a lookup landing inside the prefix agrees with the
  lookup in the appended context. \texttt{noProj\_toConstructor} says a literal's constructor
  unfolding contains no projections.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \leanok
  Structural recursion on the prefix in each of the first three;
  \texttt{noProj\_toConstructor} splits on \texttt{Nat} and \texttt{String} literals and inducts
  over the string's characters.
  \reviewnote{These are generic \texttt{VLCtx} lemmas declared as
  \texttt{Lean4Lean.Primitive.VLCtx.*} rather than next to the rest of the \texttt{VLCtx} API in
  \texttt{Lean4Lean/Verify/Typing/}; the qualified names are easy to mistake for
  \texttt{Lean4Lean.VLCtx.*}.}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A translation survives binders appended on the right]
  \label{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-weakr}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.TrExprS.weakR}
  \leanok
  \uses{ind:trexprs, prim-cond:def:noproj}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{485}
  In an ordered environment: if $e$ translates to $e'$ in $\Delta$, the context is well formed and
  $e$ is projection-free, then $e$ translates to the same $e'$ in $\Delta \mathbin{+\!\!+}
  \Delta'$. Adding context on the right does not change the reading of a term.
  \thesisref{Weakening (typesys.tex, thm:weak)}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{prim-cond:family:vlctx-append}
  \leanok
  Induction on the \texttt{TrExprS} derivation. Variables go through
  \texttt{VLCtx.find?\_append}; the binder cases need the typing side conditions weakened, which
  two local helpers (\texttt{weakT}, \texttt{weakI}) supply from \texttt{VEnv.IsDefEq.weakR} and
  \texttt{toCtx\_append}.
  \reviewnote{After the \texttt{iota} merge this still takes \texttt{env.Ordered}, unlike the six
  neighbouring lemmas that were raised to \texttt{OrderedStrong}; nothing in the file says which
  lemmas need which strength.}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A closed term is read the same way wherever it is read]
  \label{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-ofclosed}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.TrExprS.ofClosed}
  \leanok
  \uses{ind:trexprs, prim-cond:def:noproj}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{540}
  Let $\mathit{env}$ be well formed. If $e$ translates to $e'$ in $\mathit{pre} \mathbin{+\!\!+}
  \Delta$, is projection-free, has no free variables and no bound variable escaping
  $\mathit{pre}$, the prefix $\mathit{pre}$ has closed types and closed values, and $e'$ is well
  formed there, then $e'$ is closed over $\mathit{pre}$ \emph{and} $e$ already translates to $e'$
  in $\mathit{pre}$ alone. This is the converse of \ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-weakr}, and the
  reason no check has to be re-run merely to obtain a reading at the base context.
  \thesisref{Weakening, parts 3--4, i.e.\ strengthening (typesys.tex, thm:weak)}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{prim-cond:family:vlctx-append, fam:strong-inversions}
  \leanok
  Induction on the \texttt{TrExprS} derivation, proving closedness of $e'$ simultaneously with the
  narrowed translation. The typing side conditions come down by \texttt{VExpr.WF.weakN\_iff},
  \texttt{VEnv.IsType.weakN\_iff} and \texttt{VEnv.HasType.weakN\_iff}, and the types the binder
  cases carry are re-derived at the narrower context from \texttt{VExpr.WF.app\_inv} and
  \texttt{VExpr.WF.lam\_inv} rather than transported.
  \axfoot{sorryAx, inherited: those three weakN\_iff lemmas and OnCtx.weakN\_inv live in
  Theory/Typing/UniqueTyping.lean and are sorry-tainted on master already}
  \reviewnote{This takes the full \texttt{VEnv.WF} where its immediate neighbours take
  \texttt{Ordered} or \texttt{OrderedStrong}; that is master's choice, but together with the
  \texttt{iota} upgrade it leaves the file with three different environment hypotheses and no
  stated rationale for any of them.}
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Identifying a closed piece wherever it is read]
  \label{prim-cond:family:trexprs-nil-inv}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.TrExprS.of_nil_any, Lean4Lean.Primitive.TrExprS.of_nil_unique, Lean4Lean.Primitive.TrExprS.app2_nil_inv, Lean4Lean.Primitive.TrExprS.app1_nil_inv, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.prop_app2_inv, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Condition.WF.dec_app2_inv}
  \leanok
  \uses{prim-cond:def:noproj, prim-cond:struct:condition-wf}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{664}
  A projection-free translation established at the empty context holds at every context
  (\texttt{of\_nil\_any}) and is then the only translation that term has at any context at all
  (\texttt{of\_nil\_unique}). Consequently an
  application of such a closed head to one or two arguments translates to that head's fixed
  translation applied to the arguments' translations (\texttt{app1\_nil\_inv},
  \texttt{app2\_nil\_inv}), which specialises to a condition's \texttt{prop} and \texttt{dec}
  (\texttt{prop\_app2\_inv}, \texttt{dec\_app2\_inv}).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-weakr}
  \leanok
  \texttt{of\_nil\_any} is \ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-weakr} with an empty prefix;
  \texttt{of\_nil\_unique} combines it with \texttt{TrExprS.unique'} and
  \texttt{noProj.isUnique}. The inversions strip the spine through the \texttt{app} case of

\section{The reflection layer}

A \texttt{Reflection} is the source-level package a \texttt{reflectNatNat} condition is built from:
a relation \texttt{type} between a proposition and a boolean, a decision procedure \texttt{toDec},
and the \texttt{ite}/\texttt{dite} gadgets built on it. \texttt{Reflection.check} establishes the
unconditional part; \texttt{checkITE} and \texttt{checkNatDITE} are the two optional checks.

\begin{theorem}[The shape of the reflection's \texttt{ite} and \texttt{dite} gadgets]
  \label{prim-cond:thm:reflection-ite-tr}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.Reflection.ite_tr, Lean4Lean.Primitive.Reflection.natDITE_tr}
  \leanok
  \uses{prim-cond:struct:reflection-wf, prim-cond:def:condok}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Condition.lean}{912}
  If the check ran at the empty context and \texttt{type}, \texttt{toDec} satisfy
  \texttt{CondOK}, then any translation of \texttt{Reflection.ite} is exactly four lambdas -- over
  $\mathtt{Prop}$, $\mathtt{Bool}$, $\mathtt{type}'\ (\mathtt{bvar}\ 1)\ (\mathtt{bvar}\ 0)$ and
  $\mathtt{Type}$ -- whose body is $\mathtt{ite}$ at the reflection's own decision
  $\mathtt{toDec}'$ applied to the three bound variables; \texttt{natDITE\_tr} is the same with
  three lambdas and \texttt{dite} at \texttt{Nat}. In both, the $\mathtt{type}'$ and
  $\mathtt{toDec}'$ occurring under the binders are the very ones
  \ref{prim-cond:struct:reflection-wf} names.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{prim-cond:family:trexprs-nil-inv}
  \leanok
  Invert the translation of the lambda telescope constructor by constructor, then identify the
  closed subterms \texttt{type} and \texttt{toDec} with the named translations by
  \texttt{of\_nil\_unique}, which compares a reading taken at the telescope's own depth with the
  $[\,]$-level one directly, so no narrowing step is needed.
\end{proof}

\section{From a reflection to a condition}

What the reflection layer proves is stated about the reflection's own \texttt{toDec}; what a
recognizer needs is stated about the condition's \texttt{dec}. The two are joined by the
\texttt{e ≡ dec} check, and the bridges below carry each fact across. The section ends at
\ref{prim-cond:thm:condition-check-wf}, the one verification boundary the three recursion files
(\texttt{DivMod.lean}, \texttt{Gcd.lean}, \texttt{Bitwise.lean}) consume; the fifteen theorems of
\texttt{Clauses.lean} need no \texttt{Condition} at all.

\section{The simple clauses}

\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Clauses.lean}{1} holds fifteen theorems covering
the fourteen branches of the recognizer that need no recursion machinery (the fifteenth is the
schema two of them share). They all have one shape: given a
well-formed environment state, the branch's name guard on the declaration under test, and the
translation data \texttt{Data v ci' c} (\ref{vpc:struct:primitive-data}) that the dispatcher hands
in, a successful run returns a \texttt{PrimitiveResult} (\ref{vpc:struct:primitiveresult}) for the
entry \texttt{primSpecs} records under that name (\ref{ind:primspec}). The checker context is
written as a \texttt{let} rather than left abstract, and the docstring at
\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Clauses.lean}{23} says why: only a context that
reduces to \texttt{.mk' wf .safe v.levelParams} puts the probes' facts at the empty local context
on the nose, and over an abstract \texttt{VContext} each equation would need its own rewrite.

Probe variables come from \ref{vpc:thm:withprobe}, the recorded type is pinned by
\ref{vpc:thm:data-mktyeq}, the obligation is assembled by \ref{vpc:thm:data-mkresult}, and the
arithmetic is done by the reflection schemas of chapter~\ref{chap:primitives-core}. Eleven of the
file's fifteen theorems were touched by \texttt{iota}, and in every one of them the edit is
confined to the proof: a \texttt{HasPrimitives} accessor or an inversion that now wants
\ref{struct:orderedstrong} where master passed \texttt{VEnv.Ordered}. The four left alone are
\ref{prim-clauses:thm:checknatpred-wf} and the three of
\ref{prim-clauses:thm:checknatboolcases-wf}.

\section{Division and remainder}

\texttt{Nat.div} and \texttt{Nat.mod} are both defined by a fuel-driven recursion through a
\texttt{go} auxiliary, guarded by a $\le$ test whose decision the checker has to recognise.

literals.

  recursive call at $n \bmod (m+1)$, which is what the branch's \texttt{Nat.mod} guard is there
  to make compute.
  \thesisref{no counterpart}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{prim-cond:thm:condition-check-wf, vpc:thm:unfoldnatwf, vpc:thm:probebundle-probe-wf, vpc:thm:prim-contains-nat, vpc:lem:prim-isdefequ-app-congr, vpc:lem:natbinlit, vpc:thm:data-mkresult, vpc:thm:withprobe}

  \ref{prim-cond:thm:condition-check-wf} at the empty context for the gadget's conditional, then
  \texttt{unfoldNatWellFounded.WF₂}. The step probe's right-hand side is split into the induction
  variable applied to the packer at two arguments; its head is only a \emph{translation} of
  \texttt{pack}, so it is converted to \texttt{pack'} by uniqueness and

  \texttt{VContext.natBinLit} at \texttt{HasPrimitives.natMod}, which is exactly what the guard

  first argument is zero and rewriting with \texttt{Nat.gcd\_zero\_left} and
  \texttt{Nat.gcd\_rec}.
  \axfoot{sorryAx via VEnv.WF.orderedStrong and VEnv.WF.patsStrong, and via the typechecker
  correctness theorems; plus the Verify/Axioms.lean implementation-bridge axioms}
  \reviewnote{Changed on \texttt{iota} at line 24, where the local abbreviation
  \texttt{hE := ctx.Ewf.orderedStrong} replaced \texttt{ctx.Ewf.ordered}, and at lines 95--100,

\end{proof}

\section{Bitwise operations}

\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Bitwise.lean}{1} is the hard case. The operator
that \texttt{Nat.bitwise} recurses with is a \emph{free variable} of the recognizer's context, so
nothing is known about it beyond a reflection, and the equation body is three nested conditionals
over two different \texttt{Condition}s: \texttt{natEq} for the $n = 0$ and $m = 0$ tests, and
\texttt{bool} for the operator's own decisions on the low bits.

\begin{theorem}[A reflected boolean operator at arguments worth literals]
  \label{prim-bitwise:thm:boolop2-apply}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.boolOp2_apply}
  \leanok
  \uses{family:vt-reflects, vpc:def:prim-atoms}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Bitwise.lean}{16}
  In a well-formed environment and at the empty context, let
  $f : \mathtt{Bool} \to \mathtt{Bool} \to \mathtt{Bool}$ reflect $g$, meaning that $f$ at any two
  boolean literals is definitionally the literal $g$ gives. If $a$ and $b$ are merely
  \emph{definitionally} the literals $b_a, b_b$, and $f\ a\ b$ is well formed, then $f\ a\ b$ is a
  \texttt{Bool} and is definitionally the literal $g\ b_a\ b_b$. The distinction matters because
  the equation body applies the operator to decisions, which are worth literals rather than being
  them.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{vpc:thm:wf-inv-prime, vpc:thm:wf-app-inv2, vpc:thm:isdefequ-appn-prime}
  \leanok
  Two \texttt{VExpr.WF.app\_inv'} steps off $f$'s type give the typings of $a$ and $b$ and the
  \texttt{Bool} conclusion; \texttt{IsDefEqU.appN'} then replaces $a, b$ by the literals and
  \texttt{trans} composes with the reflection equation.
  \axfoot{sorryAx, inherited: VExpr.WF.app\_inv' passes henv.orderedStrong internally, and it
  and VEnv.IsDefEqU.appN'/trans rest on Theory/Typing/UniqueTyping.lean}
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{Nat.bitwise}]
  \label{prim-bitwise:thm:checknatbitwise-wf}
  \lean{Lean4Lean.Primitive.checkNatBitwise.WF}
  \leanok
  \contrib{mixed}
  \uses{ind:primspec, vpc:struct:primitiveresult, vpc:thm:data-mkresultbitwise}
  \srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Bitwise.lean}{31}
  If the \texttt{Nat.bitwise} branch succeeds then the definition satisfies the
  \texttt{Nat.bitwise} entry of \texttt{primSpecs}, which is quantified over the operator: for
  every environment extension and every $f$ reflecting $g : \mathtt{Bool} \to \mathtt{Bool} \to
  \mathtt{Bool}$, the value at $f$ and two literals $x, y$ is definitionally the literal
  $\mathtt{Nat.bitwise}\ g\ x\ y$.
  \thesisref{no counterpart}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  \uses{prim-cond:thm:condition-check-wf, prim-bitwise:thm:boolop2-apply, prim-cond:family:condition-nateq-eval, prim-cond:family:condition-tr-inv, prim-cond:family:trexprs-nil-inv, vpc:thm:unfoldnatwf, vpc:thm:probebundle-probe-wf, vpc:def:probebundle-done, vpc:thm:data-mktyeq, vpc:thm:ext-natbinlittr, thm:vtc-toplevel}
  \leanok
  The type is pinned by \texttt{Data.mkTyEqBitwise}, then
  \ref{prim-cond:thm:condition-check-wf} runs twice, both before the operator binder: once for
  \texttt{natEq} with \texttt{ite} and \texttt{dite}, once for \texttt{bool} with \texttt{ite}.
  The operator binder is opened and the recursion handed to \texttt{unfoldNatWellFounded.WF₂};
  one probe then carries the whole equation body, whose predicate says what it is worth at
  literals given the recursive call at the halves. Evaluating it means taking the outer
  \texttt{dite} apart with \texttt{Condition.dite\_tr\_inv}, the inner conditionals with
  \texttt{natEq\_diteEval} and \texttt{natEq\_decideTr}, and the operator applications with
  \ref{prim-bitwise:thm:boolop2-apply}; the halving arguments are computed by
  \texttt{Ext.natBinLitTr} at the \texttt{Nat.div} and \texttt{Nat.mod} reflections. The result is
  assembled by \texttt{Data.mkResultBitwise} at a closing that instantiates the operator variable.
  \axfoot{sorryAx via VEnv.WF.orderedStrong and VEnv.WF.patsStrong, and via the typechecker
  correctness theorems; plus the Verify/Axioms.lean implementation-bridge axioms}
  \reviewnote{Changed on \texttt{iota} in the proof only (lines 41, 86, 150, 152, 182, 315, 362).
  The two \texttt{trproj} lines in this chapter are here as well
  (\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Bitwise.lean}{203}): that commit is a pure
  re-wrap (``style: wrap the lines over the column limit''), and the \texttt{hE.ordered} the
  wrapped line carries was already \texttt{iota}'s.}
  \reviewnote{The local abbreviation \texttt{hE := ctx.Ewf.orderedStrong} introduced at line 41
  is immediately downgraded by \texttt{hE.ordered} at seven of its twelve uses (86, 150, 152,
  182, 204, 315, 362), so the name advertises a strength most of its uses do not want. At 375
  lines this is also the longest declaration of the chapter and the only clause-level
  \texttt{WF} theorem in the group without a docstring.}
\end{proof}

\section{Contribution summary}
\label{sec:prim-arith-contribution}

Twenty-six of the fifty-eight nodes of this chapter carry a contribution badge, and every one of
them carries it for the same reason. No declaration was added to these five files and none was
removed; the diff against \texttt{master} is 69 insertions against 68 deletions, the extra line
being the \texttt{trproj} re-wrap. Of 4655 lines, 67 are attributed to \texttt{iota} and 2 to
\texttt{trproj}, and all 67 re-thread one hypothesis between \texttt{VEnv.Ordered} and
\ref{struct:orderedstrong}: 61 raise it, and 6 -- the \texttt{Bitwise.lean} uses of the one
local abbreviation that was raised -- lower it again. Six theorem
\emph{statements} changed (\ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-boolprop},
\ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-propboolprop}, \ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-boolnat3},
\ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-natnatprop}, \ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-divgotype},
\ref{prim-cond:thm:trexprs-natproj}), and they changed only because the
\texttt{HasPrimitives} accessors they call were strengthened two files away
(\ref{vpc:thm:prim-trnat-trbool}, \ref{vpc:thm:prim-nat-bool-typing},
\ref{vpc:thm:prim-istype-prime}); of the other 61 lines, 55 write \texttt{c.Ewf.orderedStrong}
or \texttt{E.wf.orderedStrong} where master wrote \texttt{.ordered} and 6 write
\texttt{hE.ordered} where master wrote \texttt{hE}. The 2 \texttt{trproj} lines
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Verify/Environment/Primitive/Bitwise.lean}{203}) re-wrap one over-long line
inside \ref{prim-bitwise:thm:checknatbitwise-wf}; the content they carry is \texttt{iota}'s.

Why the edit was necessary is settled in other chapters, and the reasoning is sound. On
\texttt{master} the strong system was reached from \texttt{Ordered} by a proved theorem, which was
available only because the $\iota$ stage of \texttt{Ordered} was a stub. Once $\iota$ rules have a
real specification, an environment can be \texttt{Ordered} and still admit $\iota$ rules that do
not preserve types, so the theorem is false as stated and the honest replacement carries subject
reduction for the registered rules as a third field of \ref{struct:orderedstrong}. Two of that
structure's three fields are then discharged from \texttt{VEnv.WF} only through
\ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}: \ref{thm:wf-orderedstrong} builds its \texttt{strong} field as
\texttt{H.strong H.patsStrong} and its \texttt{pats} field from \texttt{H.patsStrong} directly
(\srcloc{Lean4Lean/Theory/Typing/EnvLemmas.lean}{339}).

How it connects. Every result in this chapter discharges the recognizer's obligation
\texttt{PrimitiveResult} (\ref{vpc:struct:primitiveresult}) for the declaration checker of
chapter~\ref{chap:primitives-core}. The four conditional-driven clauses reach it through
\ref{prim-cond:thm:condition-check-wf}; the fifteen simple clauses of \texttt{Clauses.lean} use
no \texttt{Condition} and reach \texttt{sorryAx} through the probe and \texttt{HasPrimitives}
lemmas instead. Either way the branch's single open obligation is now a hypothesis of
every arithmetic primitive: \ref{prim-clauses:thm:checknatadd-wf} through
\ref{prim-clauses:thm:checkstringoflist-wf}, \ref{prim-divmod:thm:checknatdiv-wf},
\ref{prim-divmod:thm:checknatmod-wf}, \ref{prim-gcd:thm:checknatgcd-wf} and
\ref{prim-bitwise:thm:checknatbitwise-wf} all depend on \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}, where on
\texttt{master} they depended only on \texttt{VEnv.Ordered}. The two clause nodes whose own
text escaped the edit, \ref{prim-clauses:thm:checknatpred-wf} and
\ref{prim-clauses:thm:checknatboolcases-wf} (four declarations in all), are no exception: the lemmas they call --
\ref{vpc:thm:withprobe}, \ref{vpc:thm:reflects-pred-eqns}, \ref{vpc:thm:reflects-ctor-cases} --
were strengthened in chapter~\ref{chap:primitives-core}, and the \texttt{CoeOut} coercion inserts
the rest, which is precisely what makes the change invisible in the source.

What remains open on the contributed side is exactly \ref{thm:wf-patsstrong}; nothing in these
five files can discharge it, and nothing in them is made harder or easier by it. What remains open
as a matter of hygiene is that the change is nowhere declared where it is consumed: no touched
line records that \texttt{orderedStrong} is where a \texttt{sorry} enters, three of the five
files have no module docstring at all, and the file left with three different environment
hypotheses (\texttt{VEnv.WF}, \texttt{Ordered}, \texttt{OrderedStrong}) gives no rationale for
any of them. The line counts above should also not be read as the extent of the change: because
the \texttt{CoeOut} instance's target moved, master proofs that neither branch edited became
\texttt{sorry}-dependent too, \ref{prim-cond:thm:reflection-gentele} being the clearest case.
Both points are recorded in Section~\ref{sec:prim-arith-review}. One further loose end is
\ref{prim-cond:def:noproj}: the primitives layer excludes \texttt{Expr.proj} by construction
precisely to avoid transporting a \texttt{TrProj} obligation, which is the one place where this
chapter touches the \texttt{trproj} work of chapter~\ref{chap:trexpr}, and neither side records
the connection.
