\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{TrProj.weak'\_inv} is \texttt{sorry}, blocked on master's admitted \texttt{IsDefEqU.weakN\_iff}; it keeps Theorem~\ref{thm:trexprs-weakfv-inv} and the three \texttt{ConditionallyX.weakN\_inv} lemmas sorry-dependent.
\item \texttt{TrProj.uniq} is \texttt{sorry}; its docstring does not pin how the per-field level function \texttt{uss} is chosen, so \texttt{TrProjCtor} may be under-constrained rather than merely unproved.
\item \texttt{TrProjCtor.pat} only asserts \emph{some} $\iota$ reduct is registered, not which; \texttt{TrProj} alone does not fix reduction behaviour.
\item No \texttt{TrProjCtor} witness is built in general: \texttt{inferProj.WF\_struct} is \texttt{sorry}, and the restriction to single-constructor, non-recursive structures is asserted in prose, not formalized.
\item The \texttt{iota} strengthening from \texttt{Ordered} to \texttt{OrderedStrong} for the literal family and \texttt{TrExprS.ofConst} routes every use through the admitted \texttt{VEnv.WF.patsStrong}.
\item \texttt{TrExprS.uniq}, the \texttt{TrExpr} builders, and everything built on them (beta reduction, universe substitution) inherit sorry-dependence from \texttt{TrProj.uniq}.
\end{itemize}
